## The messages

When you click **Start for Seller** or **Start for Buyer** and a session can't start because of minutes or billing, the Coach shows one of these:

| Message | What it means | Who can fix it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your team is out of coaching minutes. Let your team leader know. | Your company has used all the minutes in its plan for this billing period. | Your company's owner, by changing the plan. |
| Your team has reached its coaching hour limit. Let your team leader know. | Your team hit the monthly hour cap your admin set for it. | An owner or admin, by raising the team's cap. |
| Your team's Sayso subscription isn't active. Let your team leader know. | The subscription is paused, past due or cancelled. | Your company's owner. |

Send your owner or admin a screenshot of the message so they know which one it is. Admins can find the fix in [Set a team's monthly hour cap](/help/admins/set-team-hours/) and [Billing for teams](/help/admins/team-billing/).

## When minutes come back

Minutes reset to your plan's amount at the start of each billing period, when the next payment goes through. A team's hour cap applies per month.

## Make your minutes go further

- Press **Stop** when your dial block ends instead of leaving a session going.
- Keep **Auto Stop** on so a forgotten session ends on its own. See [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/).
- Do admin work after you press Stop, not in the middle of a session.

See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/) for what counts.

## On your own plan?

If you're not on a team, check **Settings**, **Subscription** in the web app to see your minutes and your subscription status. See [Your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/).
