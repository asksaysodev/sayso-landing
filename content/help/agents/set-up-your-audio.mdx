Most "no prompts" problems come from these settings.

## Rule 1: Place the call from your computer

Sayso listens to what plays through your computer. If the call rings on your cell phone, Sayso can't hear it.

- Dial from your CRM or dialer on the computer, in Google Chrome.
- Check your dialer's calling setting. It must be set to internet, computer or "ask me". If it's set to always use your mobile phone, the call goes to your cell and Sayso hears nothing.
- Calls answered on your cell phone, including Zillow calls, won't work.

See [Set up your dialer for Sayso](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/) for specific dialers.

## Rule 2: Use Google Chrome

Open your CRM and dialer in Chrome. Safari has grabbed the microphone in the past, so Sayso lost your side of the call.

## Headphones are optional

Your computer's speakers and built-in microphone work. If you use a headset:

- Connect it before you start a session. Don't switch audio devices in the middle of a call.
- On Windows, if a Bluetooth headset mic keeps cutting out, use a wired headset or set your laptop mic as the input.

## Check your microphone

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

1. Open **System Settings**, **Sound**, **Input**.
2. Select the mic you'll talk into.
3. Talk and watch the input level meter move.
4. Open **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, **Microphone** and turn on Sayso.
5. Open **Privacy & Security**, **Screen & System Audio Recording** (called **Screen Recording** on older macOS versions) and turn on Sayso. This is how Sayso hears the other person. It only uses the audio.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

1. Right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar and choose **Sound settings**.
2. Under **Input**, select the mic you'll talk into and check that the level bar moves when you talk.
3. Open **Settings**, **Privacy & security**, **Microphone** and turn on **Microphone access** and **Let desktop apps access your microphone**.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Test before your first dial block

1. Open the Coach widget and click **Start for Seller** or **Start for Buyer**.
2. Have a teammate call you, or call a teammate, from your dialer on the computer.
3. Talk for about 30 seconds the way you would with a lead.

If Smart Capture fills in and prompts show up within about 30 seconds, Sayso is working.

- **Smart Capture fills in but no prompts?** Sayso isn't hearing you. Recheck your mic.
- **Nothing captured from the other person?** The call audio isn't coming through the computer. Recheck your dialer setting and, on Mac, Screen & System Audio.

Press **Stop** when you're done so the test doesn't keep using your hours.
