## Before you start

Connect your CRM once in the web app under **Settings**, **Connections**. Each agent connects their own account. See [Connect Follow Up Boss](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/) or [Connect Sure Send](/help/web-app/connect-sure-send/).

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead-1.webp" alt="The Add lead menu on a conversation" width={520} />

## Send a conversation to a lead

1. In the web app, open **Conversations** and find the call.
2. Click **Add lead**.
3. Search for the lead by email or phone and pick them. If they're not in your CRM yet, choose **Create lead** and fill in the required fields.
4. Sayso links the conversation to that lead and adds a Sayso note in your CRM with the call summary, Smart Capture notes, market data and whether an appointment was booked.

Leads you create from Sayso show **Sayso** as their source in your CRM.

Full details, including the fields each CRM requires: [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/).

## Fixed your notes after sending?

If you edit Smart Capture on a conversation that's already linked, Sayso asks **Resync this conversation?** Choose **Yes, resync** to update the note in your CRM. See [Open, change or resync a linked lead](/help/web-app/edit-or-open-linked-lead/).

## Using another CRM?

Copy your notes and paste them in:

- During the call, click the copy icon at the end of the Smart Capture row in the Coach widget.
- After the call, copy from the **Smart Capture** tab in **Conversations**.
