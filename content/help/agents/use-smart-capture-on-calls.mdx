## What Smart Capture listens for

LPMAMA is the qualifying framework many agents already use. Smart Capture fills it in for you:

| Letter | Field | What it captures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L | Location | Where they want to buy or the property they want to sell |
| P | Price | Their price range or price expectations |
| M | Motivation | Why they want to move |
| A | Agent | Whether they're working with another agent |
| M | Mortgage | Their financing situation |
| A | Appointment | Whether you set a time to meet |

## Use it during the call

The Smart Capture row sits at the bottom of the insights panel in the Coach: one circle for each letter. A circle fills in when Sayso hears something for that field. Hover a circle to read what it captured. If nothing has come up yet, it says **No data yet**.

Use the empty circles as a checklist. If Motivation is still empty halfway through the call, that's your next question.

## Copy your notes

Click the copy icon at the end of the row to copy all six fields at once, then paste them into your CRM. Do this before you press **Reset**, because Reset clears the row for the next conversation.

<Callout type="tip">
Using Follow Up Boss or Sure Send? Skip the copy and paste. After the call, send the notes to the lead in one click from **Conversations**. See [Send your notes to your CRM](/help/agents/send-notes-to-your-crm/).
</Callout>

## After the call

Smart Capture notes are saved with each conversation. If a field is wrong or missing, you can edit it in the web app. See [Edit Smart Capture notes after a call](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).
