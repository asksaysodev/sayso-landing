## Getting set up

<Faq q="Does Sayso work on Windows?">

Yes. Sayso works on Windows 10 and 11, and on Mac with macOS 13 or newer. See [System requirements](/help/support/system-requirements/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Does Sayso work on a phone or tablet?">

Not yet. Sayso coaches calls placed from your computer. See [Set up your dialer](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Do I need headphones?">

No. Your computer's speakers and built-in microphone work. Headphones are optional. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Which browser should I use?">

Google Chrome, for the web app, your CRM and your dialer.

</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I change my login email?">

Email support@asksayso.com to change it. See [Update your name and phone](/help/web-app/edit-profile/).

</Faq>

## How Sayso works

<Faq q="How does Sayso know what to say?">

Sayso listens to the live conversation and coaches it in three stages: Connect, Discover and Convert. Prompts are suggestions. You decide whether to use them. See [Connect, Discover, Convert](/help/agents/call-stages/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="What's the Reset button for?">

It tells Sayso a new conversation started. Press it each time a new person picks up, never in the middle of a call. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Will the person I'm calling hear Sayso?">

No. Prompts show on your screen only. Nothing plays on the call. See [Can the person on the call hear or see Sayso?](/help/support/can-the-lead-hear-sayso/)

</Faq>

<Faq q="Can they tell I'm using Sayso?">

No. You're the one talking.

</Faq>

<Faq q="How do I find my objection handler during a call?">
Open Playbooks and type a word from the objection, like "commission", into the search bar. Use the arrows to move between matches. See [Keep your script open next to Cue](/help/agents/use-playbooks-on-calls/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="How long until it fits my style?">

Coach Tips appear after your first 10 conversations and are based on your own calls. Rate insights with a thumbs up or down in Conversations to tell Sayso which prompts help. See [Improve your coaching over time](/help/agents/improve-your-coaching/).

</Faq>

## Privacy and data

<Faq q="Does Sayso record my calls?">

Sayso never records or stores call audio. It transcribes the conversation live to coach you and saves a text transcript with the conversation, along with the summary, Smart Capture notes, the insights you saw and any market data you looked up. See [What Sayso stores](/help/support/privacy-and-data/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Does Sayso dial for me?">

No. Sayso is a coaching and notes layer. It doesn't dial and doesn't change how you dial. Keep following your brokerage's rules for calling, including Do Not Call lists. See [Is it legal to use Sayso on calls?](/help/support/call-recording-consent/)

</Faq>

<Faq q="Should I say sensitive details out loud on a coached call?">

Anything said out loud goes into the transcript, so don't read card numbers or passwords during a coached session.

</Faq>

<Faq q="Can my admin see my calls?">

If you're on a team, your owners and admins can see your conversations and activity. See [What your team admin can see](/help/agents/what-your-admin-can-see/).

</Faq>

## Compatibility

<Faq q="What dialers does Sayso work with?">

Any dialer that plays the call through your computer: Follow Up Boss, Sure Send, Mojo (dial your session number from your CRM's computer dialer), Vulcan7, REDX, Google Voice, browser dialers, Zoom and Google Meet. Phone systems like Zoom Phone work when you talk through their computer app. Calls that ring on your cell phone or a desk phone don't work. See [Set up your dialer](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="What CRMs does Sayso work with?">

Follow Up Boss and Sure Send connect directly. Any other CRM works with copy and paste. See [Send your notes to your CRM](/help/agents/send-notes-to-your-crm/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="I forgot to copy my notes. Are they gone?">

No. Every conversation is saved in Conversations at app.asksayso.com. See [What's saved for each conversation](/help/web-app/what-is-saved/).

</Faq>

## Pricing and hours

<Faq q="How much does Sayso cost?">

See [pricing](/pricing/). To talk through which plan fits, [book a demo](/demo/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Can two people share one login?">
No. Each person needs their own login. For two or more people, upgrade to a team account. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) to switch. See [Teams overview](/help/admins/teams-overview/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="What counts toward my hours?">

A conversation counts when Cue gave you an insight, Smart Capture caught something, or you used Pulse. A voicemail counts if it triggered an insight. Keep Auto Stop on so a session ends if you walk away. See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="My team is out of minutes. What do I do?">

Let your team's owner or admin know. See [Your team is out of coaching minutes](/help/agents/out-of-minutes/).

</Faq>

## Real estate scenarios

<Faq q="Does Sayso work for expireds, FSBOs and circle prospecting?">

Yes. See [Coaching by lead type](/help/agents/#use-cases).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Does Sayso work for internet leads?">

Yes, as long as you call them from your computer. See [Using Sayso for internet lead follow-up](/help/agents/internet-leads/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="Does Sayso work for listing presentations or buyer consults?">

Sayso is built for prospecting and follow-up calls. It works on Zoom and Google Meet, but the coaching is tuned for booking appointments. See [Use Sayso on Zoom or Google Meet calls](/help/agents/video-calls/).

</Faq>

<Faq q="What if the person speaks Spanish?">

Sayso supports calls in English today. Spanish is coming soon.

</Faq>
