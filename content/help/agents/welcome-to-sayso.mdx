## What Sayso does

Sayso listens to your call in real time and coaches the conversation while it happens. It helps you ask better questions, handle objections, find the person's motivation and ask for the appointment.

Sayso works alongside the tools you already use. It does not replace your CRM, your dialer or your lead sources. It sits on top of them and improves what happens inside the conversation.

## What's inside

- **Cue**: live prompts on what to ask or say next. See [Cue](/help/desktop-app/cue/).
- **Smart Capture**: notes that fill in while you talk, organized as LPMAMA (Location, Price, Motivation, Agent, Mortgage, Appointment). See [Smart Capture](/help/desktop-app/smart-capture/).
- **Pulse**: market data by ZIP code, in the middle of the call. See [Pulse](/help/desktop-app/pulse/).
- **Playbooks**: your scripts on screen next to the prompts. See [Playbooks](/help/desktop-app/playbooks/).

You use these in the **Coach**, a small window from the Sayso desktop app that stays on top of your CRM and dialer. After the call, everything is saved in the web app at app.asksayso.com.

## Before your first call

Sayso works on Mac and Windows. Three habits make it work:

1. Wear headphones.
2. Call from your computer, not your cell phone.
3. Press **Reset** each time a new person picks up.

Start with the [quick start](/help/agents/quick-start/) and the [cheat sheet](/help/agents/cheat-sheet/).

## How to get the most out of it

Use Sayso every time you prospect. After your dial blocks, open **Conversations** in the web app and rate prompts with a thumbs up or down. After your first 10 conversations, your **Coach Tips** on the Dashboard become personal to you. See [Improve your coaching over time](/help/agents/improve-your-coaching/).
