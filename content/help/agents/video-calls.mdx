## How it works

Sayso coaches whatever conversation plays through your computer. On a Zoom or Google Meet call, your voice comes from your microphone and the other person's voice counts as the lead.

1. Put your headphones on.
2. Open the Coach and click **Start for Seller** or **Start for Buyer**.
3. Press **Reset** when the other person joins and the conversation starts.
4. Press **Stop** when the meeting ends.

## Meeting detection

If you join from a web browser, like Google Meet in Chrome or Zoom in the browser, Sayso can notice the call and show **Sayso identified a call** so you can start with one click. It doesn't do this for the Zoom or Microsoft Teams desktop apps, so open the Coach yourself for those. See [Meeting detection](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/).

## Good to know

- Sayso's coaching is tuned for prospecting and follow-up conversations that lead to an appointment. It works on consults too, but expect prompts aimed at next steps.
- Use headphones on video calls too. Laptop speakers feed the other person's voice back into your mic.
- Sayso supports calls in English.
