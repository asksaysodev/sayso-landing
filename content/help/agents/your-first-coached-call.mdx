## Before you dial

1. Put your headphones on and check that your dialer calls through the computer, not your cell. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).
2. Open the Coach widget. Use the shortcut (<Kbd>Control</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Windows) or click the Sayso icon in your menu bar or taskbar and choose **Launch Coach**.
3. Click **Start for Seller** or **Start for Buyer**. The lead type can't change during a session, so group your seller calls together and your buyer calls together. See [Choose Buyer or Seller](/help/agents/choose-buyer-or-seller/).

## During the call

**Wait about 30 seconds.** Prompts start once Sayso has heard enough of the conversation.

**Follow your script.** Glance at prompts during pauses instead of reading them word for word.

**Open the insights panel.** Click the check mark icon on the right of the Coach widget to show or hide Cue's insights and the Smart Capture row. A dot on the icon means a new insight arrived while the panel was closed.

**Pin or close insights.** Hover an insight to pin it or close it. You can pin up to 3. When you close one, the next one moves up. See [Pin an insight](/help/desktop-app/pin-insights/).

**Keep your CRM in a normal window.** The Coach widget stays on top of normal windows but can hide behind full-screen apps.

## Each time a new person picks up

Press **Reset**, the orange button with a circular arrow next to the timer. Reset saves the conversation you just had and starts a fresh one with the same lead type. Skip it and you'll get "book the appointment" prompts on a fresh hello. Never press Reset in the middle of a conversation. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

Before you press Reset, click the copy icon at the end of the Smart Capture row to paste that call's notes into your CRM. If you forget, the notes are saved in **Conversations**.

## End your session

When your dial block is done, press **Stop**, the red button with a square. The Coach widget shows **Saving conversation** while it saves.

If you forget, **Auto Stop** ends the session after a few minutes without hearing the other person. See [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/).

## After your session

Open **Conversations** at app.asksayso.com. Each conversation has its summary, Smart Capture notes, Cue insights and market data. Send notes to Follow Up Boss or Sure Send with **Add lead**, and give insights a thumbs up or down. See [What to do after each call](/help/agents/after-your-call/).
