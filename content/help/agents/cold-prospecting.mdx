## What these calls look like with Sayso

You're dialing people who didn't ask to hear from you: expireds, FSBOs, circle prospecting, neighborhood calls. You have a few seconds to earn the right to keep talking.

On these calls, Sayso prompts you to:

- Get past the opening without sounding like a telemarketer
- Handle "not interested" calmly
- Ask questions that uncover the person's motivation
- Stay on pace without losing warmth

## Features to have open

- **Cue:** live prompts based on what the person just said, including objections you weren't ready for.
- **Smart Capture:** organizes the call into LPMAMA, so you don't type while you talk.
- **Pulse:** pull market data live if they ask "what are homes going for?"
- **Playbooks:** keep your script on screen. Upload your expired, FSBO or circle prospecting script, or use the ready-made playbooks.

## Tips

- **Reset at every pickup, and set Buffer Time to 0.** Press **Reset** when a person answers, so Cue skips the ringing, screeners and voicemail greetings before it. On a multi-line dialer, reset at every pickup. See [Using Sayso during a dial block or power dialer session](/help/agents/power-dialer-sessions/).
- **Choose the right lead type.** Expireds and FSBOs are seller calls. Circle prospecting is usually seller but can go either way. See [Choose Buyer or Seller](/help/agents/choose-buyer-or-seller/).
- **Wear headphones and dial from your computer.** A clean transcript keeps prompts accurate.
- **Rate your prompts after the call.** Ratings tell Sayso which prompts help. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).
