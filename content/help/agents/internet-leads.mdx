## What internet lead follow-up looks like with Sayso

They filled out a form on Zillow, Realtor.com, your IDX site or an ad. They expect a call, but they don't know you. They're usually still in research mode.

Sayso helps you:

- Get past the "I'm just looking" brush-off without pushing
- Qualify them without sounding like you're reading a form
- Find out their timeline versus "someday"
- Book the appointment without dragging out the call

## Features to have open

- **Cue:** prompts for rapport and discovery questions as the call moves along.
- **Smart Capture:** pulls location, price range, timeline and motivation into notes. Send them to the lead in Follow Up Boss or Sure Send with **Add lead**. See [Send your notes to your CRM](/help/agents/send-notes-to-your-crm/).
- **Pulse:** they're usually looking in specific ZIP codes. Type one in and share a number.
- **Playbooks:** keep a buyer lead script open, a ready-made one or your own.

## Tips

- **Match the lead source.** A Zillow buyer is different from an IDX form buyer. Same lead type, different starting motivation. Rate your prompts after so Sayso learns what works for you.
- **Don't over-discover.** The mistake on internet leads is asking 10 questions when 3 would have booked the appointment. Once motivation is clear, ask for the appointment.
- **Call from your computer.** Zillow and other lead calls answered on your cell phone won't reach Sayso. Call them back from your CRM's computer dialer. See [Set up your dialer](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/).
- **Use Pulse early.** If they say "I'm looking in 85251," look up the ZIP code and mention what you see. Instant credibility. See [Answer market questions with Pulse](/help/agents/use-pulse-on-calls/).
