## What open house follow-up looks like with Sayso

Open house visitors walked through a property on purpose. They're further along than someone who never reached out. They also visited a lot of open houses and talked to a lot of agents, so your follow-up call needs to stand out.

Sayso helps you:

- Reference specifics from the open house to show you were paying attention
- Ask discovery questions about their timeline, financing and must-haves
- Handle "we're just looking" without letting the lead go cold
- Move from casual conversation to setting the appointment

## Setup

- **Lead type:** click **Start for Buyer**.
- **Reset and Buffer Time:** press **Reset** when they answer, with Buffer Time at 0.
- **Playbooks:** keep your open house script open, or upload your own, something like: "Hey [name], this is [you]. We met at the open house on [street]. What did you think of the place?"

## Features to have open

- **Cue:** surfaces buyer discovery questions and objection handlers.
- **Pulse:** pull market data for the area they were looking at. Shows you know the local market.
- **Smart Capture:** captures their criteria so your next follow-up is even more targeted.

## Tips

- **Call within 24 hours.** Open house leads go cold fast. Same day is ideal.
- **Don't just ask if they liked the house.** Ask what they're looking for and whether that property was close.
- **Use Smart Capture notes for the second call.** When you mention their specific must-haves a week later, you stand apart from every other agent who called once and gave up.
