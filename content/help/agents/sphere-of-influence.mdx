## What sphere calls look like with Sayso

Family, friends, old coworkers, neighbors, the parents from your kid's team. They know and like you. They'd send you business if it came up naturally. Your job is to make it come up naturally without being awkward.

Sayso helps you:

- Move from personal catch-up into what you're working on
- Ask for referrals without making it feel like an ask
- Capture life updates for your next conversation

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/cue-1.webp" alt="Cue insights in the Coach widget during a call" />

## Features to have open

- **Cue:** gentle prompts that help you move from small talk into business without losing the warmth.
- **Smart Capture:** notes from the call, so you have something specific to bring up next time.
- **Playbooks:** your sphere script or talking points if you want structure.

## Tips

- **Don't force the pivot.** If the call isn't heading toward business, let it be a relationship call. Sayso still saves the conversation and its notes for next time.
- **Choose the lead type based on who they might refer.** If they're likely to send you buyers, start for Buyer. Sellers, start for Seller.
- **Follow up.** The value of sphere calls builds over time. Your notes from the last call give you something specific to talk about on the next one.
