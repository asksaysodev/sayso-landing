## Open your playbooks

- Press <Kbd>Control</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Mac or <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Windows.
- Or click the Sayso icon in your menu bar or taskbar and choose **Show Playbooks**.
- During a session, click the list icon on the right of the Coach widget.

Playbooks opens without a session. Read one before you dial.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/playbooks-1.webp" alt="The Playbooks window" />

## Use it during a call

- Pick the playbook for your calls from the list.
- Search with **Search in playbooks** (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd> on Windows). To find an objection handler, type a word from the objection, like "commission". Use the arrows to jump between matches.
- Lead with your script. Glance at Cue when the conversation goes somewhere the script doesn't cover.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/playbooks-search-1.webp" alt="The Search in Playbooks bar at the top of the Playbooks window" caption="Search in Playbooks." />

## Bring your own scripts

Sayso comes with six playbooks: Circle Prospecting, Buyer Pond, FSBO, Appt Setting, Open House Visitor and Expired. Upload your own scripts as PDF, Word (DOCX) or text files, and choose which playbook opens first. See [Upload, rename and remove your own playbooks](/help/desktop-app/upload-playbooks/).
