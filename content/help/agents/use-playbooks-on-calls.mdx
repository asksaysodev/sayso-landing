## Open your playbooks

- Press <Kbd>Control</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Mac or <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Windows.
- Or click the Sayso icon in your menu bar or taskbar and choose **Show Playbooks**.
- During a session, click the list icon on the right of the Coach widget.

Playbooks opens without a session, so you can read one before you dial.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/playbooks-1.webp" alt="The Playbooks window" />

## Use it during a call

- Pick the playbook for the calls you're making from the list.
- Search inside a playbook with **Search in playbooks** (<Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd> on Windows). Use the arrows to jump between matches.
- Lead with your script. Glance at Cue when the conversation goes somewhere the script doesn't cover.

## Bring your own scripts

Sayso comes with six ready-made playbooks (Circle Prospecting, Buyer Pond, FSBO, Appt Setting, Open House Visitor and Expired), and you can upload your own scripts as PDF, Word (DOCX) or text files. You can also choose which playbook opens first. See [Upload, rename and remove your own playbooks](/help/desktop-app/upload-playbooks/).
