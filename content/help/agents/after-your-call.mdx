## 1. Open Conversations

Go to app.asksayso.com and click **Conversations**. Each conversation from your dial block is there, newest first, with a summary at the top. A brand-new conversation shows **Creating conversation summary** for a moment while Sayso writes it.

Use the search box or **Filters** to find a specific call. See [Search and filter conversations](/help/web-app/search-and-filter-conversations/).

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/conversations-1.webp" alt="The Conversations page with a call open" />

## 2. Check the notes

Open a conversation and look at the **Smart Capture** tab. Fix anything Sayso heard wrong or missed. See [Edit Smart Capture notes after a call](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).

## 3. Send the notes to your CRM

If you've connected Follow Up Boss or Sure Send, click **Add lead** on the conversation to link it to the lead and send your notes. See [Send your notes to your CRM](/help/agents/send-notes-to-your-crm/).

Using a different CRM? Copy the notes from the Smart Capture tab and paste them in.

## 4. Rate the insights

On the **Cue** tab, give insights a thumbs up or thumbs down. Ratings tell Sayso which prompts help. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).

## 5. Book the follow-up

If you set an appointment, the conversation shows **Booked**. Send the calendar invite and add the appointment in your CRM the way you normally do. Sayso tracks booked appointments on your Dashboard but doesn't create them in your calendar.

<Callout type="tip">
Do this right after each dial block.
</Callout>
