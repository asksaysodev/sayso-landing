## What expired listing calls look like with Sayso

Expired sellers have been burned. Their house sat on the market, it didn't sell, and now every agent in town is calling them. They're skeptical, short-tempered and ready to hang up.

Sayso helps you:

- Open with empathy instead of a pitch
- Ask about what went wrong without sounding like you're blaming the last agent
- Find out whether they still want to sell
- Handle "I'm not listing with anyone" and "I'm taking it off the market"
- Move from discovery into setting the appointment naturally

## Setup

- **Lead type:** click **Start for Seller**.
- **Reset and Buffer Time:** press **Reset** the moment a person answers, with Buffer Time at 0. Expired lists often hit screeners and voicemail first, and Reset keeps those out.
- **Playbooks:** keep your expired script open. Upload your own opener if you have one, something like "I noticed your home was on the market and it didn't sell. I'm sorry about that."

## Features to have open

- **Cue:** surfaces empathy-first questions, motivation probes and objection handlers for expired sellers.
- **Pulse:** pull market data for their ZIP code if they ask "what's happening in the market?" or "why didn't my house sell?"
- **Smart Capture:** captures their situation so you can follow up with personal notes.

## Common objections Cue helps with

- "I'm done trying to sell"
- "I'm going to wait until the market gets better"
- "The last agent didn't do anything"
- "I already have an agent calling me"
- "Just take me off your list"

## Tips

- **Lead with curiosity, not credentials.** Expireds don't care about your track record yet. They care that you understand what went wrong.
- **Let Cue handle the reframes.** When they push back, glance at Cue before you respond. The prompts acknowledge their frustration before redirecting.
- **Rate these prompts often.** Expired calls have strong patterns, and your ratings help Sayso learn what works for you.
