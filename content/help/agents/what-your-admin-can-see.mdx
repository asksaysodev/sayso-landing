## What owners and admins can see

On a team account, owners and admins can filter the web app by team or by agent. For your account they can see:

- **Dashboard:** your **Conversation time**, **Appointments booked**, weekly **Activity** and Coach Tips.
- **Conversations:** each of your conversations, with its summary, the Cue insights you saw, your Smart Capture notes, the Pulse market data you looked up, the lead type, and whether an appointment was booked.
- **Members list:** your name, email, role, team and whether you've joined.

Owners and admins also get weekly or monthly email reports with your company's total conversation time. See [What Sayso stores](/help/support/privacy-and-data/) for the full list of what's saved.

## What they can't do

- **Listen to your calls.** Sayso never records audio.
- **Rate insights or edit notes on your conversations.** Only you can rate your insights and edit your Smart Capture.
- **See your CRM account.** CRM connections are per agent.

## If you leave your team

If an owner or admin removes you from the company, your account and its history are deleted. Copy any notes you need first, and talk to your team leader if you want to keep using Sayso on your own.
