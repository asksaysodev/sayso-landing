Sayso coaches a roleplay the same way it coaches a live call, so the roleplay has to sound like a real conversation.

## Set it up

1. Call your partner from your dialer or on Zoom or Google Meet. Their voice has to come through your computer for Sayso to hear them.
2. Open the Coach widget and start a session for **Seller** or **Buyer**. See [Start a coaching session](/help/desktop-app/start-a-session/).
3. Your partner answers like a real lead.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/reset-a-session-1.webp" alt="The Coach widget during a session with the orange Reset button" />

## Make it sound like a real call

Cue moves through three stages, Connect, Discover and Convert, as it hears what it listens for. See [Connect, Discover, Convert](/help/agents/call-stages/).

- **Give your partner a story:** a reason to buy or sell, a timeline, an area and a price range.
- **Talk for a few minutes.** Cue has little to coach in a 30-second call.
- **Don't read a script.** Reading lines back and forth, or jumping straight to booking the appointment, skips the part of the call where most prompts appear.
- **Check Smart Capture.** It fills in as your partner shares details. See [Use Smart Capture on your calls](/help/agents/use-smart-capture-on-calls/).

## Between roleplays

Press **Reset** before each new roleplay instead of stopping and starting a new session. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

<Callout type="tip">
Use **Full Insights** while you learn. It shows each prompt's full wording. Use **Condensed** on live calls. See [Prompt Style](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/).
</Callout>
