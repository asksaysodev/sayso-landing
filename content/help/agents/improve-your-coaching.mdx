## Rate insights after your calls

In **Conversations**, open the **Cue** tab on a call and give each insight a thumbs up or thumbs down.

- **Thumbs up:** the prompt fit the moment, or you used it.
- **Thumbs down:** it didn't fit the conversation, came at the wrong time, or isn't how you'd say it.

You can rate your own conversations only. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).

## Read your Coach Tips

**Coach Tips** on your Dashboard are personal suggestions based on your own conversations. They appear after your first 10 qualifying conversations and refresh as you make more calls. Until then, the Dashboard says your Coach Tips are on the way. See [Coach Tips](/help/web-app/coach-tips/).

## Watch your numbers

Your Dashboard shows **Conversation time** and **Appointments booked** for the last 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 3 months or 6 months, plus a weekly **Activity** chart. Compare your conversation time with the appointments you booked. See [Your Dashboard](/help/web-app/dashboard/).

## Try the settings that fit your style

- **Prompt Style:** **Full Insights** for complete suggestions, **Condensed** for short directions you say in your own words. See [Prompt Style](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/).
- **Buffer Time:** how long Sayso waits before showing insights. See [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/).
- **Playbooks:** upload your own scripts so your talk track sits next to Cue. See [Upload your own playbooks](/help/desktop-app/upload-playbooks/).
