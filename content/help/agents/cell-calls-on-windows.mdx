Cell phone calls can be passed through Windows using the Phone Link app and connection. The call then plays on your computer, where Sayso coaches it like any other call.

## Set up Phone Link

1. On your PC, open the <Ui>Phone Link</Ui> app.
2. Follow the steps to link your Android phone or iPhone.
3. Open <Ui>Calls</Ui> in Phone Link to make a call, or answer an incoming call there.

## Coach the call

Start Sayso the same way you do for any call. See [Start a coaching session](/help/desktop-app/start-a-session/).
