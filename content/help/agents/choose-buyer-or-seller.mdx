## Why lead type matters

Sayso's prompts change based on who you're calling. A question that fits a seller conversation would fall flat on a buyer call. Picking the right lead type up front keeps the coaching relevant.

## How to choose

1. Open the Coach.
2. Click **Start for Seller** or **Start for Buyer**.

The session starts right away. The lead type you picked shows in the Coach toolbar for the rest of the session.

## Which calls are seller calls and which are buyer calls

Cue works with any prospecting situation: expireds, FSBOs, Zillow and other internet leads, open house leads, circle prospecting, database reactivation, sphere of influence and more. Buyer or Seller is the top-level choice. If a buyer mentions they also need to sell, Cue picks that up.

| Call | Usually start for |
| --- | --- |
| Expireds, FSBOs | Seller |
| Circle prospecting | Seller, but it can go either way |
| Internet leads, open house follow-up | Buyer |
| Sphere of influence, database reactivation | Whichever is most likely for this person |

See [Coaching by lead type](/help/agents/#use-cases) for tips on each.

## Changing lead type

The lead type is locked once you start. **Reset** keeps the same lead type. Finished your seller calls and moving to buyers? Press **Stop**, then click **Start for Buyer**.

<Callout type="tip">
Group your calls. Do all your seller leads in one session and all your buyer leads in another.
</Callout>
