## The six rules

1. **Wear headphones.** On speakers, your mic picks up the person on the call too, which creates echo and doubles the transcript. Wired or Bluetooth both work.
2. **Call from your computer.** Dial from your CRM or dialer on the computer, with its calling option set to internet or computer, not "always use mobile phone". If the call rings on your cell phone, Sayso can't hear it.
3. **Use Google Chrome** for the web app, your CRM and your dialer.
4. **Press Reset every time a new person picks up.** Don't press it in the middle of a conversation. If you skip it, Sayso treats the new person as part of the last conversation.
5. **Find your notes in Conversations.** Every conversation is saved in **Conversations** at app.asksayso.com.
6. **Let your antivirus through on install.** If the install is blocked (common on Windows with PC Matic or McAfee), add Sayso to the allow list or pause your antivirus during the install, then turn it back on.

## Every dial block

1. Headphones on, mic selected.
2. Open the Coach widget: <Kbd>Control</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Windows, or **Launch Coach** from the Sayso menu. Playbooks: <Kbd>Control</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Windows.
3. Click **Start for Seller** or **Start for Buyer**. The lead type can't change during a session, so group your calls.
4. Give prompts about 30 seconds to warm up.
5. A new person picks up: press **Reset** (the orange button).
6. Done for the day: press **Stop** (the red button). Leave **Auto Stop** on to end a session if you walk away.

## During the call

- Follow your script and glance at prompts during pauses. Don't read prompts word for word.
- Try **Condensed** prompts (**Settings**, **Cue**, **Prompt Style**): short directions you say in your own words.
- **Buffer Time:** if you press Reset right when someone picks up, set it to 0.
- Pin a prompt you want to keep, close one you don't need.
- Keep your CRM in a normal window, not full screen, or the Coach widget can hide behind it.

## Quick fixes

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Notes fill in, no prompts | Sayso isn't hearing you. Check your mic and permissions. |
| Nothing from the other person | The call isn't playing through the computer. Check your dialer setting. On Mac, check Screen & System Audio. |
| Echo or repeated transcript | Put headphones on. |
| "Book the appointment" right after hello | Press Reset at every pickup. |
| Coach won't open | Quit Sayso fully from the Sayso menu and reopen it. |
| "Windows protected your PC" | Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. |
