With Calls from iPhone turned on, your iPhone calls play on your Mac, where Sayso coaches them like any other call.

## Set up Calls from iPhone

1. Sign in to the same Apple Account on your iPhone and your Mac, and connect both to the same Wi-Fi network.
2. On your iPhone, open <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Apps</Ui> > <Ui>Phone</Ui> > <Ui>Calls on Other Devices</Ui>, turn on <Ui>Allow Calls on Other Devices</Ui>, then turn on your Mac.
3. On your Mac, open <Ui>FaceTime</Ui> > <Ui>Settings</Ui> and select <Ui>Calls from iPhone</Ui>.
4. Make and answer calls from FaceTime or the Phone app on your Mac.

## Coach the call

Start Sayso the same way you do for any call. See [Start a coaching session](/help/desktop-app/start-a-session/).
