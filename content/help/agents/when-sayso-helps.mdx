Sayso helps only while you're talking with a consumer. It coaches that conversation as it happens: what to ask next, how to handle an objection, when to ask for the appointment.

## What Sayso doesn't change

- **Pickups.** Sayso doesn't get more people to answer.
- **Anything outside the conversation.** Your lists, your dialer and your follow-up schedule work the same as before.

## What to expect

Most agents dial 10 to 30 numbers before they have a conversation. Sayso helps in that one conversation. So the number to watch is what happens in your conversations: more questions asked, objections handled, and conversations that end in a booked appointment.

## Measure it on conversations

- Agents: review your calls in [Conversations](/help/web-app/conversations/).
- Admins and owners: look at conversation time and appointments on the [Dashboard](/help/admins/team-dashboard/), not dial counts.
