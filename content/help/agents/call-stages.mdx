## The three stages

| Stage | What it's for | Prompts you'll see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connect** | Build rapport and earn the right to keep talking. | Openers, rapport, getting past the first brush-off. |
| **Discover** | Find out why they'd move and what stands in the way. | Motivation, timing, price, current situation, objections. |
| **Convert** | Turn what you learned into an appointment. | Asking for the appointment, handling last objections, next steps. |

## How Cue moves from one stage to the next

Cue listens for what's happening in the conversation, with time limits as a backup:

- **Connect to Discover:** once Cue hears rapport building, or about a minute after the other person starts talking, whichever comes first.
- **Discover to Convert:** once Cue has heard both why they might move (their motivation) and something about their situation (timing, readiness or concerns). If that doesn't happen, Cue moves to Convert after about five minutes in Discover.
- **Straight to Convert:** if the other person brings up a time to meet, Cue can jump ahead to the appointment.

Cue doesn't start counting until the other person speaks, so ringing and a slow hello don't push you forward.

## Why Reset matters

Every session and every **Reset** starts back at Connect. If you skip Reset between calls, Cue stays in the stage the last conversation reached, and you get "book the appointment" prompts on a fresh hello. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

## Prompts are suggestions

You're the one on the call. Use a prompt when it fits, say it in your own words, and ignore the ones that don't. Your thumbs up and thumbs down ratings after the call tell Sayso which prompts help. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).
