## How Sayso hears the call

If you can hear the call through your computer's headphones, Sayso can hear it too. If the call rings on your cell phone, Sayso can't.

Sayso doesn't dial. It coaches the conversation while your dialer places the call. Check your dialer's calling setting: it must be internet, computer or "ask me", never "always use mobile phone".

## Dialers and CRMs that work

| Tool | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Follow Up Boss | Call from Follow Up Boss on your computer, in Chrome. Use Calling Lists to dial one lead after another. Connect Follow Up Boss to send notes. |
| Sure Send | Call from Sure Send on your computer. Connect Sure Send with an API token to send notes. |
| Mojo | Dial your Mojo session number from your CRM's computer dialer (not your cell), so the session plays through your computer. |
| Vulcan7, REDX | Use the computer dialer, not call-through to your cell. |
| Google Voice | A free computer dialer if you don't have one. Identity verification can take a day. |
| Browser-based dialers | Any dialer that plays the call in your browser or a desktop app works. |
| Zoom, Google Meet | Works for video consults. The other person's audio counts as the lead. |

## What doesn't work

- Calls placed or answered on your cell phone, including Zillow calls on your phone.
- Dialers set to route calls to your mobile phone.
- A separate desk phone or handset.

<Callout type="tip">
Got a lead call on your cell phone? Call them back from your CRM's computer dialer so Sayso can coach the conversation.
</Callout>

## Sending notes to your CRM

Follow Up Boss and Sure Send connect directly. See [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/). For any other CRM, copy your Smart Capture notes from the Coach widget or from **Conversations** and paste them in.

Want your CRM or dialer to connect directly? [Request an integration](/help/support/request-a-feature/).

## Troubleshooting

- **Sayso isn't hearing the call:** the audio isn't coming through your computer. Check your dialer setting and wear headphones. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).
- **Prompts react to screeners or hold music:** press **Reset** when a person answers, or raise **Buffer Time**. See [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/).
- **Using a power dialer or multi-line dialer?** See [Using Sayso during a dial block or power dialer session](/help/agents/power-dialer-sessions/).
