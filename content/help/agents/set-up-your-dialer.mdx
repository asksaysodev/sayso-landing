## How Sayso hears the call

If you hear the call through your computer, Sayso hears it too. If the call rings on your cell phone, Sayso can't hear it.

Sayso doesn't dial. It coaches while your dialer places the call. Set your dialer's calling setting to internet, computer or "ask me", never "always use mobile phone".

## Dialers and CRMs that work

| Tool | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Follow Up Boss | Call from Follow Up Boss in Chrome on your computer. Use Calling Lists to dial one lead after another. Connect Follow Up Boss to send notes. |
| Sure Send | Call from Sure Send on your computer. Connect Sure Send with an API token to send notes. |
| Mojo | Dial your Mojo session number from your CRM's computer dialer, not your cell. |
| Vulcan7, REDX | Use the computer dialer, not call-through to your cell. |
| Google Voice | A free computer dialer. Identity verification takes up to a day. |
| Browser-based dialers | Any dialer that plays the call in your browser or a desktop app. |
| Zoom, Google Meet | For video consults. The other person's audio counts as the lead. |

## Office phone systems (Zoom Phone, Vonage and others)

Many phone systems have an app for your computer, called a softphone. Sayso works when you talk through that app. You don't need headphones. Sayso doesn't work when the call connects on a desk phone or your cell, even if you clicked to dial on your computer.

1. Open your phone system's app on your computer.
2. Set calls to ring on the computer, not a desk phone or your cell.
3. Make a test call. If you hear the other person through your computer, Sayso hears them too.

## What doesn't work

- Calls placed or answered on your cell phone, including Zillow calls.
- Dialers set to route calls to your cell phone.
- A desk phone or handset, even if you clicked to dial on your computer.

<Callout type="tip">
Got a lead call on your cell phone? Call them back from your CRM's computer dialer.
</Callout>

## Sending notes to your CRM

Follow Up Boss and Sure Send connect directly. See [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/). For any other CRM, copy your Smart Capture notes from the Coach widget or from **Conversations** and paste them in.

To ask for a direct connection to your CRM or dialer, [request an integration](/help/support/request-a-feature/).

## Troubleshooting

- **Sayso isn't hearing the call:** the audio isn't playing through your computer. Check your dialer's calling setting. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).
- **Prompts react to screeners or hold music:** press **Reset** when a person answers, or raise **Buffer Time**. See [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/).
- **Power dialer or multi-line dialer:** see [Using Sayso during a dial block or power dialer session](/help/agents/power-dialer-sessions/).
