## Accept your invite

1. On the computer you make calls from, open the invite email from Sayso and click the link.
2. You see **You've been invited** and a **Join** button with your company's name.
3. Enter your **First name**, **Last name** and a **Password**, then confirm the password. Your email is already filled in.
4. Click **Join** followed by your company name.

Your company's subscription covers you, so you don't pick a plan. You land on your Dashboard. Next, [download the desktop app](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).

<Callout type="note">
Use the email address the invite was sent to. To use a different email, ask your admin to invite that address.
</Callout>

## Can't find the invite email?

1. Search your inbox for **Sayso**. The invite comes from Sayso, not your admin.
2. Check your spam, junk and promotions folders.
3. Check the inbox of the email address your admin invited.
4. If it's not there, ask your admin to resend it. See [Resend or revoke an invite](/help/admins/resend-or-revoke-invite/).

## If the invite doesn't work

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This invite has expired | Invites last 14 days. | Ask your admin to resend it. |
| This invite has been revoked | Your admin cancelled this invite. | Ask your admin for a new invite. |
| This invite link isn't valid | The link is incomplete or wrong. | Open the full link from the email. If it still fails, ask your admin. |
| This invite has already been accepted | Your account already exists. | Sign in at app.asksayso.com. |
| Too many attempts | Too many tries in a short time. | Wait a minute, then try again. |

## Already have your own Sayso account?

If you signed up before your team invited you, the invite can't create a second account with the same email. Email support@asksayso.com with your email address and your team's name. We'll fix it with your admin.

## What changes when you join a team

- Your coaching minutes come from your team's plan, not your own subscription.
- Your team's owners and admins can see your conversations and activity. See [What your team admin can see](/help/agents/what-your-admin-can-see/).
- Your team's owner handles billing. You won't see billing options in Settings.
