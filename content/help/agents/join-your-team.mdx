## Accept your invite

1. Open the invite email from Sayso and click the link. Use the full link from the email.
2. You'll see **You've been invited** and a **Join** button with your company's name.
3. Enter your **First name**, **Last name** and a **Password**, then confirm the password. Your email is already filled in from the invite.
4. Click **Join** followed by your company name.

That's it. You don't pick a plan: your company's subscription covers you. You land on your Dashboard, where you can [download the desktop app](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).

<Callout type="note">
Use the email address the invite was sent to. If you want to use a different email, ask your admin to send a new invite to that address.
</Callout>

## If the invite doesn't work

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This invite has expired | Invites last 14 days. | Ask your admin to resend it. |
| This invite has been revoked | Your admin cancelled this invite. | Ask your admin for a new invite. |
| This invite link isn't valid | The link is incomplete or wrong. | Open the full link from the email. If it still fails, ask your admin. |
| This invite has already been accepted | Your account already exists. | Sign in at app.asksayso.com. |
| Too many attempts | Too many tries in a short time. | Wait a minute, then try again. |

## Already have your own Sayso account?

If you signed up on your own before your team invited you, the invite can't create a second account with the same email. Email support@asksayso.com with your email address and your team's name, and we'll sort it out with your admin.

## What changes when you join a team

- Your coaching minutes come from your team's plan, not your own subscription.
- Your team's owners and admins can see your conversations and activity. See [What your team admin can see](/help/agents/what-your-admin-can-see/).
- Billing is handled by your team's owner, so you won't see billing options in Settings.
