Work through these steps in order. Each step links to its full article.

## 1. Get into your account

- **Signing up on your own:** create your account at app.asksayso.com. See [Create your Sayso account](/help/web-app/create-account/).
- **Joining a team:** open the invite email from your team and click the link. See [Join your team from an invite](/help/agents/join-your-team/).

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/download-sayso-1.webp" alt="The Download Sayso window" />

## 2. Install the desktop app

1. In the web app, click **Download Sayso** and pick your computer. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).
2. Install it on [Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/) or [Windows](/help/desktop-app/install-on-windows/).
3. Sign in with the same email and password you use in the web app. See [Sign in to the desktop app](/help/desktop-app/sign-in-desktop/).

## 3. Allow the permissions

Sayso needs your microphone to hear you. On a Mac it also needs **Screen & System Audio** to hear the person on the call.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

Follow [Allow microphone and screen & system audio on Mac](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/). You'll need to quit and reopen Sayso once after you turn on Screen & System Audio.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

Follow [Allow microphone access on Windows](/help/desktop-app/windows-microphone-permission/).

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## 4. Set up your audio and dialer

- Put on headphones. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).
- Set your dialer to place calls through your computer, not your cell phone. See [Set up your dialer for Sayso](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/).

## 5. Find Sayso

Sayso lives in your Mac menu bar or your Windows taskbar, not in the Dock. See [Sayso's menu explained](/help/desktop-app/tray-menu/), then keep its icon visible:

- [Find Sayso in the Mac menu bar](/help/desktop-app/find-sayso-mac-menu-bar/)
- [Keep Sayso visible on the Windows taskbar](/help/desktop-app/pin-sayso-windows-taskbar/)

## 6. Make your first coached call

Follow [Your first coached call](/help/agents/your-first-coached-call/). Before a full dial block, do a test call with a teammate to confirm Sayso hears both sides.

## 7. Optional: connect your CRM

If you use Follow Up Boss or Sure Send, connect it to send call notes to the lead. See [Connect Follow Up Boss](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/) or [Connect Sure Send](/help/web-app/connect-sure-send/).
