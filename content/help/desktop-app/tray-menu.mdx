Sayso runs in the background. You control it from the **Sayso menu**: click the Sayso icon (the lightning bolt) in your Mac menu bar or in the Windows taskbar notification area.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/tray-menu-1.webp" alt="The Sayso menu open with Launch Coach, Show Playbooks, Settings, My Account, Help Center, Quit and Log Out" caption="The Sayso menu." />

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/tray-menu-windows.webp" alt="Sayso's menu on Windows, opened from the taskbar, with the Alt + Shift shortcuts" caption="On Windows." width={520} />

Can't find the icon? See [Find Sayso in the Mac menu bar](/help/desktop-app/find-sayso-mac-menu-bar/) or [Keep Sayso visible on the Windows taskbar](/help/desktop-app/pin-sayso-windows-taskbar/).

## Menu items

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> | Opens the Coach widget, the small bar you use during calls. Changes to <Ui>Close Coach</Ui> while the Coach widget is open. Shortcut: <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Windows. |
| <Ui>Show Playbooks</Ui> | Opens your scripts in their own window. Changes to <Ui>Hide Playbooks</Ui> while it's open. Shortcut: <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Mac, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> on Windows. |
| <Ui>Settings</Ui> | Opens desktop settings: General, Cue, Pulse, Playbooks, Accessibility and Software Update. See [Desktop settings](/help/desktop-app/desktop-settings/). |
| <Ui>My Account</Ui> | Opens the web app in your browser, already signed in, on your Dashboard. Shows <Ui>Opening…</Ui> for a moment. |
| <Ui>Update Available</Ui> | Appears only when a new version is ready. Opens Settings on the Software Update tab. See [Update the desktop app](/help/desktop-app/update-sayso/). |
| <Ui>Help Center</Ui> | Opens the Sayso Help Center in your browser. |
| <Ui>Quit</Ui> | Closes Sayso completely. See [Quit and reopen Sayso](/help/desktop-app/quit-sayso/). |
| <Ui>Log Out</Ui> | Signs you out of the desktop app. Shows <Ui>Log In</Ui> when you're signed out. See [Log out or switch accounts](/help/desktop-app/log-out-desktop/). |

## Why an item is missing or grayed out

- **<Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> and <Ui>Show Playbooks</Ui> are missing:** you're signed out, or the account has no active subscription. Sign in, or check [your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/). On a team, ask your owner or admin.
- **Items are grayed out:** Sayso lost its internet connection and is reconnecting, or an update is installing (<Ui>Update in progress…</Ui>). They come back once Sayso reconnects or the update finishes. <Ui>My Account</Ui> still works while reconnecting.
- **<Ui>Settings</Ui> is missing:** you're signed out.
- **<Ui>Move to Applications Folder</Ui> appears (Mac):** Sayso isn't in your Applications folder and can't update. See [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/).

<Callout type="tip">
Opening Sayso again from your Applications folder, Dock, Start menu or a taskbar pin while it's already running opens this same menu.
</Callout>
