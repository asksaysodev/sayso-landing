Pulse pulls local market stats mid-call, so you can answer "what are homes doing around here?" with numbers.

## Look up a ZIP code

1. During a session, click the <Ui>Zip Code</Ui> field in the Coach widget.
2. Type a 5-digit US ZIP code. Pulse shows <Ui>Processing Information</Ui> while it loads, then opens the results under the Coach widget.
3. Pick a property type tab: <Ui>Single Family</Ui>, <Ui>Multi-Family</Ui> or <Ui>Condo</Ui> (only types with data appear).
4. Click the arrow next to the ZIP to hide or show the results.

## What you get

| Stat | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Median Price</Ui> | The middle sale price, which isn't skewed by one unusual sale. |
| <Ui>Median Price / Sq Ft</Ui> | A quick way to compare value across home sizes. |
| <Ui>Median Days on Market</Ui> | How fast homes are moving. |
| <Ui>New Listings</Ui> | Listings added this calendar month. |
| <Ui>Market</Ui> | Whether it's a buyer's market, a seller's market or balanced. |
| <Ui>L90D Price Trend</Ui> | Whether median prices went up, down or held steady over the last 90 days. |

Hover over any stat to see a plain-English explanation you can use on the call. A dash means there's no data for that stat.

## When it helps

- A circle prospecting or farm call where they ask what their home is worth.
- An internet lead asking about a different area.
- An expired listing seller asking why their home didn't sell.

Don't read the whole panel out loud. Pick one number that makes your point, then get back to booking the appointment.

## Messages

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "No market data available for ZIP …" | There's no data for that ZIP code. Check the number, or try a nearby ZIP. |
| <Ui>Pulse data is temporarily unavailable.</Ui> | Click <Ui>Try again</Ui> in a moment. |

## Good to know

- US ZIP codes only. Canadian postal codes aren't supported.
- Pulse only works during a session. The ZIP code clears when you press Reset or Stop.
- Every lookup is saved with the conversation, so you can see it later in the web app.
- To hide the Zip Code field, open <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Pulse</Ui> and set <Ui>Visible in Coach</Ui> to <Ui>Hidden</Ui>. If there's no Pulse tab in Settings, Pulse isn't included in your account.
- If a number looks wrong, tell us at support@asksayso.com.
