On Windows, Sayso puts its lightning bolt icon in the notification area of the taskbar, next to the clock. Windows usually hides new icons, so you may need to look behind the <Ui>^</Ui> arrow first.

## Find Sayso

1. Click the <Ui>^</Ui> (Show hidden icons) arrow next to the clock.
2. Click the Sayso bolt. The [Sayso menu](/help/desktop-app/tray-menu/) opens.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/pin-sayso-windows-taskbar-1.webp" alt="The Windows hidden icons panel with the Sayso icon next to the taskbar" width={520} />

## Keep the icon always visible

The quickest way is to drag the Sayso bolt out of the hidden icons panel and drop it on the taskbar next to the clock.

Or use Windows Settings:

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Windows 11">

1. Right-click an empty spot on the taskbar and click <Ui>Taskbar settings</Ui>.
2. Expand <Ui>Other system tray icons</Ui>.
3. Turn on <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows 10">

1. Right-click an empty spot on the taskbar and click <Ui>Taskbar settings</Ui>.
2. Under <Ui>Notification area</Ui>, click <Ui>Select which icons appear on the taskbar</Ui>.
3. Turn on <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Pin Sayso to the taskbar

Pinning adds a Sayso button to your taskbar. Clicking it opens Sayso if it's closed, or opens the Sayso menu if it's already running.

1. Open the Start menu and find <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.
2. Right-click it and click <Ui>Pin to taskbar</Ui>.

Sayso also puts a shortcut on your desktop when it installs.

<Callout type="tip">
You can open the Coach widget without the icon. Press <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> from any app. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/desktop-app/keyboard-shortcuts/).
</Callout>
