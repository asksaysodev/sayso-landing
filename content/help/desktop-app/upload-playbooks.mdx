You manage playbooks in the desktop app's settings.

## Upload a playbook

1. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> from the Sayso menu or the gear in the Coach widget.
2. Click <Ui>Playbooks</Ui>.
3. Drag your file onto <Ui>Drop a file here, or browse</Ui>, or click <Ui>browse</Ui> to pick it.
4. In <Ui>Enter a name for this Script</Ui>, give it a clear name, like "Expired Opener" or "FSBO Objections".
5. Click <Ui>Upload</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/upload-playbooks-1.webp" alt="Settings, Playbooks tab, with the upload area and the Scripts list" caption="Settings > Playbooks." />

The new playbook shows <Ui>Processing</Ui> while Sayso prepares it, then <Ui>Ready</Ui>. If it shows <Ui>Failed</Ui>, Sayso couldn't read the file; delete it and try a cleaner version.

### File rules

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| File types | PDF, DOCX (Word) or TXT |
| File size | Up to 20 MB |
| How many | Up to 10 of your own playbooks, on top of Sayso's |

Using Google Docs? Download it first with <Ui>File</Ui> > <Ui>Download</Ui> > <Ui>PDF Document</Ui> or <Ui>Microsoft Word</Ui>. At 10 playbooks, Sayso shows "Limit reached" and you'll need to delete one before uploading another.

## The Scripts list

Below the upload area, <Ui>Scripts</Ui> lists every playbook with its <Ui>NAME</Ui>, <Ui>OWNER</Ui> and <Ui>STATUS</Ui>. Owner is <Ui>Private</Ui> for ones you uploaded (only you see them) and <Ui>Sayso</Ui> for the ones that come with Sayso.

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Rename one of yours | Click the edit (pencil) icon, type the new name, click <Ui>Save</Ui>. |
| Choose your favorite | Click the star. Your favorite opens first when <Ui>Default Script</Ui> is set to <Ui>Favorite</Ui>. Any ready playbook, yours or Sayso's, can be the favorite. |
| Change the order | Drag a playbook by its handle. The Playbooks window lists them in this order. |
| Delete one of yours | Click the trash icon. It's removed right away, with no confirmation. |

Sayso's own playbooks can't be renamed or deleted.

## Default Script

<Ui>Default Script</Ui> ("Shown first each time you open a new session") sets which playbook opens first: <Ui>Last Used</Ui> (the default) or <Ui>Favorite</Ui>.

## Write scripts that work on a call

Playbooks show your script as written, and you read them mid-call. Before you upload:

- Remove notes to yourself, filler and anything you wouldn't say out loud.
- Split a big script book into one file per call type: expired, FSBO, buyer lead, sphere, circle prospecting.
- Use bullets and short phrases instead of paragraphs: an opener, your top objection responses, a transition into questions, and how you ask for the appointment.

Long or messy PDF? Email it to support@asksayso.com and we'll help clean it up.

<Callout type="note">
Playbooks you upload are private to your account. To share a script with your team, each agent uploads their own copy. See [Sharing scripts with your team](/help/admins/share-scripts-with-your-team/).
</Callout>
