<Callout type="tip" title="First step, always">
[Quit Sayso completely](/help/desktop-app/quit-sayso/) from the Sayso menu and open it again. That clears a waiting update or an expired sign-in. On a Mac, if the icon is missing, use Apple menu > <Ui>Force Quit</Ui>, choose Sayso, then reopen it from Applications.
</Callout>

## Find your symptom

| What you see | Fix |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> isn't in the Sayso menu | You're signed out or the account has no active subscription. Sign in, or check [your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/). On a team, ask your owner or admin. |
| The shortcut does nothing | Same as above, or Sayso is reconnecting to the internet. Wait a moment and try again. On a Mac, use <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd>, not <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd>. |
| The Permissions screen opens instead of the Coach | Sayso is missing microphone access. See [Mac permissions](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/) or [Windows microphone access](/help/desktop-app/windows-microphone-permission/). |
| <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> is grayed out | Sayso is reconnecting, or an update is installing. Wait for it to finish. |
| Nothing happens when you click <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> | An update may be waiting. Quit fully and reopen. Big updates can take a few minutes. |
| The Coach opened on another screen | It opens on the screen your mouse is on. Move your mouse to the screen you want and open it again. |
| "Sayso needs to be in your Applications folder" (Mac) | Click <Ui>Move to Applications</Ui>. See [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/). |
| The app won't open at all on an older Mac | Sayso needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer. Check Apple menu > <Ui>About This Mac</Ui>. |
| Permissions are on, but Sayso says they're missing (Mac) | See [Permissions look on but Sayso says they are missing](/help/desktop-app/permissions-missing-on-mac/). |
| Signing in keeps sending you back to the sign-in screen | Your app may be out of date. Download the latest version and install it over the old one. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/). |
| Can't find Sayso after installing (Windows) | Click the <Ui>^</Ui> next to the clock. See [Keep Sayso visible on the Windows taskbar](/help/desktop-app/pin-sayso-windows-taskbar/). |
| Windows install fails or stalls | See [Antivirus or SmartScreen blocked Sayso](/help/desktop-app/antivirus-blocking/). |

## Check your Mac version

On a Mac, the wrong version for your chip (Intel on Apple Silicon, or the other way around) can fail without an error. See [Which Mac do I have?](/help/desktop-app/which-mac-chip/)

## Still not working

Email support@asksayso.com with:

- Mac or Windows, and the version.
- On a Mac, whether you downloaded Apple Silicon or Intel.
- What happens when you click <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> (an error, nothing, a flicker).
- [Your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).
