## Microsoft Edge won't keep the download

Edge may block the installer and say it isn't commonly downloaded.

1. In Edge's downloads list, hover over the Sayso installer and click <Ui>...</Ui> (More actions).
2. Click <Ui>Keep</Ui>.
3. If Edge asks again, click <Ui>Show more</Ui>, then <Ui>Keep anyway</Ui>.
4. Click <Ui>Open file</Ui>.

## "Windows protected your PC"

Windows SmartScreen sometimes shows this for new apps. The Sayso installer is signed by AskSayso, Inc.

1. Click <Ui>More info</Ui>.
2. Check that the publisher shows AskSayso, Inc.
3. Click <Ui>Run anyway</Ui>.

Do this only for an installer you downloaded from app.asksayso.com.

## Antivirus blocks the install or the app

If the installer stalls or fails, or Sayso disappears after installing, your antivirus blocked it. PC Matic and McAfee have blocked Sayso.

1. Open your antivirus program.
2. Find Sayso in its quarantine or blocked list and restore it.
3. Add Sayso to the allow list (also called exclusions or exceptions).
4. Run the installer again.

If you can't find the setting, pause your antivirus during the install, then turn it back on. On a company computer, ask your IT team to allow Sayso.

## On a Mac

Sayso for Mac is signed and notarized by Apple. The first time you open it, macOS says it was downloaded from the internet. Click <Ui>Open</Ui>. If your company's security software blocks it, ask your IT team to allow Sayso.
