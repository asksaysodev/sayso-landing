## "Windows protected your PC"

Windows SmartScreen sometimes shows this for apps it hasn't seen many times before. The Sayso installer is signed by AskSayso, Inc.

1. Click <Ui>More info</Ui>.
2. Check that the publisher shows AskSayso, Inc.
3. Click <Ui>Run anyway</Ui>.

Only do this for an installer you downloaded from the Sayso web app at app.asksayso.com.

## Antivirus blocks the install or the app

If the installer stalls, fails, or Sayso disappears after installing, your antivirus probably blocked it. PC Matic and McAfee have both done this.

1. Open your antivirus program.
2. Look for Sayso in its quarantine or blocked list and restore or allow it.
3. Add Sayso to the allow list (sometimes called exclusions or exceptions).
4. Run the installer again.

If you can't find the setting, pause your antivirus just for the install, then turn it back on right after. On a company computer, ask your IT team to allow Sayso.

## On a Mac

Sayso for Mac is signed and notarized by Apple. The first time you open it, macOS may say it was downloaded from the internet; click <Ui>Open</Ui>. If your company uses security software that blocks apps, ask your IT team to allow Sayso.
