## Open Settings

- Click the Sayso icon in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows), then <Ui>Settings</Ui>.
- Or click the gear in the Coach widget before you start a session.

Use the <Ui>Search</Ui> box at the top of Settings to jump to any setting by name, for example "auto stop" or "font".

## Every setting

| Tab | Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| <Ui>General</Ui> | <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> | Starts Sayso in the background when you sign in to your computer. [More](/help/desktop-app/launch-at-login/) | Disabled |
| <Ui>General</Ui> | <Ui>Meeting detection</Ui> | Asks to launch Sayso when a call starts in your browser. [More](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/) | Enabled |
| <Ui>General</Ui> | <Ui>Auto Stop</Ui> | Ends a session after the lead has been silent this long, 2 to 10 minutes. [More](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/) | 5 min |
| <Ui>Cue</Ui> | <Ui>Prompt Style</Ui> | <Ui>Full Insights</Ui> or <Ui>Condensed</Ui> prompts. [More](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/) | Full Insights |
| <Ui>Cue</Ui> | <Ui>Buffer Time</Ui> | Seconds to wait after Start or Reset before coaching begins, 0 to 30. [More](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/) | 0s |
| <Ui>Pulse</Ui> | <Ui>Visible in Coach</Ui> | Shows or hides the Zip Code field for [Pulse](/help/desktop-app/pulse/) market data. | Visible |
| <Ui>Playbooks</Ui> | Upload, <Ui>Scripts</Ui> | Add, rename, star, reorder and delete playbooks. [More](/help/desktop-app/upload-playbooks/) | |
| <Ui>Playbooks</Ui> | <Ui>Default Script</Ui> | Which playbook opens first: <Ui>Last Used</Ui> or <Ui>Favorite</Ui>. | Last Used |
| <Ui>Accessibility</Ui> | <Ui>Font Size</Ui> | <Ui>Small</Ui>, <Ui>Medium</Ui> or <Ui>Large</Ui> text in the Coach widget and Playbooks. [More](/help/desktop-app/font-size/) | Small |
| <Ui>Software Update</Ui> | | Shows your version and installs updates. [More](/help/desktop-app/update-sayso/) | |

The <Ui>Pulse</Ui> tab only appears if Pulse is included in your account.

## Where settings are saved

Everything except <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> is saved to your Sayso account, so it follows you to any computer you sign in on. <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> is set separately on each computer.

Changes to Cue, Pulse and Auto Stop apply to the next session you start.

Account settings like your name, password, two-factor authentication, CRM connections and billing live in the web app instead. See [Account Settings in the web app](/help/web-app/settings-overview/).
