New insights push older ones down and off the list. When Cue gives you something you want to come back to, like a question you haven't asked yet, pin it.

## Pin or unpin

- Click the pin icon on an insight. It moves to the top of the list, above a divider, and stays there.
- To unpin it, click the pin again. (When you hover over a pinned insight's pin, it shows a crossed-out pin.)

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/pin-insights-1.webp" alt="The insights list with one pinned insight above the divider" caption="Pinned insights sit above the divider." />

## Limits

- You can pin up to **three** insights at a time. Once three are pinned, the pin on other insights shows <Ui>Max 3 pins reached</Ui>. Unpin one to pin another.
- Pins last for the current conversation. When you press Reset or Stop, the list starts empty for the next one.
- You can still close a pinned insight with its <Ui>X</Ui>.
