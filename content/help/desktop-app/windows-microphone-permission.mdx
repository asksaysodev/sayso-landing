On Windows, Sayso needs one thing: permission to use your microphone. It hears the lead through your computer's audio output, which needs no permission.

Microphone access is on by default in Windows, so most people never see this screen. If it's off, Sayso shows a <Ui>Permissions</Ui> screen after you sign in, and again any time you try to start a session.

## Turn on microphone access

1. On the Sayso Permissions screen, click <Ui>Open Microphone Settings</Ui>. Windows opens <Ui>Settings</Ui> at <Ui>Privacy & security</Ui> > <Ui>Microphone</Ui>.
2. Turn on <Ui>Microphone access</Ui>.
3. Turn on <Ui>Let desktop apps access your microphone</Ui>.
4. Go back to Sayso. The Microphone row turns green within a couple of seconds.
5. Click <Ui>Continue →</Ui>.

No restart is needed on Windows.

<Callout type="note">
Windows has no separate switch for Sayso. The two switches above cover every desktop app at once, so both must be on.
</Callout>

## Still blocked?

- Some company-managed computers lock these settings. Ask your IT team to allow microphone access for desktop apps.
- If your microphone works in other apps but not in Sayso, see [My microphone isn't working](/help/desktop-app/microphone-not-working/).
