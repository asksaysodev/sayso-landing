The Coach widget changes shape depending on whether a session is running.

## Before a session

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/coach-window-tour-1.webp" alt="The idle Coach with labels on the drag handle, Start buttons, settings gear and close button" caption="The Coach before a session." />

<Clip src="/help/clips/call-timelapse.mp4" poster="/help/clips/call-timelapse.webp" alt="A full call in the Coach widget, sped up, with Cue prompts stacking and Smart Capture filling in" caption="A full call, sped up 6 times." width={592} />

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| Drag handle (dotted grip on the left) | Click and drag to move the Coach widget. |
| <Ui>Start for Seller</Ui> and <Ui>Start for Buyer</Ui> | Starts a coaching session for that kind of lead. See [Start a session](/help/desktop-app/start-a-session/). |
| Gear | Opens desktop [Settings](/help/desktop-app/desktop-settings/). |
| <Ui>X</Ui> | Closes the Coach widget. Sayso keeps running in your menu bar or taskbar. |

## During a session

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/coach-window-tour-2.webp" alt="The active Coach with labels on the lead type, Zip Code, Reset, Stop, timer, insights and Playbooks buttons, with insights and the LPMAMA row below" caption="The Coach during a session." />

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Buyer</Ui> or <Ui>Seller</Ui> label | The lead type you chose. It can't be changed mid-session. |
| <Ui>Zip Code</Ui> | Type a 5-digit ZIP to pull up [Pulse](/help/desktop-app/pulse/) market data. Shown when Pulse is on. |
| Reset (orange, circular arrow) | Saves the current conversation and starts a fresh one for the next call. See [Reset a session](/help/desktop-app/reset-a-session/). |
| Stop (red, square) | Ends the session and saves it. See [Stop a session](/help/desktop-app/stop-and-save/). |
| Timer | How long this session has been running (hours:minutes:seconds). |
| Insights (check circle) | Shows or hides Cue's insights. A red dot means new insights arrived while they were hidden. |
| Playbooks (list) | Opens or closes your [Playbooks](/help/desktop-app/playbooks/) next to the Coach widget. |

## Below the bar

When the insights panel is open, it shows:

- **Cue's insights:** pinned insights at the top, then the newest insights. Each has a pin and an <Ui>X</Ui>. See [Cue](/help/desktop-app/cue/) and [Pin an insight](/help/desktop-app/pin-insights/).
- **The Smart Capture row:** six letters for Location, Price, Motivation, Agent, Mortgage and Appointment, plus a copy button. See [Smart Capture](/help/desktop-app/smart-capture/).

Messages also appear under the bar, for example when Sayso is reconnecting or a session couldn't start. See [Messages in the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/out-of-minutes-messages/).
