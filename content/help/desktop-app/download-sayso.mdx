Sayso has two parts. The **web app** at app.asksayso.com is where you manage your account and review your conversations. The **desktop app** is what coaches you live on your calls, so you need it installed on the computer you call from.

## Before you start

- Create your account and choose your plan first. See [Create your Sayso account](/help/web-app/create-account/).
- Check that your computer is supported: macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, or Windows 10 or 11. See [System requirements](/help/support/system-requirements/).
- Download on the computer you make your calls from.

## Download the app

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com).
2. Click <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui> in the left sidebar. The <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui> window opens. The version that matches your computer is marked <Ui>RECOMMENDED</Ui>.
3. Pick your version:

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

Under <Ui>Sayso for Mac</Ui>, click <Ui>Download</Ui> and choose <Ui>Apple Silicon</Ui> or <Ui>Intel</Ui>.

Picking the wrong one is the most common reason Sayso doesn't work on a Mac. If you are not sure, click <Ui>Not sure which chip you have?</Ui> or read [Which Mac do I have?](/help/desktop-app/which-mac-chip/)

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

Under <Ui>Sayso for Windows</Ui>, click <Ui>Download</Ui>. The installer works on Windows 10 and 11.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The file lands in your Downloads folder. Next, follow [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/) or [Install Sayso on Windows](/help/desktop-app/install-on-windows/).

## On a phone or tablet

Sayso is a desktop app, so it can't be installed on a phone. If you open the download from your phone, Sayso offers to email you the link instead:

1. Enter your email address.
2. Tap <Ui>Send me the Link</Ui>.
3. Open the email on your computer and download from there.

<Callout type="tip">
You can also go straight to [app.asksayso.com/download](https://app.asksayso.com/download). It opens your Dashboard with the download window already open.
</Callout>
