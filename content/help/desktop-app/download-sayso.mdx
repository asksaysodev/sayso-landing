Sayso has two parts. The **web app** at app.asksayso.com is where you manage your account and review conversations. The **desktop app** coaches you live on calls. Install it on the computer you call from.

<Callout type="warning" title="Get Sayso only from app.asksayso.com">
Sayso isn't in the Mac App Store or the Microsoft Store, and apps with similar names aren't Sayso. Download it from the web app.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- Create your account and choose your plan. See [Create your Sayso account](/help/web-app/create-account/).
- Your computer needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, or Windows 10 or 11. See [System requirements](/help/support/system-requirements/).
- Download on the computer you make your calls from.

## Download the app

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com).
2. Click <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui> in the left sidebar. The version that matches your computer is marked <Ui>RECOMMENDED</Ui>.
3. Pick your version:

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

Under <Ui>Sayso for Mac</Ui>, click <Ui>Download</Ui> and choose <Ui>Apple Silicon</Ui> or <Ui>Intel</Ui>.

The wrong one is the most common reason Sayso doesn't work on a Mac. To check, click <Ui>Not sure which chip you have?</Ui> or read [Which Mac do I have?](/help/desktop-app/which-mac-chip/)

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

Under <Ui>Sayso for Windows</Ui>, click <Ui>Download</Ui>.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

The file lands in your Downloads folder. Next, follow [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/) or [Install Sayso on Windows](/help/desktop-app/install-on-windows/).

## On a phone or tablet

Sayso doesn't install on a phone. If you open the download on your phone, Sayso offers to email you the link.

1. Enter your email address.
2. Tap <Ui>Send me the Link</Ui>.
3. Open the email on your computer and download from there.

<Callout type="tip">
[app.asksayso.com/download](https://app.asksayso.com/download) opens your Dashboard with the download window open.
</Callout>
