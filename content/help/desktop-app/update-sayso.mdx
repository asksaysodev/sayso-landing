## How updates work

Sayso checks for a new version when it opens and every hour after that. When one is ready, Sayso shows <Ui>New Update Available</Ui> with two choices:

- <Ui>Update Now</Ui> downloads the update. Sayso closes the Coach, installs the update and restarts on its own. You don't need to click anything else.
- <Ui>Not Now</Ui> hides the prompt. You can update later from the Sayso menu or from Settings.

<Callout type="note" title="Never mid-call">
If an update arrives while you're in a coaching session, Sayso waits. The prompt shows after your session ends, and the Sayso menu shows <Ui>Update Available</Ui> in the meantime.
</Callout>

## Update later

You have two places to update from:

- **The Sayso menu:** click the Sayso icon in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows), then click <Ui>Update Available</Ui>. It opens Settings on the <Ui>Software Update</Ui> tab.
- **Settings:** open <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Software Update</Ui> and click <Ui>Update Now</Ui>.

While an update downloads, the Sayso menu shows <Ui>Update in progress…</Ui> and the Coach and Settings items are unavailable for a moment.

## Check which version you have

Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Software Update</Ui>. When you're current, it says <Ui>Sayso vX.X.X is up to date.</Ui> Click <Ui>Check for Updates</Ui> to check right now.

## If the update won't install

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Move Sayso to Applications to install updates.</Ui> (Mac) | Quit Sayso, drag it into your Applications folder, then open it from there. See [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/). |
| <Ui>No internet connection. Please check your network and try again.</Ui> | Reconnect to the internet, then click <Ui>Try Again</Ui>. |
| <Ui>Couldn't reach the update server. Please try again shortly.</Ui> | Wait a few minutes and click <Ui>Try Again</Ui>. |
| Your Mac asks for your password after an update | This is expected. Enter it to finish. |

If an update keeps failing, download the latest version from the web app and install it over the old one, choosing <Ui>Replace</Ui> on a Mac. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).
