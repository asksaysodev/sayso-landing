Every time you start a session or press Reset, Sayso waits for the Buffer Time before Cue starts prompting and Smart Capture starts taking notes. It's there so ringing, call-screening messages ("please state your name") and voicemail greetings don't get treated as the conversation.

## Recommended: 0 seconds

Leave it at 0 (the default) and press Reset when a person answers and starts talking. Coaching starts right away and nothing from before the pickup gets in.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/prompt-style-1.webp" alt="Desktop Settings, Cue tab, with Buffer Time" />

## When to add seconds

- You press Reset as soon as you dial, before anyone answers.
- Your dialer plays a screening message or menu after the person picks up.

In those cases, set enough seconds to cover what plays before the person is on the line. Start around 7 to 10 seconds and adjust.

## Change it

1. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> from the Sayso menu or the gear in the Coach widget.
2. Click <Ui>Cue</Ui>.
3. Drag the <Ui>Buffer Time</Ui> slider. You can choose from 0 to 30 seconds.

The setting is saved to your account. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/) for how Reset and Buffer Time work together.

## Buffer Time and Auto Stop are different

| | Auto Stop | Buffer Time |
|---|---|---|
| What it does | Ends the session after the lead has been silent too long | Waits before coaching starts |
| When it applies | During a session, when no one is talking | Right after you press Start or Reset |
| Where to set it | Settings > General | Settings > Cue |
| Range | 2 to 10 minutes (default 5) | 0 to 30 seconds (default 0) |

Read more about [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/).
