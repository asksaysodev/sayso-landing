The desktop app uses the same account as the web app. There is no separate desktop password.

## Sign in

1. Open Sayso. The <Ui>Welcome</Ui> screen appears.
2. Enter your <Ui>Email</Ui> and <Ui>Password</Ui>.
3. Click <Ui>Log In</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/sign-in-desktop-1.webp" alt="The Sayso desktop Welcome screen with email and password fields" caption="The desktop sign-in screen." />

If you turned on two-factor authentication, Sayso shows <Ui>One More Step</Ui>. Open your authenticator app, type the 6-digit code for Sayso and click <Ui>Verify</Ui>. If the code is rejected, wait for a new code and try again. <Ui>Back to Log In</Ui> takes you back to the first screen.

The first time you sign in on a computer, Sayso walks you through permissions next. See [Mac permissions](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/) or [Windows microphone access](/help/desktop-app/windows-microphone-permission/).

## No account yet, or forgot your password?

- <Ui>Sign Up</Ui> opens account creation in your browser. See [Create your Sayso account](/help/web-app/create-account/).
- <Ui>Forgot password? Click here</Ui> opens password reset in your browser. See [Reset your password](/help/web-app/reset-password/).

## "Your session has expired"

Sayso keeps you signed in, but a sign-in can expire. When it does, Sayso shows <Ui>Your session has expired. Please log in again.</Ui> Sign in again on the Welcome screen.

## Signed in but you can't launch the Coach

<Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> and <Ui>Show Playbooks</Ui> only appear in the Sayso menu when your account has an active subscription. If you're on a team, ask your team's owner or admin to check the subscription. If it's your own account, see [Your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/).
