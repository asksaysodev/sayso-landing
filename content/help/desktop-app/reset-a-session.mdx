The orange **Reset** button sits next to Stop in the Coach during a session. Press it every time a new person picks up.

## What Reset does

In one click, Reset:

1. Ends and saves the conversation you just had. It shows up in [Conversations](/help/web-app/conversations/) in the web app like any other.
2. Starts a new conversation right away with the same lead type (Buyer or Seller).
3. Clears the insights, the Smart Capture row and the Pulse ZIP code, and sets the timer back to 0:00:00.
4. Restarts Cue at the start of the call, so the next person gets opening prompts, not prompts meant for the end of your last call. Your [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/), if you set one, starts again too.

There's no confirmation step, so Reset is instant.

## When to press it

- When a new person answers. Not when you dial, when a person picks up.
- After a voicemail, before your next live conversation.
- On a power or multi-line dialer, at every pickup.

Never press it in the middle of a conversation; Cue would start that person over from the top. More on the habit in [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

## Grab your notes before you reset

Reset clears the Smart Capture row in the Coach. If you want to paste the notes into your CRM right away, click the copy button at the end of the Smart Capture row first. If you forget, the notes are saved with the conversation in the web app. See [Smart Capture](/help/desktop-app/smart-capture/).

## Reset vs. Stop

| Button | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Reset | The next call is starting and you're keeping the same lead type. |
| Stop | Your dial block is over, or you need to switch between Buyer and Seller. |
