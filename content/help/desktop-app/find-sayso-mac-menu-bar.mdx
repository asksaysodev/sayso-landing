On a Mac, Sayso puts a lightning bolt icon in the menu bar at the top right of your screen, near the clock, Wi-Fi and battery icons. Click it to open the [Sayso menu](/help/desktop-app/tray-menu/).

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/find-sayso-mac-menu-bar-1.webp" alt="The Mac menu bar with the Sayso lightning bolt icon highlighted" caption="The Sayso bolt in the Mac menu bar." />

## Keep the bolt visible

The menu bar fills up fast, and on MacBooks with a camera notch, icons that don't fit are hidden behind the notch. Move the bolt to the right so it always shows:

1. Hold down <Kbd>⌘ Command</Kbd>.
2. Click the Sayso bolt and drag it to the right, toward the clock.
3. Let go.

You can also free up space by removing menu bar icons you don't use (hold <Kbd>⌘ Command</Kbd> and drag them down off the menu bar), or with a menu bar manager app.

## The bolt isn't there

1. **Check that Sayso is open.** Open your Applications folder and double-click <Ui>Sayso</Ui>. If Sayso is already running, this opens the Sayso menu.
2. **Check that it's allowed in the menu bar.** On newer versions of macOS, open <Ui>System Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Menu Bar</Ui> and make sure Sayso is turned on.
3. **Make room.** Quit a few other menu bar apps, or move the bolt to the right as shown above.

## Use the Dock instead

While Sayso is running, its icon also shows in your Dock. Clicking it opens the Sayso menu. To keep it there:

1. Right-click the Sayso icon in the Dock.
2. Click <Ui>Options</Ui>.
3. Click <Ui>Keep in Dock</Ui>.

<Callout type="tip">
You don't need the icon at all to open the Coach. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> from any app. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/desktop-app/keyboard-shortcuts/).
</Callout>
