When something stops a session from starting or running, the Coach shows a message under the bar. Click the <Ui>X</Ui> on a message to close it.

## Minutes and subscription

| Message | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| <Ui>Your team is out of coaching minutes. Let your team leader know.</Ui> | The account has used all the coaching minutes in its plan for this billing period. | On a team, tell your owner or admin. On your own account, see [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/) and [Your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/). |
| <Ui>Your team's Sayso subscription isn't active. Let your team leader know.</Ui> | The subscription is cancelled, paused or has a payment problem. | On a team, tell your owner. On your own account, check [Your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/). |
| <Ui>Your team has reached its coaching hour limit. Let your team leader know.</Ui> | Your team's owner or admin set a monthly hour cap for your team, and it's used up. | Ask your owner or admin to raise it. See [Set a team's monthly hours](/help/admins/set-team-hours/). |
| <Ui>We can't start a session right now. Please try again later.</Ui> | Sayso couldn't start the session for an account reason. | Try again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, [contact support](/help/support/contact-support/). |

These messages say "team leader" even on a solo account. If it's your own account, you're the one to check the subscription. Agents on a team can also read [Your team is out of coaching minutes](/help/agents/out-of-minutes/).

## Connection

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| <Ui>No internet connection</Ui>, "we'll reconnect automatically." | Wait. Sayso reconnects on its own and the message goes away. The Start buttons and Sayso menu items are grayed out until it does. |
| <Ui>Couldn't connect to Sayso. Check your network and try again.</Ui> | Check your Wi-Fi or network, then start again. |
| <Ui>We couldn't start Cue. Please try again.</Ui> | Start again. If it keeps happening, quit and reopen Sayso. |

## Microphone and audio

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Sayso couldn't access your microphone. Another app may be using it.</Ui> | Close other apps that might be holding the microphone, then start again. See [My microphone isn't working](/help/desktop-app/microphone-not-working/). |
| <Ui>Sayso isn't receiving microphone audio yet.</Ui> | Check that the right microphone is selected and not muted. If you just stopped a session, wait a few seconds before starting again. |
| <Ui>Sayso lost your microphone and couldn't reconnect it. Click Reset to start a new session.</Ui> | Your headset was unplugged or the microphone dropped. Reconnect it and press Reset. |
| <Ui>Sayso's audio engine isn't ready. Restart the app and try again.</Ui> | [Quit and reopen Sayso](/help/desktop-app/quit-sayso/). If it continues, reinstall. |

If the Permissions screen opens when you try to start, Sayso is missing a permission. See [Mac permissions](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/) or [Windows microphone access](/help/desktop-app/windows-microphone-permission/).

## Sign-in and saving

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Your session has expired. Please re-login from the main window.</Ui> | Your sign-in expired. Sign in again from the Sayso menu. |
| <Ui>Your session stopped, but we couldn't save it cleanly.</Ui> | Check Conversations in the web app. If it's missing, [send us your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/). |
