The first time you sign in on a Mac, Sayso shows a <Ui>Permissions</Ui> screen with two items. Sayso needs both to coach you:

| Permission | Why Sayso needs it |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Microphone</Ui> | To capture your side of the conversation during calls |
| <Ui>Screen & System Audio</Ui> | To capture audio from your leads during conversations |

Sayso only captures audio during active calls. It does not record or save your screen. See [Why Sayso needs these permissions](/help/support/why-permissions/).

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions-1.webp" alt="The Sayso Permissions screen on Mac with Microphone and Screen and System Audio rows" caption="The Permissions screen on a Mac." />

## Turn on both permissions

1. Click <Ui>Allow Microphone</Ui>. When macOS asks, click <Ui>Allow</Ui>. The Microphone row turns green.
2. Click <Ui>Open System Settings</Ui>. macOS shows its own screen recording prompt. Click its <Ui>Open System Settings</Ui> button.
3. In System Settings, under <Ui>Privacy & Security</Ui>, open <Ui>Screen & System Audio Recording</Ui> (called <Ui>Screen Recording</Ui> on some macOS versions).
4. Turn on the switch next to <Ui>Sayso</Ui>. If macOS asks, enter your Mac password.
5. Go back to Sayso and click <Ui>Quit and Reopen →</Ui>.

Sayso restarts. If both permissions are on, Sayso opens in the menu bar.

<Callout type="note" title="Why Sayso has to restart">
macOS only applies the screen and system audio permission when an app starts. Sayso can't see it until it reopens, which is why the last step is <Ui>Quit and Reopen</Ui>. If the permission was not turned on, Sayso shows the Permissions screen again after it reopens.
</Callout>

## If you clicked "Don't Allow"

macOS won't ask a second time. Turn the permission on yourself:

1. Open the Apple menu, then <Ui>System Settings</Ui>.
2. Click <Ui>Privacy & Security</Ui>.
3. Open <Ui>Microphone</Ui> and turn on <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.
4. Open <Ui>Screen & System Audio Recording</Ui> (or <Ui>Screen Recording</Ui>) and turn on <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.
5. Quit Sayso from the menu bar and open it again.

## Sayso keeps asking even though both are on

This usually means there are two copies of Sayso, or permissions from an older version are stuck. See [Permissions look on but Sayso says they are missing](/help/desktop-app/permissions-missing-on-mac/).
