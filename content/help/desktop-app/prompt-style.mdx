Prompt Style changes how much Cue says in each insight.

| Style | What you get | Example |
|---|---|---|
| <Ui>Full Insights</Ui> | A complete suggestion with context. This is the default. | "Ask 'What got you thinking about moving initially?' to uncover their motivation without pushing." |
| <Ui>Condensed</Ui> | A short direction you take in at a glance and say in your own words. Condensed also skips some types of prompts to keep things lean. | "Ask about timing." |

## Our recommendation: Condensed

On live calls, Condensed keeps you in the conversation. You glance, get the idea and say it your way, instead of reading a paragraph while the lead is talking. It feels odd for the first few calls; within a dial block or two, glancing becomes automatic.

Full Insights is useful when you're learning how a conversation should flow, like practicing with a roleplay partner or studying prompts after a call.

## Change it

1. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> from the Sayso menu or the gear in the Coach.
2. Click <Ui>Cue</Ui>.
3. Under <Ui>Prompt Style</Ui>, choose <Ui>Full Insights</Ui> or <Ui>Condensed</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/prompt-style-1.webp" alt="Sayso Settings, Cue tab, with Prompt Style and Buffer Time" caption="Settings > Cue." />

The change applies to the next session you start, and it's saved to your account.
