Smart Capture listens to the conversation and fills in the key details as they come up, organized around **LPMAMA**:

| Letter | Field |
|---|---|
| L | Location |
| P | Price |
| M | Motivation |
| A | Agent |
| M | Mortgage |
| A | Appointment |

## Read it during the call

The Smart Capture row sits at the bottom of the insights panel in the Coach widget: six letters and a copy button.

- A letter changes look once Sayso has captured something for that field.
- Hover over a letter to read what was captured, for example "Price: around 600k". A field with nothing yet says "No data yet", which tells you what to ask next.

## Copy your notes

Click the copy button at the end of the row. A check mark confirms it. Sayso copies all six fields, one per line, ready to paste into your CRM.

Do this before you press Reset, because Reset clears the row for the next conversation.

## After the call

Smart Capture notes are saved with each conversation in the web app. There you can:

- Edit any field Sayso got wrong. See [Edit Smart Capture notes](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).
- Send the notes to Follow Up Boss or Sure Send. See [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/).

## Good to know

- Smart Capture can mishear names and street names. Glance before you paste.
- If you set a [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/), Smart Capture waits for it, just like Cue.
- Smart Capture is part of your coaching session. If the row doesn't appear, it isn't included in your account.
