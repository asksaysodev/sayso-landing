## Before you start

- Your Mac needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer.
- Download the version that matches your chip. See [Which Mac do I have?](/help/desktop-app/which-mac-chip/)

## Install

1. Open your Downloads folder and double-click the Sayso file. An <Ui>Install Sayso</Ui> window opens.
2. Drag the Sayso icon onto the <Ui>Applications</Ui> folder in that window.
3. If your Mac asks whether to replace an older copy, choose <Ui>Replace</Ui>. Never choose <Ui>Keep Both</Ui>.
4. When it finishes copying, eject the installer (drag it from your desktop or the Finder sidebar to the Trash, or right-click it and choose <Ui>Eject</Ui>).

<Callout type="warning" title="Always choose Replace">
Two copies of Sayso fight over permissions. Sayso will then say permissions are missing even when they look turned on. If you already have two copies, see [Permissions look on but Sayso says they are missing](/help/desktop-app/permissions-missing-on-mac/).
</Callout>

## Open Sayso for the first time

1. Open your Applications folder and double-click <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.
2. If macOS says the app was downloaded from the internet, click <Ui>Open</Ui>.
3. Sign in with your Sayso email and password. See [Sign in to the desktop app](/help/desktop-app/sign-in-desktop/).
4. Allow the two permissions Sayso asks for. See [Allow microphone and screen and system audio on Mac](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/).

## "Sayso needs to be in your Applications folder"

If you open Sayso straight from the installer or from your Downloads folder, Sayso stops and shows <Ui>Move Sayso to Applications</Ui>. It can't install updates from anywhere else.

- Click <Ui>Move to Applications</Ui>. Sayso moves itself and reopens. Your Mac may ask for your password.
- If Sayso can't move itself, it shows <Ui>Show in Finder</Ui>. Quit Sayso, drag it into your Applications folder yourself, then open it from there.

## The install window never showed up

If you use an external monitor as your only screen, the installer can open on a display you can't see. Open System Settings, go to <Ui>Displays</Ui>, set your displays to mirror, then open the installer again.
