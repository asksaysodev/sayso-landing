The **Coach** is the small floating bar where you start a session and see Cue's prompts, Smart Capture and Pulse during a call. It stays on top of your other windows, including a full-screen browser, so you can see it while you dial.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/open-the-coach-1.webp" alt="The Coach bar before a session, with Start for Seller and Start for Buyer" caption="The Coach, ready for your next call." />

## Four ways to open it

1. **The Sayso menu:** click the Sayso icon in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows) and click <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui>.
2. **Keyboard:** press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on a Mac, or <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> on Windows.
3. **When a call starts:** if [meeting detection](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/) is on, Sayso asks <Ui>Sayso identified a call</Ui> when a call starts in your browser. Choose <Ui>Start for Seller</Ui> or <Ui>Start for Buyer</Ui> and the Coach widget opens with the session already started.
4. **From the web app:** click <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> in the web app's left sidebar. Your browser may ask to open Sayso; allow it. The desktop app has to be installed on that computer.

The Coach widget opens near the top-left corner of the screen your mouse is on. Drag it anywhere you like. See [Move the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/move-and-resize-coach/).

## If the Coach widget doesn't open

- **Nothing happens:** check that you're signed in and your account has an active subscription. <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> is hidden from the Sayso menu without one.
- **The Permissions screen opens instead:** Sayso doesn't have microphone access yet. See [Mac permissions](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/) or [Windows microphone access](/help/desktop-app/windows-microphone-permission/).
- **Still nothing:** see [The Coach widget or Sayso won't open](/help/desktop-app/coach-wont-open/).

## Close the Coach widget

Before a session, click the <Ui>X</Ui> on the right side of the Coach widget, press the shortcut again, or choose <Ui>Close Coach</Ui> in the Sayso menu. During a session, click Stop first so your conversation is saved.
