## Open and close Sayso windows

These work from any app while Sayso is running, even when your dialer or CRM is in front. Press the same shortcut again to close the window.

| Action | Mac | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| Open or close the Coach widget | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> | <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> |
| Open or close Playbooks | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> | <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> |

On a Mac, the shortcut uses the <Kbd>Control</Kbd> key, not <Kbd>⌘ Command</Kbd>. <Kbd>⌘ Command</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> still saves in your other apps as usual.

The shortcuts do nothing while you're signed out, while your account has no active subscription, or while Sayso is reconnecting to the internet. The Playbooks shortcut also needs Playbooks to be included in your account. Shortcuts can't be changed.

<Callout type="warning" title="Close the Coach widget only after you Stop">
Pressing the Coach widget shortcut during a session closes the Coach widget. Click Stop first so your conversation is saved. See [Stop a session](/help/desktop-app/stop-and-save/).
</Callout>

## Search inside a playbook

| Action | Mac | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| Jump to the search box | <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd> | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd> |
| Next match | <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>G</Kbd> | <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>G</Kbd> |
| Previous match | <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>G</Kbd> | <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>G</Kbd> |
| Clear the search | <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> | <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> |

See [Playbooks](/help/desktop-app/playbooks/) for more.
