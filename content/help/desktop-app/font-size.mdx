Make the text bigger on a laptop or a monitor that's far away, or smaller on a big screen to fit more.

## Change it

1. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> from the Sayso menu or the gear in the Coach widget.
2. Click <Ui>Accessibility</Ui>.
3. Under <Ui>Font Size</Ui>, choose <Ui>Small</Ui>, <Ui>Medium</Ui> or <Ui>Large</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/font-size-1.webp" alt="Settings, Accessibility tab, with Font Size options Small, Medium and Large" caption="Settings > Accessibility > Font Size." />

The default is <Ui>Small</Ui>. The size applies to the Coach widget and the Playbooks window, and the Coach widget grows to fit. It's saved to your account. If you see <Ui>Failed to save. Please try again.</Ui>, check your internet connection and choose again.

## Other ways to make the Coach widget easier to read

- Use [Condensed prompts](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/) so there's less to read.
- Hide insights you don't need with their <Ui>X</Ui>, or hide the whole panel with the insights button and bring it back when the red dot appears.
- [Move the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/move-and-resize-coach/) close to where you look while dialing.
