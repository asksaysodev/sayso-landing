## Move the Coach widget

Click and hold the dotted grip on the left end of the Coach widget, then drag it wherever you like. Most agents keep it at the top of the screen, close to where they're looking during calls.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/move-and-resize-coach-1.webp" alt="The Coach with the drag grip on the left highlighted" caption="Drag the Coach by its grip." />

## Where the Coach widget opens

Each time you open it, the Coach widget appears near the top-left corner of the screen your mouse pointer is on. With two monitors, move your mouse to the monitor you want before you open the Coach widget (for example with the keyboard shortcut), and it opens there.

## Size

You don't resize the Coach widget by hand. It grows and shrinks on its own as insights, Smart Capture and Pulse appear, and it stays clear of your Dock or taskbar. To make everything in it bigger or smaller, change the font size. See [Change the text size](/help/desktop-app/font-size/).

## It stays on top

The Coach widget stays above your other windows, including a full-screen browser, so it doesn't get lost behind your dialer or CRM. It doesn't add its own button to the Windows taskbar.

## The Playbooks window

The Playbooks window opens next to the Coach widget. Unlike the Coach widget, you can resize it: drag any edge or corner. It also stays on top of your other windows.

<Callout type="tip">
On a small laptop screen, use a smaller [font size](/help/desktop-app/font-size/) and keep the Coach widget at the very top, so your dialer stays visible underneath.
</Callout>
