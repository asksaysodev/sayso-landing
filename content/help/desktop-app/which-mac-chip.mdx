Sayso has one version for Macs with Apple Silicon and one for Macs with an Intel processor. The wrong version can install fine and then fail without a clear error, so check before you download.

## Check your chip

1. Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen.
2. Click <Ui>About This Mac</Ui>.
3. Look at the line that says <Ui>Chip</Ui> or <Ui>Processor</Ui>.

| What you see | Download |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Chip</Ui>: Apple M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer | <Ui>Apple Silicon</Ui> |
| <Ui>Processor</Ui>: something with Intel in it | <Ui>Intel</Ui> |

Not sure? Most Macs bought in late 2020 or later have Apple Silicon. Most Macs from 2019 or earlier are Intel.

## Already installed the wrong version?

1. Quit Sayso from the Sayso menu (click the Sayso icon in the menu bar, then <Ui>Quit</Ui>).
2. Open your Applications folder and drag Sayso to the Trash.
3. Download the right version and install it again. See [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/).
