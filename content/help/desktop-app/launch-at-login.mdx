Sayso only coaches you, and only notices calls, while it's running. Turning on <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> means you never have to remember to open it.

## Turn it on

1. Click the Sayso icon in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows).
2. Click <Ui>Settings</Ui>.
3. On the <Ui>General</Ui> tab, set <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> to <Ui>Enabled</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/launch-at-login-1.webp" alt="Sayso Settings, General tab, with Launch at Login set to Enabled" caption="Settings > General > Launch at Login." />

From then on, Sayso starts quietly in the background when you sign in to your computer. No window opens; the icon simply appears in your menu bar or taskbar.

It's off until you turn it on. This setting belongs to the computer, so set it on each computer you use.

## Turn it off

Set <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> back to <Ui>Disabled</Ui>. You can also remove Sayso from your computer's startup list:

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

<Ui>System Settings</Ui> > <Ui>General</Ui> > <Ui>Login Items & Extensions</Ui> (or <Ui>Login Items</Ui>), then remove Sayso from <Ui>Open at Login</Ui>.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

<Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Apps</Ui> > <Ui>Startup</Ui>, then turn off Sayso. You can also do this in Task Manager on the <Ui>Startup apps</Ui> tab.

</Tab>
</Tabs>
