## Log out

1. Click the Sayso icon in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows).
2. Click <Ui>Log Out</Ui>.

If a coaching session is running, Sayso stops it and saves the conversation before signing you out. The Coach widget, Playbooks and Settings windows close, and the sign-in screen opens.

Logging out of the desktop app doesn't sign you out of the web app in your browser. Use <Ui>Log out</Ui> in the web app's user menu for that.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/tray-menu-1.webp" alt="Sayso's menu with Log Out" width={520} />

## Sign in with a different account

1. Log out as shown above.
2. On the <Ui>Welcome</Ui> screen, sign in with the other account's email and password.

To sign back in later, click the Sayso icon and choose <Ui>Log In</Ui>, or just open Sayso.

<Callout type="note">
Your desktop settings that live on your account (Meeting detection, Auto Stop, Prompt Style, Buffer Time, Pulse, Playbooks and Font Size) follow you to any computer you sign in on. <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> stays with the computer.
</Callout>
