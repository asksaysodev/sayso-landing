If you finish your last call and forget to press Stop, Auto Stop ends the session for you so it doesn't keep counting time.

## How it works

Auto Stop ends a session when **both** of these are true:

- The session has been running longer than your Auto Stop time.
- Sayso hasn't heard the lead (the other person) speak for longer than your Auto Stop time.

The default is **5 minutes**. When it triggers, the Coach shows <Ui>Session Stopped</Ui> with "We have stopped the session due to inactivity. You can launch a new session to continue." Click <Ui>Understood</Ui>. The conversation is saved like any other.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/auto-stop-1.webp" alt="The Session Stopped message in the Coach after Auto Stop" caption="What you see when Auto Stop ends a session." />

## Change the time

1. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> from the Sayso menu or the gear in the Coach.
2. Click <Ui>General</Ui>.
3. Drag the <Ui>Auto Stop</Ui> slider. You can choose from 2 to 10 minutes.

The setting is saved to your account, so it follows you to any computer.

<Callout type="note">
The setting's description in the app says "How long after the call starts before Sayso automatically stops the session." In practice, the clock that matters is how long the lead has been silent, as described above.
</Callout>

## What we recommend

Leave it at 5 minutes. Between calls, do only what has to happen now: send the calendar invite if you booked an appointment, copy your notes into your CRM, and dial the next lead. Save follow-up emails and CRM cleanup for after you press Stop.

If Auto Stop ends sessions in the middle of your dial block, your gaps between conversations are longer than your Auto Stop time. Raise it a little, or press Stop and start again after a long break.
