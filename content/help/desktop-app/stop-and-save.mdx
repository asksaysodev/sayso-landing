## Stop the session

Click the red **Stop** button in the Coach. The Coach shows <Ui>Saving conversation</Ui>, then goes back to the Start buttons, ready for your next session.

Stop when your dial block is over, or when you need to switch between Buyer and Seller.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/stop-and-save-1.webp" alt="The Coach showing Saving conversation after Stop" caption="Sayso saves the conversation when you stop." />

## Where it goes

Every conversation is saved to **Conversations** in the web app at app.asksayso.com. Open it from the Sayso menu with <Ui>My Account</Ui>, then click <Ui>Conversations</Ui> in the sidebar.

Each saved conversation includes a summary, Cue's insights, your Smart Capture notes and any Pulse data you looked up. From there you can rate insights, edit your notes and send them to Follow Up Boss or Sure Send. See [Find and review your conversations](/help/web-app/conversations/) and [What's saved for each conversation](/help/web-app/what-is-saved/).

Sayso never records or stores audio. See [Privacy and your data](/help/support/privacy-and-data/).

## Other ways a session ends

| What happened | What it means |
|---|---|
| You pressed Reset | The conversation was saved and a new one started. See [Reset a session](/help/desktop-app/reset-a-session/). |
| <Ui>Session Stopped</Ui>: "…due to inactivity" | [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/) ended the session because the lead was silent for too long. Click <Ui>Understood</Ui> and start a new session when you're ready. |
| <Ui>Session Stopped</Ui>: "This session is no longer available…" | The session ended on Sayso's side. Click <Ui>Understood</Ui> and start a new one. |
| You logged out | Sayso stopped and saved the session first. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Stop before you close or quit">
Click Stop before you close the Coach or quit Sayso. Closing the Coach or quitting mid-session ends the audio right away and may not save the conversation cleanly.
</Callout>

If the Coach shows <Ui>Your session stopped, but we couldn't save it cleanly.</Ui>, the session ended but saving had a problem. Check Conversations in the web app; if the conversation is missing, [send us your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).
