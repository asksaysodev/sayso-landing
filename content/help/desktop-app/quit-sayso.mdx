Closing the Coach doesn't close Sayso. Sayso keeps running in your menu bar or taskbar so it's ready for your next call. To close it completely, quit it from the Sayso menu.

## Quit Sayso

1. If a coaching session is running, click Stop in the Coach so the conversation is saved.
2. Click the Sayso icon in your menu bar (Mac) or taskbar (Windows).
3. Click <Ui>Quit</Ui>.

While Sayso is closed, it can't coach you or ask to launch when a call starts.

## Open it again

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

Open your Applications folder and double-click <Ui>Sayso</Ui>, or click Sayso in your Dock if you kept it there. You can also press <Kbd>⌘ Command</Kbd> + <Kbd>Space</Kbd>, type Sayso and press <Kbd>Return</Kbd>.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

Double-click the Sayso shortcut on your desktop, or open the Start menu and click <Ui>Sayso</Ui>.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## If Sayso is frozen or the icon is gone

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

1. Open the Apple menu and click <Ui>Force Quit</Ui>.
2. Select <Ui>Sayso</Ui> and click <Ui>Force Quit</Ui>.
3. Open Sayso again from Applications.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

1. Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to open Task Manager.
2. Select <Ui>Sayso</Ui> and click <Ui>End task</Ui>.
3. Open Sayso again from the desktop shortcut or Start menu.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="tip">
Quitting and reopening is the first thing to try for almost any problem. It also installs an update that's waiting.
</Callout>
