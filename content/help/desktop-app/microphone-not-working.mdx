Sayso uses whichever microphone is selected as your computer's input, and it follows that setting if you change it.

<Callout type="tip" title="First step, always">
[Quit Sayso](/help/desktop-app/quit-sayso/) from the Sayso menu and open it again.
</Callout>

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

### Check the permission

1. Open <Ui>System Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Privacy & Security</Ui> > <Ui>Microphone</Ui>.
2. Make sure <Ui>Sayso</Ui> is on.

Permission on but Sayso says it's missing? See [Permissions look on but Sayso says they are missing](/help/desktop-app/permissions-missing-on-mac/).

### Check which microphone is selected

1. Open <Ui>System Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Sound</Ui> > <Ui>Input</Ui>.
2. Select your headset microphone.
3. Talk and make sure the input level moves.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

### Check the permission

1. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Privacy & security</Ui> > <Ui>Microphone</Ui>.
2. Make sure <Ui>Microphone access</Ui> and <Ui>Let desktop apps access your microphone</Ui> are both on.

### Check which microphone is selected

1. Right-click the speaker icon in the taskbar and click <Ui>Sound settings</Ui>.
2. Under <Ui>Input</Ui>, select your headset microphone.
3. Talk and make sure the level bar moves.

Bluetooth headset mic cutting out? Some Windows laptops struggle with Bluetooth microphones. Use a wired headset, or keep the Bluetooth headset for listening and set the laptop microphone as your input.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Other causes

- **Another app is holding the microphone.** If the Coach widget says <Ui>Sayso couldn't access your microphone. Another app may be using it.</Ui>, close other calling or recording apps and start again.
- **Your headset was unplugged mid-call.** If the Coach widget says <Ui>Sayso lost your microphone and couldn't reconnect it</Ui>, reconnect it and press Reset.
- **You just stopped a session.** If the Coach widget says <Ui>Sayso isn't receiving microphone audio yet</Ui>, wait a few seconds and try again.
- **Safari.** Open your dialer and CRM in Google Chrome. Safari has grabbed the microphone in the past.
- **Muted headset.** Check the mute switch on your headset cable or earcup.

Connect your headset before you start a session. Switching audio devices mid-call is the hardest case for any app.

## Still not working

Email support@asksayso.com with your computer (Mac or Windows) and its version, the headset you use, your dialer and CRM, and [your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).
