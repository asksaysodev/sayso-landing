Uninstalling the desktop app doesn't delete your Sayso account or your saved conversations. Those stay in the web app. To close your account, see [Delete your account or data](/help/web-app/delete-your-account/).

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

1. If a session is running, click Stop in the Coach widget first.
2. Click the Sayso icon in the menu bar, then <Ui>Quit</Ui>.
3. Open your Applications folder.
4. Drag <Ui>Sayso</Ui> to the Trash. Your Mac may ask for your password.
5. Empty the Trash.

To reinstall cleanly, delete every copy of Sayso from Applications first (including ones named "Sayso 2"), empty the Trash, then follow [Install Sayso on a Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/).

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

1. If a session is running, click Stop in the Coach widget first.
2. Click the Sayso icon near the clock, then <Ui>Quit</Ui>.
3. Open <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Apps</Ui> > <Ui>Installed apps</Ui> (on Windows 10: <Ui>Apps & features</Ui>).
4. Find <Ui>Sayso</Ui>, click the <Ui>...</Ui> menu (or select it) and click <Ui>Uninstall</Ui>.
5. Follow the prompts.

Uninstalling also removes Sayso from your startup apps if you had <Ui>Launch at Login</Ui> turned on.

To reinstall, download the latest installer and run it. See [Install Sayso on Windows](/help/desktop-app/install-on-windows/).

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="tip">
A reinstall fixes most problems that a restart doesn't, especially "permissions look on but Sayso says they're missing" on a Mac.
</Callout>
