Cue is the core of Sayso. While you talk, it suggests the next question, reframe or objection response, right in the Coach. Prompts are suggestions; you stay in control of the call.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/cue-1.webp" alt="The Coach during a call with Cue insights listed below the bar" caption="Cue's insights appear under the Coach." />

## How prompts appear

- The first insight opens the insights panel under the Coach on its own.
- The newest insight is at the top of the list and stands out from the rest. Up to five recent insights stay in the list; older ones drop off as new ones arrive.
- Click the <Ui>X</Ui> on an insight to remove it. The others move up.
- Click the pin on an insight to keep it at the top. See [Pin an insight](/help/desktop-app/pin-insights/).
- To hide the panel, click the insights button (the check circle) in the Coach. While it's hidden, a red dot on that button tells you a new insight arrived. Click it again to show them.

## How Cue follows the call

Cue coaches each conversation through three stages:

- **Connect:** building rapport and earning the right to ask questions.
- **Discover:** motivation, timing, money and their agent situation.
- **Convert:** booking the appointment.

Cue moves forward as the conversation covers each stage, starting from the moment the lead starts talking. That's why [Reset](/help/desktop-app/reset-a-session/) matters: press it when a new person picks up, and the new conversation starts at Connect instead of halfway through your last call. More in [Connect, Discover, Convert](/help/agents/call-stages/).

## Make prompts fit how you work

- **[Prompt Style](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/):** short directions (Condensed) or full sentences with context (Full Insights).
- **[Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/):** how long Cue waits after you start or reset before it begins.
- **Lead type:** pick Seller or Buyer when you start, so Cue coaches for the right conversation.

## What Cue needs

- Headphones, so Sayso hears you and the lead separately and cleanly.
- The call playing through your computer, not a cell phone.

See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/). If prompts aren't showing, see [Why am I not seeing prompts?](/help/desktop-app/no-prompts/)

## Help Cue learn your style

After the call, rate insights with a thumbs up or down in Conversations in the web app. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).

<Callout type="tip">
Glance, don't read. Lead with your own words or your script and use Cue's prompts in the pauses.
</Callout>
