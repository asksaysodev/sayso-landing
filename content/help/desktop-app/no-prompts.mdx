<Callout type="tip" title="First step, always">
[Quit Sayso completely](/help/desktop-app/quit-sayso/) from the Sayso menu and open it again. A waiting update or an expired sign-in is often the cause.
</Callout>

## Find your symptom

| What you see | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Smart Capture fills in, but no prompts | Sayso isn't hearing you | Check that your headset is your computer's selected microphone and Sayso has microphone access. See [My microphone isn't working](/help/desktop-app/microphone-not-working/). |
| Nothing at all, no notes and no prompts | Sayso can't hear the call | Place the call from your computer, not your cell phone. Set your dialer to call through the computer or internet, not "always use mobile phone". On a Mac, check the screen and system audio permission. See [Sayso hears me but not the lead](/help/desktop-app/lead-not-heard/). |
| Prompts are off-topic, or say "book the appointment" right after hello | No Reset since the last conversation | Press [Reset](/help/desktop-app/reset-a-session/) every time a new person picks up. |
| Prompts are messy or repeat | You're on speakers | Wear headphones, so the microphone doesn't pick up the lead's voice too. |
| Prompts take a long time to start | Buffer Time, or the normal warm-up | Give it the first exchange of the call. If you Reset at pickup, set [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/) to 0. |
| The insights panel is closed | The panel was hidden | Click the insights button (check circle) in the Coach widget. A red dot means new insights are waiting. |
| A message under the Coach widget | Sayso stopped the session or couldn't start it | See [Messages you might see in the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/out-of-minutes-messages/). |

## Other things to check

- **Lead type:** you started with <Ui>Start for Seller</Ui> or <Ui>Start for Buyer</Ui> to match the person you're calling.
- **Browser:** open your CRM and dialer in Google Chrome. Safari has grabbed the microphone in the past.
- **Voicemails:** voicemail greetings can trigger prompts. Reset before your next live conversation.
- **Mac version:** Sayso needs macOS 13 or newer.
- **The timer is running:** if the Coach widget shows the Start buttons, no session is running. Start one.

## Still not seeing prompts

Email support@asksayso.com with:

- The dialer and CRM you use.
- Whether Smart Capture notes are filling in.
- How far into the call you were.
- Your logs. See [Send your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).
