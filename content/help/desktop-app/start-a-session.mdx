A **session** is one coached conversation. Sayso listens, Cue prompts you, Smart Capture takes notes, and when you stop, the conversation is saved to your web app.

## Start a session

1. Put on your headphones and get your dialer ready. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).
2. [Open the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/open-the-coach/).
3. Click <Ui>Start for Seller</Ui> or <Ui>Start for Buyer</Ui>, depending on who you're calling.

The Coach widget shows <Ui>Starting conversation</Ui> for a moment, then grows into the session view with a timer. You're live.

If you started from the <Ui>Sayso identified a call</Ui> prompt, hover <Ui>Start</Ui> and it splits into <Ui>Start for Seller</Ui> and <Ui>Start for Buyer</Ui>. See [Meeting detection](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/).

## Choosing Seller or Buyer

Cue tailors its prompts to the lead type, so pick the one that matches the person on the other end. The choice is locked once the session starts. If you picked the wrong one, click Stop, then start again with the right type. See [Choose Buyer or Seller before you start](/help/agents/choose-buyer-or-seller/).

## What to expect in the first minute

- Cue needs a little conversation before it has something useful to say, so the first prompt usually shows after the opening exchange.
- If you set a [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/), Cue and Smart Capture wait that long before they start.
- The first insight opens the insights panel on its own.

## Starting on every dial

On a dial block, keep one session running and press Reset each time someone new picks up. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

## If the session won't start

The Coach widget shows a message under the bar. See [Messages in the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/out-of-minutes-messages/). The Start buttons are grayed out while Sayso is reconnecting to the internet.
