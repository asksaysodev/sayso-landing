## Search an address

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) or tap the Sayso Map icon on your home screen.
2. Type the address in <Ui>Enter an address</Ui> and tap a suggestion to search.
3. To search without a suggestion, enter the street number and street name, plus the city and state or ZIP code. Tap <Ui>Search</Ui>. If you see “Add city and state, or pick from the list,” complete the address or select a suggestion.

Each search uses one Comp Report. If you're logged out, <Ui>Create your account</Ui> opens first. The search finishes after you confirm your email or log in. See [Create a Sayso Map account and log in](/help/tools/get-sayso-map/).

On a free account, the dots next to <Ui>Free Comp Reports</Ui> fill as you use them. See [Sayso Map plans and billing](/help/tools/sayso-map-plans/).

## Read the results

- **Subject Property** is the home you searched. It shows details such as beds, baths and square feet. On a phone, tap the card to expand it. A home listed for sale shows its list price.
- **Similar homes** shows the closest matches. On a phone, swipe or use the arrows to see the next card. Each card includes listing details and links to Zillow and Maps.
- **The map** shows the subject property, the similar homes (numbered) and other nearby listings. The dotted circle is the search area.
- If Sayso Map had to widen the search to find enough matches, a note says what it changed, for example "Widened: price ±20%". If no home matched closely enough, the note says "Showing the closest similar homes".

## Check an off-market estimate

For an off-market home, Sayso Map may show an <Ui>AVM</Ui>, an automated estimate of the home's value. Tap the information icon next to the confidence label for details. Use it as a starting point for a conversation and confirm the details in your MLS.

See [Understand off-market value estimates in Sayso Map](/help/tools/sayso-map-off-market-estimates/) for what can change the confidence label.

## Check a similar home's condition

The first few similar homes in the list show a condition badge. Tap it for more detail. Sayso Map uses available listing photos to estimate the home's condition. <Ui>Condition n/a</Ui> means a rating is unavailable.

See [What Sayso Map condition ratings mean](/help/tools/sayso-map-condition-ratings/) for all the labels and what can appear when you open a badge.

## Send the homes to your lead

Under the similar homes, <Ui>Text to send</Ui> has a message with the homes already filled in. Edit it, tap <Ui>Copy</Ui>, then paste it into a text to your lead.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-3.webp" alt="The Text to send box with a message listing the similar homes and the Copy button" />

Sayso Map uses the buyer template when the home you searched is listed for sale and the seller template when it is off market. To edit either template, see [Change Sayso Map filters](/help/tools/sayso-map-filters/).

## Download or email the results

To save a PDF, tap <Ui>Download PDF</Ui>, the download icon next to <Ui>Email me</Ui>. If an older Comp Report asks you to search again, repeat the search and try the download again.

To email the report, tap <Ui>Email me</Ui>, enter your email address and tap <Ui>Next</Ui>. Check the address and tap <Ui>Yes</Ui> to send. If Sayso Map already has your email address, it opens the confirmation first. Tap the pencil icon to edit the address.

The email also has a <Ui>Download PDF</Ui> button.

## Other properties

<Ui>Other Properties</Ui> lists the rest of the nearby listings that didn't make the similar homes. Sort it by <Ui>Distance</Ui>, <Ui>Price</Ui> or <Ui>Sq ft</Ui>.

## Recent Comp Reports

Your past Comp Reports are listed under <Ui>Recent Comp Reports</Ui> on the home screen. Tap <Ui>Comp Reports</Ui> at the top to get there. Tap one to open it again. Reopening a Comp Report doesn't use another one. Comp Reports are saved to your account, so they show on every device where you log in. To remove one, open it and tap <Ui>Delete Comp Report</Ui> at the bottom.

Each row shows the address, beds, baths, square feet and when you searched. The <Ui>AVM</Ui> column shows <Ui>On market</Ui> for a home that was listed for sale when you searched. For a home that was off market, it shows the AVM with a dot for its confidence: green for high, yellow for medium, red for low. Tap the information icon next to <Ui>AVM</Ui> for a short explanation.
