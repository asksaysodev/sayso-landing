## Search an address

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) or tap the Sayso Map icon on your home screen.
2. Type the address in <Ui>Enter an address</Ui>. Suggestions appear as you type. Tap the right one.
3. Tap <Ui>Search</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-1.webp" alt="The Find Comps screen with the Enter an address field and the Search button" width={300} />

## Read the results

- **Subject Property** is the home you searched, with its price, beds, baths and square feet. Tap it to see more.
- **Similar homes** shows the closest matches, one card at a time. Swipe or use the arrows to see the next one. Each card shows how the home compares to the subject property, plus links to open it in Zillow or Maps.
- **The map** shows the subject property, the similar homes (numbered) and other nearby listings. The dotted circle is the search area.
- If Sayso Map had to widen the search to find enough matches, a note says what it changed, for example "Widened to find comparables: price ±20%".

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-2.webp" alt="Sayso Map results with the map, Subject Property and a similar home card" width={300} />

## Send the homes to your lead

Under the similar homes, <Ui>Text to send</Ui> has a message with the homes already filled in. Edit it if you want, then tap <Ui>Copy</Ui> and paste it into your text to the lead.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-3.webp" alt="The Text to send box with a message listing the similar homes and the Copy button" width={300} />

To change the default message, see [Change Sayso Map filters](/help/tools/sayso-map-filters/).

## Email the results to yourself

Tap <Ui>Email me</Ui>, enter your email and tap <Ui>Next</Ui>. Sayso Map sends the results to that address.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-4.webp" alt="The Email these comps to field with the Next button" width={300} />

## Other properties

<Ui>Other Properties</Ui> lists the rest of the nearby listings that didn't make the similar homes. Sort it by <Ui>Distance</Ui>, <Ui>Price</Ui> or <Ui>Sq ft</Ui>.

## Recent searches

Your past searches are saved under <Ui>Recent searches</Ui> on the home screen, or tap <Ui>Searches</Ui> at the top. Tap one to open it again without using another search. Searches are saved in the browser on that device, so your phone and your computer each keep their own list. To remove one, open it and tap <Ui>Delete search</Ui> at the bottom.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-5.webp" alt="Recent searches with one saved search" width={300} />

<Callout type="note">
Listings can change quickly. Confirm property details with your local MLS before you share them.
</Callout>
