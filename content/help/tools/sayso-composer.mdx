Sayso Composer is a web app at [composer.asksayso.com](https://composer.asksayso.com). Answer a few questions about the lead you're about to call and it writes a one-page "master script" you can follow on the call, with coaching notes. It works for buyers, sellers and any lead source.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-composer-1.webp" alt="The Sayso Composer home page with the Build My Script button" />

## Build a script

1. Go to [composer.asksayso.com](https://composer.asksayso.com) and click <Ui>Build My Script</Ui>.
2. Answer the six steps:
   - <Ui>Who are you calling?</Ui> Sellers, Buyers or Not sure yet.
   - <Ui>Where did this lead come from?</Ui> For example Sphere, Past client, Online inbound lead, Open house, Sign call, FSBO, Expired listing or Circle prospecting.
   - <Ui>How warm is this lead?</Ui> Cold, Warm or Hot.
   - <Ui>What's the goal of this call?</Ui> For example Book an appointment, Set a listing appointment or Re-engage a dead lead.
   - <Ui>Tell us about the call:</Ui> what you offer, why you're calling and what you know about the lead.
   - <Ui>Your style:</Ui> Casual & laid-back, Balanced or Direct & assertive. Add your name, team and market, or leave them blank to get placeholders.
3. Sign in or create your Composer account when asked.
4. Your script appears in seconds. It's saved to your Composer dashboard so you can open it again later.

## Use it with Sayso

Upload the scripts you build to the desktop app so they sit next to the Coach widget during calls. See [Upload and manage your playbooks](/help/desktop-app/upload-playbooks/).
