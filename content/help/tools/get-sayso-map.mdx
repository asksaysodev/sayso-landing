## Request access

1. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) from the email on your Sayso account.
2. Ask for access to Sayso Map.
3. We reply with your passcode and next steps.

There's no extra cost for current Sayso customers.

## Sign in the first time

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) on your phone or computer.
2. On <Ui>Team access</Ui>, enter your first and last name and the passcode.
3. Tap <Ui>Continue</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/get-sayso-map-1.webp" alt="The Sayso Map Team access screen with name and passcode fields and the Continue button" />

You do this once on each device. Using your phone and your computer? Enter it on both.

<Callout type="note">
If you see "That passcode didn't work", check for extra spaces, then email support.
</Callout>

## Daily limit

While Sayso Map is in test groups, each person can run up to 8 searches per day. The count resets at midnight Eastern time.
