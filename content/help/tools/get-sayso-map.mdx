You need a Sayso Map account to search an address or open <Ui>Filters</Ui>. A new account comes with 3 free Comp Reports. After that, choose a plan or join with a team passcode.

## Create your account

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) and search an address, or tap <Ui>Log in</Ui> at the top and then <Ui>Sign up</Ui>.
2. In <Ui>Create your account</Ui>, enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Tap <Ui>Create account</Ui>.
4. Open the email from Sayso Map and click the confirmation link. To get a new link, tap <Ui>Resend email</Ui>.

If you searched an address before you signed up, Sayso Map finishes that search after you confirm your email.

## Join with a team passcode

Sayso Map is included for agents on a Sayso team. Get the passcode from your admin or owner.

1. In <Ui>Create your account</Ui> or <Ui>Log in</Ui>, tap <Ui>Enter passcode</Ui> next to <Ui>On a Sayso team?</Ui>.
2. Enter the passcode, then your email and password.
3. Tap <Ui>Create account with team access</Ui> or <Ui>Log in with team access</Ui>.

Already have an account? Tap your initial at the top, then <Ui>Settings</Ui>, <Ui>Subscription</Ui> and <Ui>Have a team passcode?</Ui>. Enter the passcode and tap <Ui>Apply</Ui>.

## Log in

1. Tap <Ui>Log in</Ui> at the top of Sayso Map.
2. Enter your email and password and tap <Ui>Log in</Ui>.

If you use Sayso Composer, log in with your Composer email and password. Log in once on each phone or computer you use. Your Comp Reports and filters are saved to your account, so they show on every device.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-log-in.webp" alt="The Log in box with Composer user? Use your Composer login, the Forgot password? link and the Log in button" />

## Reset your password

1. In <Ui>Log in</Ui>, tap <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui>.
2. Enter your email and tap <Ui>Send link</Ui>.
3. Open the link in the email, enter a new password and tap <Ui>Save password</Ui>.

If you see <Ui>Reset link expired</Ui>, tap <Ui>Request new link</Ui>.

## Sign out

Tap your initial at the top, then <Ui>Sign out</Ui>.
