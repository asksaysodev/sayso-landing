## Current Sayso customers

Composer is included with your Sayso subscription at no extra cost.

1. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) from the email on your Sayso account.
2. Ask for access to Composer.
3. We reply with the next steps.

## Agents without a Sayso subscription

Composer is also available on its own as a paid tool for agents who don't use Sayso. See the current options at [composer.asksayso.com](https://composer.asksayso.com).

## Want live coaching too?

Composer helps you prepare before a call, and Sayso coaches you during it. [Book a demo](/demo/) to see Sayso on a call.
