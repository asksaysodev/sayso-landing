## Current Sayso customers

Composer is included with your Sayso subscription at no extra cost.

1. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) from the email on your Sayso account.
2. Ask for access to Composer.
3. We reply with the next steps.

## Agents without a Sayso subscription

Anyone can use Composer on its own. A new Composer account includes 3 scripts at no cost. After that, there are two paid plans:

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $4.99 a month | Fresh scripts every month, every lead type and tone. Cancel anytime. |
| Lifetime | $50 once | Unlimited scripts with one payment and no renewals. |

See the current plans at [composer.asksayso.com/pricing](https://composer.asksayso.com/pricing). Payments are handled by Stripe, and a monthly plan can be canceled anytime from your Composer dashboard.
