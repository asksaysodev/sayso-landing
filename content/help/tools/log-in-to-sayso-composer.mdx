Click <Ui>Log in</Ui> or <Ui>Create account</Ui> at the top of any Composer page. A pop-up opens on the page you're on. When you build a script without being logged in, the same pop-up opens before your script appears.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-composer-log-in.webp" alt="The Sayso Composer Log in pop-up with Email and Password fields, Forgot password and the Log in button" width={390} />

## Log in

1. Enter your email and password.
2. Click <Ui>Log in</Ui>.

If you use Sayso Map, log in with your Map login.

## Create an account

1. If the pop-up shows <Ui>Log in</Ui>, click <Ui>Sign up</Ui>.
2. On <Ui>Create your account</Ui>, enter your email and a password.
3. Click <Ui>Create account</Ui>.
4. The pop-up shows <Ui>Confirm your email</Ui>. Click the link in the confirmation email.

If the email doesn't arrive, click <Ui>Resend email</Ui>. If your email already has an account, the pop-up switches to <Ui>Log in</Ui> and says so.

## Reset your password

1. On <Ui>Log in</Ui>, click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui>.
2. Enter your email and click <Ui>Send link</Ui>.
3. Open the link in the email.
4. On <Ui>New password</Ui>, enter a new password and click <Ui>Save password</Ui>.

If you see <Ui>Reset link expired</Ui>, click <Ui>Request new link</Ui> and start again.
