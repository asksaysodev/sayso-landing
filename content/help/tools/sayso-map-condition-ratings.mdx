In <Ui>Similar homes</Ui>, tap a condition badge to see what Sayso Map noticed. Sayso Map rates a home from its listing photos, looking at kitchens, bathrooms, flooring and wear. New construction shows <Ui>New</Ui> from the listing details alone. A spinner shows while Sayso Map checks a home.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-condition.webp" alt="A similar home with its Updated badge open, showing Updated kitchen and Newer flooring" />

## What each badge means

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>New</Ui> | The home appears to be new construction. |
| <Ui>Fully updated</Ui> | The home appears recently renovated or in like-new condition. |
| <Ui>Updated</Ui> | The home appears well maintained, with some updated features. |
| <Ui>Average</Ui> | The home appears adequately maintained, with older finishes or normal wear. |
| <Ui>Dated</Ui> | The photos suggest deferred maintenance or components that may need repair. |
| <Ui>Needs work</Ui> | The photos suggest substantial damage or major work. |
| <Ui>Condition n/a</Ui> | A rating is unavailable. Sayso Map couldn't find the listing or enough photos. |

## What appears when you open a badge

- **Visible details** may include observations such as updated flooring or original bathrooms.
- **Per listing** identifies details reported in the listing description, such as a roof replacement. Verify those details before sharing them.
- **An exclamation mark** means the photos and the listing description disagree. Open the badge to see what Sayso Map flagged.

Confirm important details in the listing or with the listing agent. A badge can't replace an inspection.
