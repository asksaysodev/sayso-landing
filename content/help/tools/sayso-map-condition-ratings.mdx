In <Ui>Similar homes</Ui>, select a condition badge to see what Sayso Map noticed. Map usually rates a home from available listing photos, looking at visible details such as kitchens, bathrooms, flooring and wear. A home may show <Ui>New</Ui> based on listing details alone. You may see a spinner while Map checks a home.

## What each badge means

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>New</Ui> | The home appears to be new construction. |
| <Ui>Fully updated</Ui> | The home appears recently renovated or in like-new condition. |
| <Ui>Updated</Ui> | The home appears well maintained, with some updated features. |
| <Ui>Average</Ui> | The home appears adequately maintained, with older finishes or normal wear. |
| <Ui>Dated</Ui> | The photos suggest deferred maintenance or components that may need repair. |
| <Ui>Needs work</Ui> | The photos suggest substantial damage or major work. |
| <Ui>Condition n/a</Ui> | A rating is unavailable. This can happen when Map cannot find a matching listing or enough listing photos. |

## What appears when you open a badge

- **Visible details** may include observations such as updated flooring or original bathrooms.
- **Per listing** identifies details reported in the listing description, such as a roof replacement. Verify those details before sharing them.
- **An exclamation mark** means the photos and the listing description appear to disagree. Open the badge to see what Map flagged.

Use the badge to decide what to look at more closely when comparing homes. Check important details in the listing or with the listing agent. A badge reflects the available information and cannot show everything you would learn from an inspection.
