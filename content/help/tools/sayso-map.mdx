Sayso Map is a web app at [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com). Type an address to see similar homes for sale nearby and how each one compares to the home you searched. It covers the whole United States, works on your phone and your computer, and you can add it to your phone's home screen.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-2.webp" alt="A Sayso Map Comp Report with the home you searched, similar homes and the map" caption="A Comp Report for one address: the home you searched, similar homes and the map." />

## What's in a Comp Report

- **Similar homes:** active listings near the address, matched on criteria such as bedrooms, bathrooms, square feet and price. Cards show listing details. The first few cards also show a [condition badge](/help/tools/sayso-map-condition-ratings/).
- **A map** with the home you searched, the similar homes and the other nearby listings.
- **A text message** with the similar homes filled in, ready to copy and send.
- **Download PDF** saves a report you can share. **Email me** sends it to your inbox.
- **[A value estimate](/help/tools/sayso-map-off-market-estimates/)** may appear for a home that is off market.
- **Other properties:** the rest of the nearby listings, sorted by distance, price or square feet.

## Who can use it

Anyone can create a Sayso Map account with an email and password. A new account comes with 3 free Comp Reports. After that, choose a monthly plan. Agents on a Sayso team get Sayso Map with a team passcode from their admin or owner.

See [Create a Sayso Map account and log in](/help/tools/get-sayso-map/) and [Sayso Map plans and billing](/help/tools/sayso-map-plans/).
