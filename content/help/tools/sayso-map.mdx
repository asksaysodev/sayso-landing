Sayso Map is a web app at [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com). Type an address to see similar homes for sale nearby and how each one compares to the home you searched. It covers the whole United States, works on your phone and your computer, and you can add it to your phone's home screen.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/use-sayso-map-2.webp" alt="Sayso Map results for an address, with a map, the subject property and similar homes" caption="Results for one address: the map, the home you searched and similar homes." />

## What you get for each search

- **Similar homes:** active listings near the address that match on bedrooms, bathrooms, square feet and price. Each one shows its price, how it compares to the home you searched, distance, days listed, price per square foot, year built, lot size, listing brokerage and MLS number.
- **A map** with the home you searched, the similar homes and the other nearby listings.
- **A text message** with the similar homes filled in, ready to copy and send.
- **Email me** to send the results to your inbox.
- **Other properties:** the rest of the nearby listings, sorted by distance, price or square feet.

## Who can use it

Sayso Map is included for current Sayso customers at no extra cost. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) to get access. See [Get access to Sayso Map](/help/tools/get-sayso-map/).
