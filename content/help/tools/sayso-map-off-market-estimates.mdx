When the home you searched is off market, Sayso Map may show an automated value estimate labeled <Ui>AVM</Ui> near the address. Tap the information icon next to the confidence label for details. Use the estimate as a starting point and review the comparable homes before discussing value or list price.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-estimate.webp" alt="The AVM amount, High confidence label and open information note for an off-market home" />

## What the confidence label means

- **High:** the estimate has a narrow range of likely values.
- **Medium:** the range is wider.
- **Low:** the range is wide, comparable data is limited, or nearby homes may not reflect this home's value.

Confidence can drop after the [condition badges](/help/tools/sayso-map-condition-ratings/) load. For example, if the home last sold many years ago and most of the rated similar homes are new or fully updated, Sayso Map may show <Ui>Nearby homes look more updated. Estimate may be high.</Ui> The confidence label drops. The dollar amount stays the same.

## How Sayso Map uses the estimate

Sayso Map matches nearby homes by price using the estimate. With no estimate, it still shows similar homes but skips the price match. For a home listed for sale, it matches on the list price.
