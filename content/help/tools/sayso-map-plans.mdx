Each search uses one Comp Report. Reopening a saved Comp Report doesn't use another one.

| Plan | Comp Reports |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 3 in total |
| <Ui>Map 10</Ui> | 10 per month |
| <Ui>Map 30</Ui> | 30 per month |
| <Ui>Map Unlimited</Ui> | Unlimited |
| <Ui>Team access</Ui> | Included with your team's Sayso subscription |

Plans are billed monthly. <Ui>Choose your plan</Ui> shows the price of each one.

## Choose a plan

1. On a free account, tap <Ui>See plans</Ui> under the search box or in the bar at the top of a Comp Report. You can also tap your initial at the top, then <Ui>Settings</Ui>, <Ui>Subscription</Ui> and <Ui>Upgrade</Ui>.
2. In <Ui>Choose your plan</Ui>, tap <Ui>Get Map 10</Ui>, <Ui>Get Map 30</Ui> or <Ui>Get Map Unlimited</Ui>.
3. Enter your card on the checkout page.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-choose-plan.webp" alt="Choose your plan with Map 10, Map 30 marked Most popular, Map Unlimited and Have a team passcode?" />

On a Sayso team? Tap <Ui>Have a team passcode?</Ui> instead. See [Join with a team passcode](/help/tools/get-sayso-map/#join-with-a-team-passcode).

## See your plan and usage

Tap your initial at the top, then <Ui>Settings</Ui> and <Ui>Subscription</Ui>. The plan card shows your plan, its status, the renewal date and how many Comp Reports you've used.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-subscription.webp" alt="Account settings on the Subscription tab with the Free plan, 2 of 3 Comp Reports used, the Upgrade button and Have a team passcode?" />

## Upgrade to a bigger plan

1. In <Ui>Subscription</Ui>, tap <Ui>Upgrade</Ui>.
2. In <Ui>Get more Comp Reports</Ui>, pick a plan.
3. Tap <Ui>Upgrade now</Ui>.

The new plan starts as soon as you confirm.

## Invoices and your card

- <Ui>Invoices</Ui> lists each bill with its date, total and status. Tap a row to open the invoice.
- <Ui>Manage billing</Ui> opens the billing page, where you update your card.
- If a payment fails, <Ui>Update your card</Ui> shows at the top of Sayso Map and in <Ui>Subscription</Ui>. Tap it to add a new card.
