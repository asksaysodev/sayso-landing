Tap <Ui>Filters</Ui> at the top of Sayso Map. Changes apply to your next search.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-filters-1.webp" alt="The Radius control under Search area in Sayso Map Filters" />

## Search area

<Ui>Radius</Ui> sets how far from the address to look, from a quarter mile up to 10 miles. By default Sayso Map starts at 0.5 miles and widens up to 1 mile until enough homes match.

## How close a match

- <Ui>Bedrooms</Ui> and <Ui>Bathrooms</Ui>: <Ui>Exact</Ui>, <Ui>±1</Ui> or <Ui>±2</Ui>.
- <Ui>Sq Ft</Ui> and <Ui>Price</Ui>: within ±5%, ±10%, ±15% or ±20% of the home you searched. For an off-market home, the price filter uses the value estimate when one is available.

## Results

<Ui>Homes</Ui> sets how many similar homes Sayso Map looks for, from 1 to 5. By default it widens the search until it finds 2 and shows up to 3.

## Text template

In <Ui>Filters</Ui>, scroll to <Ui>Text template</Ui>. Edit <Ui>Buyer text</Ui> for a home listed for sale or <Ui>Seller text</Ui> for an off-market home. Sayso Map uses the matching template under <Ui>Text to send</Ui> when you open the results.

Tap where you want to insert a field, then tap <Ui>Subject address</Ui> or <Ui>Similar homes list</Ui>. Tap <Ui>Reset</Ui> in that template to restore its original message.

<Screenshot src="/help/tools/sayso-map-text-templates.webp" alt="The separate Buyer text and Seller text editors in Filters" />

## Start over

<Ui>Reset to defaults</Ui> puts every filter back to its original setting.
