Tap your initial at the top of Sayso Map, then <Ui>Settings</Ui> and <Ui>Subscription</Ui>. Under <Ui>Subscription management</Ui>, tap <Ui>I want to cancel</Ui>.

If a pause is available, choose <Ui>Pause subscription</Ui> or <Ui>Cancel subscription</Ui> and tap <Ui>Confirm selection</Ui>. Otherwise the cancel steps open.

## Pause

1. Pick a length in <Ui>Pause duration</Ui>. Sayso Map shows when access ends, when access and billing resume, and the price when you come back.
2. Tap <Ui>Confirm pause</Ui>.

You keep full access until the pause begins. Your plan resumes on its own at the end of the pause.

## Cancel

1. In <Ui>Why are you leaving?</Ui>, pick a reason. Add a comment in <Ui>Anything else?</Ui> if there's more to say. Tap <Ui>Continue</Ui>. To skip it, tap <Ui>Skip this step</Ui>.
2. You may see <Ui>Would a lower price help?</Ui>. Tap <Ui>Apply discount & stay</Ui> to keep your plan at the lower price, or <Ui>Continue to cancel</Ui>.
3. In <Ui>Cancel your renewal</Ui>, tap <Ui>Confirm</Ui>.

You keep access until the date shown. Your plan won't renew after that. To stop at any step, tap <Ui>Changed my mind</Ui> or <Ui>Keep subscription</Ui>.

## Reactivate before access ends

1. In <Ui>Subscription</Ui>, tap <Ui>Reactivate subscription</Ui>.
2. Check the next bill date and tap <Ui>Reactivate subscription</Ui>.

There's no charge today. Your plan and price stay the same. Your plan renews on its original date, or when your pause ends if you set one.
