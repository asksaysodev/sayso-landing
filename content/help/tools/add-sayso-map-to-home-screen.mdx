Sayso Map doesn't need an app store download. Add it to your home screen once and it opens full screen from the Sayso icon, like any other app.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="iPhone">

### In Chrome

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **Share** button in the address bar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

### In Safari

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen (on some versions it's in the **⋯** menu first).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Android">

### In Chrome

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to home screen**.
4. Tap **Install** (or **Add**, depending on your version).

### In Samsung Internet

1. Open [map.asksayso.com](https://map.asksayso.com) in Samsung Internet.
2. Tap the **≡** menu at the bottom right.
3. Tap **Add page to**, then **Home screen**, then **Add**.

## After you add it

- The icon is the Sayso logo and the name is **Sayso Map**. Press and hold the icon to move it like any other app.
- On iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own sign-in, so enter your name and passcode once more the first time you open it. See [Get access to Sayso Map](/help/tools/get-sayso-map/).
