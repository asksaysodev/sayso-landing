<Callout type="warning" title="This can't be undone">
<Ui>Remove member</Ui> permanently deletes the person's Sayso account and their history, including their conversations. It is not a deactivation. They can no longer sign in to the web app or the desktop app. To add them again later you send a new invite, and they start with a new, empty account.
</Callout>

## Before you remove someone

- If you only want them off a team, use <Ui>Remove from team</Ui> on the team's page instead. See [Move an agent to another team or off a team](/help/admins/change-an-agents-team/).
- Review or copy anything you want to keep from their conversations first, such as summaries and Smart Capture notes. Notes they already sent to Follow Up Boss or Sure Send stay in your CRM.

## Remove a member

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui>.
2. Open the person's row menu and choose <Ui>Remove member</Ui>.
3. Read the message: "This action cannot be undone. Their account and history are permanently deleted, not deactivated."
4. Click <Ui>Yes, remove</Ui>. You will see "Member removed."

## Who can remove whom

- The Owner and Admins can remove Members and Admins.
- Nobody can remove the Owner, and you can't remove yourself.
