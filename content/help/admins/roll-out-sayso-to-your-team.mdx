## Before you invite anyone

- Decide who will be an Admin and help you run it day to day.
- Decide whether you want teams (for example by office or by role) and whether each team gets an hour cap. See [How companies and teams work](/help/admins/teams-overview/).
- Take a snapshot of your CRM leaderboard (calls, conversations, appointments) for the 30 to 60 days before Sayso, so you can measure the change.

## Invite and set up

1. [Create your teams](/help/admins/create-a-team/) if you're using them.
2. [Invite your agents](/help/admins/invite-agents/) and put each one on a team in the same step.
3. Send each agent [Join your team from an invite](/help/agents/join-your-team/), the [cheat sheet](/help/agents/cheat-sheet/) and [Download and install Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).
4. Watch <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui> until everyone shows <Ui>Active</Ui>. Resend anything that shows <Ui>Expired</Ui>.

## First week

- Book a 30-minute one-on-one with each agent to install Sayso, set up headphones and their dialer, and make one practice call. Use [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/) and [Set up your dialer](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/).
- Have each agent connect their own CRM. Connections are per agent. See [CRM connections on a team](/help/admins/crm-connections-for-teams/).
- If you use team scripts, send agents the files to upload. See [Sharing scripts with your team](/help/admins/share-scripts-with-your-team/).
- Open a shared chat channel for questions.

## After that

- Check in every two weeks.
- Read your weekly [email report](/help/admins/email-reports/) and use [Dashboard filters](/help/admins/team-dashboard/) to see who is using Sayso and who needs help.
- Point agents with questions to [weekly office hours](/help/support/office-hours/).
