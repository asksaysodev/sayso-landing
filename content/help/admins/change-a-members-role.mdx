Only the Owner can change roles. Admins do not see the role options.

## Change the role of someone who has joined

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com) and open <Ui>Company</Ui> in the sidebar.
2. On the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab, find the person. You can type their name or email in <Ui>Search...</Ui>.
3. Open the menu at the end of their row and choose <Ui>Update role</Ui>.
4. Pick <Ui>Member</Ui> or <Ui>Admin</Ui> in the <Ui>Role</Ui> field.
5. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Role updated."

You can do the same from a team page: open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Teams</Ui>, open the team, and choose <Ui>Update member role</Ui> in the person's row menu.

## Change the role on an invite that hasn't been accepted

If you invited someone with the wrong role, you can fix it before they join.

1. On the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab, find the invite. Its status shows <Ui>Invited</Ui> or <Ui>Expired</Ui>.
2. Open the row menu and choose <Ui>Update role</Ui>.
3. Pick the role they should get when they accept, then click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Invite role updated."

## What you can't change

- Your own role. Your row has no menu.
- The Owner's role.
- Roles are Member or Admin only. The Owner role can't be given to someone else in the app. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) if ownership needs to move.
