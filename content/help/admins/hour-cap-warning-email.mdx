## When it is sent

- Only for teams with an hour cap.
- When the team crosses 80% of its cap, right after a coaching session ends.
- Once per team per calendar month. If the team drops back under 80% (for example, you transfer an agent off it or raise the cap), it can be sent again that month.

<Screenshot src="/help/admins/hour-cap-warning-email-1.webp" alt="A Team usage alert email showing hours used, the allocation and the percent used" width={520} />

## Who gets it

The Owner and every Admin in the company. Members don't.

## What's in it

The subject reads "[Team name] has used 80% of its Sayso hours" (the percentage is the team's usage when the email is sent). Inside: hours used this month, the team's allocation, the percentage used, and a <Ui>Manage team</Ui> button that opens the team's page.

## What to do

- Raise the cap: see [Set a team's monthly hours](/help/admins/set-team-hours/).
- Or leave it. When the team hits 100%, its agents can't start new sessions until the 1st of next month.

There is no setting to turn this email off. Removing the team's cap stops it.
