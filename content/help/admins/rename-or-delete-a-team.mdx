## Rename a team

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Teams</Ui> and click the team.
2. Click the pencil next to the team name (<Ui>Edit team name</Ui>).
3. Type the new <Ui>Team name</Ui> (up to 80 characters) and click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Team updated."

The name must be different from your other teams.

## Delete a team

1. Open the team's page and click <Ui>Delete team</Ui>.
2. Read the message: "Its members stay in your company without a team. This action cannot be undone."
3. Click <Ui>Yes, delete</Ui>. You will see "Team deleted."

Everyone on the team keeps their account, role and conversations. They just have no team and no team cap until you add them to another one.
