These options work on invites with the status <Ui>Invited</Ui> or <Ui>Expired</Ui>. Once someone joins, use [Move an agent to another team](/help/admins/change-an-agents-team/) or [Change someone's role](/help/admins/change-a-members-role/) instead.

## Change the team

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui> and find the invite.
2. Open the row menu and choose <Ui>Change team</Ui>. The <Ui>Change invite team</Ui> window opens.
3. Pick a <Ui>Team</Ui>, or the dash for no team.
4. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Invite team updated."

The person joins that team when they accept.

## Change the role (Owner only)

1. In the invite's row menu, choose <Ui>Update role</Ui>.
2. Pick <Ui>Member</Ui> or <Ui>Admin</Ui> and click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Invite role updated."
