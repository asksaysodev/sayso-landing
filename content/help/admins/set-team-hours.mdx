A team cap limits how many coaching hours the agents on one team can use each calendar month. It is optional. Teams without a cap use your company's plan hours freely.

## Set or change the cap

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Teams</Ui> and click the team.
2. In <Ui>Allocated hours</Ui>, click <Ui>Set hours</Ui> (no cap yet) or <Ui>Edit hours</Ui>.
3. Enter the monthly hours in <Ui>Allocated hours</Ui>, from 1 to 10,000. You can also use <Ui>Quick add</Ui> to add 10, 20 or 30 hours.
4. <Ui>Used so far</Ui> previews what the team's status will be with the new number.
5. Click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>. You will see "Team updated."

<Screenshot src="/help/admins/set-team-hours-1.webp" alt="The Allocated hours window with Used so far, the hours field and Quick add buttons" caption="Edit hours" />

To remove the cap, clear the <Ui>Allocated hours</Ui> field and click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>.

## How usage is counted

- The month runs from the 1st of the calendar month and resets on the 1st of the next.
- Usage is the coaching time of everyone currently on the team this month. When you transfer an agent, their usage this month moves with them.
- Hours show rounded on the page. The percentage uses exact minutes.

## Team status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>On track</Ui> | Under 80% of the cap. |
| <Ui>Approaching</Ui> | 80% or more of the cap. The Owner and Admins get a warning email. |
| <Ui>Over</Ui> | The full cap is used. Agents on the team can't start new sessions. |
| <Ui>No cap set</Ui> | No limit for this team. |

## What happens at the cap

When a team has used all its hours, agents on that team see "Your team has reached its coaching hour limit. Let your team leader know." when they try to start a session. A session that's already going keeps going. To let them continue this month, raise the cap. Otherwise they can start again on the 1st.

The team cap never gives a team more hours than your plan has. If the whole company runs out of plan hours, everyone is stopped, whatever the team caps say.
