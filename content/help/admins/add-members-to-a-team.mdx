1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Teams</Ui> and click the team.
2. In <Ui>Team members</Ui>, click <Ui>Add member</Ui>.
3. In <Ui>Members</Ui>, pick one or more people. Anyone already on a team is greyed out.
4. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Member added." or "Members added."

Only people who have joined your company can be added this way. For someone who hasn't joined yet, choose the team when you invite them, or change it later with <Ui>Change team</Ui> on their invite.

Adding an agent to a team is never blocked by the team's hour cap. The cap only stops new coaching sessions once the team has used its hours.

## The Team members list

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Member</Ui> | Their name. |
| <Ui>E-mail</Ui> | Their sign-in email. |
| <Ui>Role</Ui> | Owner, Admin or Member. |
| <Ui>Last conversation</Ui> | When they last used Sayso on a call, or "No conversations yet". |
