## Who manages billing

The Owner, the person who subscribed to the team plan. Admins and Members don't see <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Subscription</Ui>.

If an Admin or Member opens the web app when the company has no active plan, they see "Your company doesn't have an active plan. Contact your account owner to get access to Sayso."

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/subscription-tab-1.webp" alt="Account Settings, Subscription tab" />

## Where the Owner manages it

In <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Subscription</Ui> you can see your plan, its status, hours per month and minutes used this period, and your invoices. <Ui>Subscription management</Ui> opens a secure billing page to update your payment method and billing information. See [Update your card and download invoices](/help/web-app/update-payment-method/).

## Shared hours

Your plan includes a number of coaching hours each billing period, shared by everyone in the company. Hours reset with each paid invoice. A call only uses hours when Sayso helped on it: Cue gave a prompt, Smart Capture caught something, or the agent used Pulse. See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).

To control how the hours are split, give teams a monthly cap. See [Set a team's monthly hours](/help/admins/set-team-hours/).

## Cancelling or pausing

The owner cancels or pauses from <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Subscription</Ui> > <Ui>I want to cancel</Ui>. Your team keeps access until the end of the period you paid for. See [Cancel, pause or change your plan](/help/web-app/cancel-subscription/).

## Getting more hours or changing your plan

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) or [book a call](/demo/) to move to a bigger plan if your team runs out of hours often, or to change your plan or billing period.

Team discounts are available for teams bringing on 3 or more agents. See [pricing](/pricing/) for the current plans.
