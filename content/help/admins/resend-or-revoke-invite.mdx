Owners and Admins can do both from <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui>.

## Resend an expired invite

<Ui>Resend invite</Ui> appears only on invites with the status <Ui>Expired</Ui>.

1. Find the invite on the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab.
2. Open the row menu and choose <Ui>Resend invite</Ui>.
3. You will see "Invite resent." The agent gets a new email, and the invite is valid for another 14 days.

<Callout type="note">
You can't resend an invite that is still <Ui>Invited</Ui> (not expired yet). If the agent lost the email, revoke it and send a new one with <Ui>Add member</Ui>. If you try to invite the same address while the first invite is still valid, it is skipped with "Invite already pending for this email".
</Callout>

<Screenshot src="/help/admins/company-page-1.webp" alt="The Company page Members tab" />

## Revoke an invite

1. Find the invite on the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab.
2. Open the row menu and choose <Ui>Revoke invite</Ui>.
3. Confirm with <Ui>Yes, revoke</Ui>. You will see "Invite revoked."

The link stops working right away and the invite disappears from the list. You can invite the same email again later.

## Fix a typo in the email

You can't edit the email on an invite. Revoke it, then send a new invite to the correct address.
