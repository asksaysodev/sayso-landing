Owners and Admins can invite agents. Agents join by email invite only; there is no way to add someone's account directly.

## Send invites

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com) and open <Ui>Company</Ui> in the sidebar.
2. On the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab, click <Ui>Add member</Ui>. The <Ui>Invite team member</Ui> window opens.
3. In <Ui>Email addresses</Ui>, type an email and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>, <Kbd>Space</Kbd>, <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> or a comma. Each address turns into a chip. Add as many as you need. You can also paste a list.
4. If you are the Owner, choose a <Ui>Role</Ui>: <Ui>Member</Ui> for agents or <Ui>Admin</Ui> for people who help run the team. Admins don't see this field, and everyone they invite joins as a Member.
5. Optional: choose a <Ui>Team</Ui>. The person joins that team when they accept. Leave it on the dash to invite them with no team.
6. Click <Ui>Send invite</Ui>. You will see "Invite sent." (or "3 invites sent." for several).

<Screenshot src="/help/admins/invite-agents-1.webp" alt="The Invite team member window with two email chips, the Role field and the Team field" caption="Invite team member" />

New invites show on the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab with the status <Ui>Invited</Ui>.

<Callout type="tip">
Ask agents to accept with an email they'll keep. The sign-in email can't be changed later from the app, so a personal email is safer than a brokerage email they might lose if they move.
</Callout>

## If some addresses weren't invited

The window stays open and lists each address that wasn't sent, with the reason. The others were sent.

| Reason | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invalid email format | The address has a typo. | Fix it and send again. |
| User already in your organization | This person already has an account in your company. | Nothing. Find them on the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab. |
| Invite already pending for this email | You already sent an invite that hasn't expired. | Wait for them to accept, or revoke it and send a new one. |
| Failed to create invite | Something went wrong on our side. | Try again. If it keeps failing, email support. |

The field also won't take an address that isn't a valid email, and it won't let you invite yourself.

## What the agent gets

The invite email has the subject "[Your company name] has invited you to Sayso". It shows who invited them and the team if you chose one, and has a <Ui>Join Team</Ui> button. The invite is valid for 14 days.

When they click it, they see "Join [your company name]", enter their first name, last name and a password, and land on the Dashboard. They don't pick a plan; they use your company's subscription. The Owner gets an email when someone joins.
