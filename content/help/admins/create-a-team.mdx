Owners and Admins can create teams.

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> in the sidebar and switch to the <Ui>Teams</Ui> tab.
2. Click <Ui>Add team</Ui>. If you don't have any teams yet, the button is under <Ui>Create your first team</Ui>.
3. Enter a <Ui>Team name</Ui> (required, up to 80 characters). Each team in your company needs a different name.
4. Optional: in <Ui>Add members</Ui>, pick the agents to put on the team. People who are already on another team are greyed out; move them first with [Transfer member](/help/admins/change-an-agents-team/).
5. Optional: enter <Ui>Set allocated hours</Ui>, the team's monthly cap, from 1 to 10,000 hours. Leave it empty for no cap.
6. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/admins/create-a-team-1.webp" alt="The Add team window with Team name, Add members and Set allocated hours" caption="Add team" />

You will see <Ui>Team created successfully</Ui> with the team name, its members and its allocated hours. Click <Ui>Continue</Ui> to go back to the list, or <Ui>Edit</Ui> to open the team's page.

## If it doesn't save

- "A team with that name already exists": pick a different name.
- "Some accounts already belong to a team": someone you picked joined another team in the meantime. Remove them from the list and try again.

You can also invite people straight onto a team: choose the team in the <Ui>Team</Ui> field when you [invite agents](/help/admins/invite-agents/).
