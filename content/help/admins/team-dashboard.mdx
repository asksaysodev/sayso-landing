As an Owner or Admin, the Dashboard shows your whole company by default ("Across this company").

## Filter to a team or an agent

1. Open <Ui>Dashboard</Ui> in the sidebar.
2. Click the <Ui>Filters</Ui> button next to the title.
3. Pick one team under <Ui>Team</Ui> or one person under <Ui>Members</Ui>. Use <Ui>Search team or members</Ui> to find them.
4. Click <Ui>Apply filters</Ui>.

The filter shows as a chip under the title. Remove the chip, or choose <Ui>Clear all</Ui> in the filters panel, to go back to the whole company.

## What you see

- <Ui>Coach Tips</Ui>: coaching patterns for the selection.
- <Ui>Activity</Ui>: minutes of conversation per day, one week at a time.
- <Ui>Overview</Ui>: <Ui>Conversation time</Ui> and <Ui>Appointments booked</Ui> for <Ui>Last 7 days</Ui>, <Ui>Last 14 days</Ui>, <Ui>Last 30 days</Ui>, <Ui>Last 3 months</Ui> or <Ui>Last 6 months</Ui>, compared with the period before.

To see an agent's individual calls, use [Conversations](/help/admins/review-team-conversations/).

## Use it for coaching

Pick one agent, look at their conversation time and appointments over the last 30 days, then open their recent conversations. Patterns to look for: skipping motivation questions, not asking for the appointment, or getting stuck on the same objection. That makes a ready agenda for a 1:1.

To measure the change Sayso makes, take a snapshot of your CRM numbers (calls, conversations, appointments) from the 30 to 60 days before you started, and compare.
