## Who gets the report

- The Owner and every Admin of a team account.
- Your company needs an active subscription. A plan that has been cancelled keeps getting reports until the end of the paid period.
- Members (agents) and individual accounts don't get it.

Each person sets their own preferences, so one Admin can get it weekly while another gets it monthly or turns it off.

## When it arrives

| Setting | Sent | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Ui>Weekly</Ui> (the default) | On your delivery day, Monday through Friday. Monday unless you change it. | The 7 days before that day. |
| <Ui>Monthly</Ui> | On the 1st of every month. | The previous calendar month. |

Reports go out around 7:00 AM Central Time (CT), whatever time zone you are in. The <Ui>Reports</Ui> settings page shows this as "Reports are sent around 7:00 AM CT".

No report is sent for a period with no conversation time, and none is sent for a period that ended before your company signed up.

## What's in it

- Subject: "Your Sayso weekly report · Oct 19 – Oct 25" or "Your Sayso monthly report · October 2026".
- Your company name and its total Sayso conversation time for the period.
- The change against the previous period of the same length, shown when the previous period had activity.
- An <Ui>Open dashboard</Ui> button and a <Ui>Manage report preferences</Ui> link.

The report covers the whole company. For a breakdown by team or agent, open the [Dashboard with filters](/help/admins/team-dashboard/). You can reply to the email to reach the Sayso team.

## Change or turn off your reports

1. In the web app, open <Ui>Settings</Ui> and choose <Ui>Reports</Ui>.
2. Turn <Ui>Email reports</Ui> on or off.
3. Under <Ui>Frequency</Ui>, pick <Ui>Weekly</Ui> or <Ui>Monthly</Ui>.
4. For weekly, pick a <Ui>Delivery day</Ui> from Monday to Friday. Monthly reports always say "Sent on the 1st of every month."

Changes save automatically. You will see "Report preferences saved", and the page shows <Ui>Next report</Ui> with the date.

<Screenshot src="/help/admins/email-reports-2.webp" alt="Settings Reports tab with Email reports on, Weekly selected and Monday as the delivery day" caption="Settings > Reports" />

If you don't see <Ui>Reports</Ui> in Settings, you are a Member or on an individual account.
