Each agent can be on one team at a time. Owners and Admins can move them from the team's page.

## Transfer an agent to another team

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Teams</Ui> and click the agent's current team.
2. In the <Ui>Team members</Ui> list, open the agent's row menu and choose <Ui>Transfer member</Ui>.
3. <Ui>From</Ui> shows their current team. Pick the new team in <Ui>To</Ui>.
4. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>. You will see "Member transferred to" followed by the new team name.

If you see "No other teams yet", create the second team first with <Ui>Add team</Ui>.

The agent's usage this month moves with them, so both teams' hours update. If the old team had a usage warning and drops back below 80% of its cap, it can warn again later in the month.

## Take an agent off a team

1. Open the team's page and choose <Ui>Remove from team</Ui> in the agent's row menu.
2. Confirm with <Ui>Yes, remove</Ui>. You will see "Member removed from team."

They stay in your company with no team, keep their account and conversations, and can be added to a team again any time.

<Callout type="note">
<Ui>Remove from team</Ui> only takes someone off a team. <Ui>Remove member</Ui> on the <Ui>Members</Ui> tab deletes their account. See [Remove an agent from your company](/help/admins/remove-an-agent/).
</Callout>

An agent with no team has no team hour cap. They use your company's plan hours like everyone else.
