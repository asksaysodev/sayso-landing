Admins can invite agents, build teams, set hour caps, read reports and review conversations. They can't manage billing, rename the company or change roles. See [Owner, Admin and Member roles](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/) for the full list.

## Make an existing agent an Admin

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui>.
2. In the agent's row menu, choose <Ui>Update role</Ui>.
3. Pick <Ui>Admin</Ui> and click <Ui>Save</Ui>.

## Invite someone new as an Admin

1. Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui> and click <Ui>Add member</Ui>.
2. Enter their email.
3. Set <Ui>Role</Ui> to <Ui>Admin</Ui>. Only the Owner sees the <Ui>Role</Ui> field. When an Admin sends an invite, the person always joins as a Member.
4. Click <Ui>Send invite</Ui>.

<Callout type="tip">
A new Admin starts getting the weekly email report and the team hour cap warnings. They can turn the report off or change how often it comes in <Ui>Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Reports</Ui>.
</Callout>

## Can there be more than one Owner?

No. The Owner is the person who subscribed to the team plan, and there is one per company. If the Owner is leaving or ownership needs to move, email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com).
