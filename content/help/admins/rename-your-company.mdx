Only the Owner can rename the company.

1. In the web app, open <Ui>Settings</Ui> and choose <Ui>Company</Ui>.
2. Change <Ui>Company name</Ui>. It can't be empty.
3. Click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>. You will see "Company name updated."

The new name shows on new invite emails, on the invite page agents see ("Join" and your company name), and in email reports.

If you don't see <Ui>Company</Ui> in Settings, you're an Admin or a Member. Ask your Owner to make the change.
