Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui> and look at the <Ui>Status</Ui> column.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Invited</Ui> | The invite was sent and hasn't been accepted. It is still valid. |
| <Ui>Expired</Ui> | The invite is more than 14 days old and wasn't accepted. The link no longer works. |
| <Ui>Active</Ui> | The person accepted and has an account in your company. |

Accepted invites become <Ui>Active</Ui> member rows. Revoked invites disappear from the list.

## How long an invite lasts

An invite is valid for 14 days from when it was sent or last resent. Opening the link doesn't use it up; it stops working only when it expires, is accepted or is revoked.

## What the agent sees if the link doesn't work

| Message on the agent's screen | Why | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This invite has expired | More than 14 days passed. | Use <Ui>Resend invite</Ui> on their row. |
| This invite has been revoked | Someone revoked it. | Send a new invite with <Ui>Add member</Ui>. |
| This invite has already been accepted | They already joined. | Tell them to sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com). |
| This invite link isn't valid | The link was cut off or copied wrong. | Ask them to open the full link from the email, or resend. |
| Too many attempts | The link was tried many times in a short window. | Wait a minute and try again. |
| An account with this email already exists | They made their own Sayso account with this email before you invited them. | See [Inviting an agent who already has a Sayso account](/help/admins/invite-someone-with-a-solo-account/). |
