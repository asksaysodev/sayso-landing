## Setting up your team

<Faq q="How do I add agents?">
Open <Ui>Company</Ui> > <Ui>Members</Ui> and click <Ui>Add member</Ui>. See [Invite agents to your company](/help/admins/invite-agents/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="How do I onboard a new agent?">
Send them [Join your team from an invite](/help/agents/join-your-team/), the [cheat sheet](/help/agents/cheat-sheet/) and [Download and install Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/). What works best: a 30-minute one-on-one to install, set up headphones and the dialer and make one practice call, a shared chat channel for questions, and a check-in every two weeks. See [Roll out Sayso to your team](/help/admins/roll-out-sayso-to-your-team/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="What's the difference between an Owner, an Admin and a Member?">
The Owner manages billing, renames the company and changes roles. Admins invite agents, build teams, set hour caps, read reports and review conversations. Members use Sayso on their calls and see their own data. See [Owner, Admin and Member roles](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I have more than one admin?">
Yes, as many as you need. The Owner can make anyone an Admin. See [Add another admin](/help/admins/add-another-admin/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I limit which features agents use?">
No. Every agent gets every feature.
</Faq>

<Faq q="How long does an invite last?">
14 days. After that it shows <Ui>Expired</Ui> and you can use <Ui>Resend invite</Ui>.
</Faq>

## Hours and billing

<Faq q="How does team pricing work?">
Your team plan includes a pool of coaching hours each billing period, shared by everyone in your company. You can split it between groups with team hour caps. See [Billing for team accounts](/help/admins/team-billing/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="What counts toward hours?">
A call counts when Cue gave a prompt, Smart Capture caught something, or the agent used Pulse. Voicemails can trigger prompts, so they can count. Auto Stop ends a session when the line goes quiet for a while. See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I see how many hours each agent is using?">
Yes. On the Dashboard, filter to one member to see their conversation time. The <Ui>Teams</Ui> tab shows each team's hours this month.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I set hours for each agent?">
Hour caps are set per team, not per agent. If you need a cap for one agent, put them on their own team. See [Set a team's monthly hours](/help/admins/set-team-hours/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="What happens when we run out of hours?">
If a team reaches its cap, its agents can't start new sessions until you raise the cap or the month resets on the 1st. If the whole company runs out of plan hours, nobody can start sessions until the next billing period. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) or [book a call](/demo/) to talk about a bigger plan.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can an Admin see invoices or change the card?">
No. Only the Owner can. See [Billing for team accounts](/help/admins/team-billing/).
</Faq>

## Scripts

<Faq q="Can I share scripts with the whole team?">
Not directly yet. Uploaded scripts are personal. Send agents the file and have them upload it. See [Sharing scripts with your team](/help/admins/share-scripts-with-your-team/).
</Faq>

## Reports and coaching

<Faq q="Can I see my team's calls?">
Yes. Owners and Admins see every conversation in the company. See [Review your agents' conversations](/help/admins/review-team-conversations/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I listen to my agents' calls?">
No. Sayso never records audio. You can read each call's summary, Cue insights, Smart Capture notes and Pulse data.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I use Sayso for coaching 1:1s?">
Yes. Filter the Dashboard and Conversations to one agent, scan their recent calls, and look for patterns: skipping motivation questions, not asking for the appointment, or getting stuck on the same objection. Use that as your 1:1 agenda.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Do I get a summary by email?">
Yes. Owners and Admins get a weekly email report by default. See [Weekly and monthly email reports](/help/admins/email-reports/).
</Faq>

## Accounts and CRM

<Faq q="Does the team connect one CRM for everyone?">
No. Each agent connects their own Follow Up Boss or Sure Send account. See [CRM connections on a team](/help/admins/crm-connections-for-teams/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="An agent made their own account before I invited them. What now?">
See [Inviting an agent who already has a Sayso account](/help/admins/invite-someone-with-a-solo-account/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can an agent change their sign-in email?">
Not from the app. Have agents join with an email they'll keep. If an email must change, contact support.
</Faq>

<Faq q="What happens to an agent's data if they leave?">
If you remove them with <Ui>Remove member</Ui>, their account and history are permanently deleted. If you only take them off a team with <Ui>Remove from team</Ui>, they stay in your company with everything intact. See [Remove an agent from your company](/help/admins/remove-an-agent/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I move someone from agent to admin, or back?">
Yes, if you're the Owner. See [Change someone's role](/help/admins/change-a-members-role/).
</Faq>
