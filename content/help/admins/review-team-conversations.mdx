## Open the company's conversations

1. Open <Ui>Conversations</Ui> in the sidebar. As an Owner or Admin you see calls from the whole company.
2. To narrow it down, open the filters and pick a team under <Ui>Team</Ui> or a person under <Ui>Members</Ui>.
3. Combine with search, the date range and the other filters (summary created, lead type, appointment booked or not).
4. Click a conversation to open it.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/search-and-filter-conversations-1.webp" alt="The Conversations Filters panel" />

## What you can see on an agent's call

- The call summary, date, time and lead type.
- The lead's name, if the agent linked the call to a lead in the CRM.
- The <Ui>Cue</Ui> tab: the insights Cue showed during the call.
- The <Ui>Smart Capture</Ui> tab: the LPMAMA notes.
- The <Ui>Pulse</Ui> tab: the market data looked up on the call.

## What stays with the agent

On someone else's call you can read everything above, but you can't rate Cue's insights, edit Smart Capture or send the call to the CRM. Those are done by the agent who made the call.

Sayso never records audio. See [Privacy and your data](/help/support/privacy-and-data/).
