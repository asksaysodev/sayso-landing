The <Ui>Company</Ui> page is in the sidebar of the web app at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com). Only the Owner and Admins on a team account see it. Agents with the Member role and individual accounts don't have it.

## Members tab

<Ui>Members</Ui> lists everyone in your company and every invite that hasn't been accepted yet.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Member</Ui> | The person's name. Pending invites have no name yet. |
| <Ui>E-mail</Ui> | The email they sign in with, or the email you invited. |
| <Ui>Role</Ui> | Owner, Admin or Member. |
| <Ui>Status</Ui> | <Ui>Active</Ui> (joined), <Ui>Invited</Ui> (invite sent, not accepted yet) or <Ui>Expired</Ui> (invite older than 14 days). |

Click a column name to sort. Use <Ui>Search...</Ui> to find someone by name or email. Use <Ui>Add member</Ui> to send invites. Each row has a menu with the actions you're allowed to take on that person.

## Teams tab

<Ui>Teams</Ui> lists the teams inside your company.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Name</Ui> | The team name. |
| <Ui>Members</Ui> | How many people are on the team. |
| <Ui>Hours allocated</Ui> | Hours used this month out of the team's cap, for example 20 / 25h. A dash means no cap is set. |
| <Ui>Team status</Ui> | <Ui>On track</Ui>, <Ui>Approaching</Ui>, <Ui>Over</Ui> or <Ui>No cap set</Ui>. |

Click a team to open its page, where you can add members, set hours, rename or delete it. Use <Ui>Add team</Ui> to create one. If you have no teams yet, you'll see <Ui>Create your first team</Ui>.
