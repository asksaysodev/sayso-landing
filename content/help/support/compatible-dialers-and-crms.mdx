Sayso sits alongside your CRM, dialer and lead sources and coaches the conversation. It does not dial, and it does not replace any of your tools.

## The one rule

If you can hear the call through your computer's headphones, Sayso can hear it too. If the call rings on your cell phone, Sayso cannot.

Check your dialer's calling setting. It should be set to call from your computer or browser (sometimes called internet calling, computer audio or "ask me"), never to always use your mobile phone.

## Works with Sayso

| Tool | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Follow Up Boss** | Call from Follow Up Boss on your computer, in Chrome. Calling Lists let you dial one lead after another. Connect Follow Up Boss to send notes with one click. See [Connect Follow Up Boss](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/). |
| **Sure Send** | Call from Sure Send on your computer. Connect Sure Send with an API token to send notes. See [Connect Sure Send](/help/web-app/connect-sure-send/). |
| **Mojo** | Dial into your Mojo session from a computer dialer, not your cell phone, so the session plays through your computer. |
| **Vulcan7, REDX** | Use the computer dialer, not the option that rings your phone. |
| **Google Voice** | A free computer dialer if you do not have one. Use it in Chrome on your computer. |
| **Other browser or desktop dialers** | Any dialer that plays the call through your computer works. |
| **Zoom, Google Meet** | Works for video consultations. The other person's audio counts as the lead. |

For any other CRM, such as Sierra or kvCORE, copy your notes from the Coach or from Conversations in the web app and paste them into the lead's record.

## Does not work today

- Calls placed or answered on your cell phone, including Zillow calls that ring your phone
- Dialers set to route calls to your mobile phone
- A separate desk phone or handset

## Troubleshooting

- **Sayso is not hearing the call:** the audio is not coming through your computer. Check your dialer's calling setting and wear headphones. See [Set up your dialer for Sayso](/help/agents/set-up-your-dialer/).
- **Prompts react to a voicemail greeting, screener or hold music:** press Reset when a person answers, or raise Buffer Time. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/) and [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/).

Want Sayso to connect directly to your CRM or dialer? See [Request a feature or integration](/help/support/request-a-feature/).
