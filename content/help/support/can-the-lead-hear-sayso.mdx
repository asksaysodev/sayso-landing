## How it works

Sayso hears your voice through your microphone and the lead through your computer's audio. It shows prompts on your screen. It never plays audio or adds any sound to the call, so the lead has a normal conversation with you.

## What about clicking and typing?

Smart Capture takes your notes, so you do not need to type during the call. Clicking in the Coach will not be heard either, as long as your microphone does not pick up your keyboard and mouse. A headset microphone helps with that, and headphones are needed for Sayso to work well anyway. See [Set up your audio](/help/agents/set-up-your-audio/).

## Screen sharing on video calls

If you share your **entire screen** on a Zoom or Google Meet call, the other people can see anything on that screen, including the Coach. Share a single window or browser tab instead, and keep the Coach on a part of the screen you are not sharing. See [Move, resize and position the Coach](/help/desktop-app/move-and-resize-coach/).

## Do I need to tell the lead?

Sayso is silent, but it does transcribe the conversation. Some states require everyone on a call to agree before it is recorded or transcribed. See [Is it legal to use Sayso on calls?](/help/support/call-recording-consent/)
