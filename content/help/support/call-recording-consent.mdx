## What Sayso does on a call

- Sayso **does not record or store audio.** It streams the call audio to be transcribed in real time, and the audio is not kept.
- Sayso **does transcribe** the conversation, and a written transcript is saved with the conversation in your account.
- Sayso **does not place calls, send texts or dial anyone.** You make the calls with your own dialer.

See [Privacy and your data](/help/support/privacy-and-data/) for everything Sayso saves.

## Why consent can matter

Recording and eavesdropping laws vary by state. Some states let one person on the call agree to it. Others require everyone on the call to agree, and some of these laws can cover transcription as well as audio recording.

When you sign up for Sayso, you agree to give any notice and get any consent the law requires before you use Sayso on a call, including in states that require everyone on the call to agree. That is part of the [Sayso Privacy Policy](/privacy/).

## A simple disclosure

Many agents mention it at the start of the call. For example:

> "Just so you know, I use software that transcribes and analyzes our conversation so I can serve you better. Is that okay with you?"

If the lead says no, stop the session in the Coach and continue the call without Sayso.

<Callout type="tip">
If you call across state lines, the stricter state's rules may apply. When in doubt, use the disclosure on every call.
</Callout>
