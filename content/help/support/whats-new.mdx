The desktop app updates itself. To see which version you have, open the Sayso menu, choose <Ui>Settings</Ui>, then <Ui>Software Update</Ui>. See [Update the desktop app](/help/desktop-app/update-sayso/). The web app at app.asksayso.com is always on the latest version.

## November 2026

**Desktop app 1.3.8** (Nov 3)

- **One-click start.** The Coach widget shows <Ui>Start for Seller</Ui> and <Ui>Start for Buyer</Ui> instead of a lead type dropdown. See [Start a coaching session](/help/desktop-app/start-a-session/).
<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/coach-window-tour-1.webp" alt="The Coach widget with Start for Seller and Start for Buyer" />

- **A more compact Coach widget** that opens near the left edge of your screen. See [Coach widget tour](/help/desktop-app/coach-window-tour/).
- **Meeting detection.** When a call starts in your web browser, Sayso shows a small prompt, "Sayso identified a call," with a Start button for Buyer or Seller. Turn it off under <Ui>Settings</Ui>, then <Ui>General</Ui>, then <Ui>Meeting detection</Ui>. Requires macOS 14.2 or newer on a Mac. See [Meeting detection](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/).
- **Clearer messages when coaching can't start.** If your team is out of coaching minutes or has reached its hour cap, the Coach widget says so and tells you to check with your team's owner or admin. See [Messages you might see in the Coach widget](/help/desktop-app/out-of-minutes-messages/).
- The Coach widget no longer gets stuck after your Mac wakes from sleep.
- Fewer crashes when audio devices change, and the Sayso icon now shows in the Windows taskbar at startup.

<Screenshot src="/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection-1.webp" alt="The Sayso identified a call prompt" width={520} />

**Web app**

- **Cancel or pause inside the web app** (Nov 24). On the <Ui>Subscription</Ui> tab, click <Ui>I want to cancel</Ui> to pause for 1, 2 or 3 months or cancel your renewal. Resume early, or reactivate a canceled plan before it ends, from the same tab. See [Cancel, pause or change your plan](/help/web-app/cancel-subscription/).
- **Email reports for owners and admins** (Nov 11). Team owners and admins get a weekly email with the company's conversation time. Change the frequency or day, or turn it off, under <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>, then <Ui>Reports</Ui>. See [Weekly and monthly email reports](/help/admins/email-reports/).
- **Change your password in Settings** (Nov 13), under <Ui>Security</Ui>. Sayso emails you when your password changes. See [Change your password](/help/web-app/change-password/).
- **Download from your Dashboard** (Nov 12). After you sign up, you land on your Dashboard with the <Ui>Download</Ui> button highlighted, and the download opens in a window. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).
- **Leads you create from Sayso show "Sayso" as their source** in Follow Up Boss (Nov 10). Creating a lead no longer links the conversation to an existing lead with the same details.
- **Account Settings opens over the page** on a computer, and full screen on a phone (Nov 10 to 16).
- **New sign in, password reset and sign up screens** (Nov 2 to 5). Signing up takes two steps, Account and then Subscription. Agents invited to a team skip the plan step. See [Create your Sayso account](/help/web-app/create-account/).
- **Billing is managed by the company owner** on team accounts (Nov 5). Admins and members who reach a billing screen are asked to contact their account owner. See [Owner, Admin and Member](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/).
- The <Ui>Reports</Ui> tab only shows on team accounts.

**Emails**

- **Office hours and workshop invites** (Nov 4). Sayso emails you before the weekly office hours and the monthly workshop, with a link to RSVP. See [Weekly office hours](/help/support/office-hours/).

**Sayso tools**

- **Sayso Map: accounts and plans** (Nov 27). Sign up with your email and a password and get 3 free Comp Reports, then choose <Ui>Map 10</Ui>, <Ui>Map 30</Ui> or <Ui>Map Unlimited</Ui>. Agents on a Sayso team join with a team passcode. Searches are now called Comp Reports and are saved to your account on every device. Manage, pause or cancel your plan under <Ui>Settings</Ui>, then <Ui>Subscription</Ui>. See [Create a Sayso Map account and log in](/help/tools/get-sayso-map/) and [Sayso Map plans and billing](/help/tools/sayso-map-plans/).
- **Sayso Composer: new log in pop-up** (Nov 27). <Ui>Log in</Ui> and <Ui>Create account</Ui> open a pop-up on the page you're on, and you reset your password in the same pop-up. If you use Sayso Map, log in with your Map login. See [Log in to Sayso Composer](/help/tools/log-in-to-sayso-composer/).
- **Sayso Map: AVM column in recent searches.** Each recent search shows <Ui>On market</Ui> for a listed home, or the AVM with a confidence dot for an off-market home. See [Recent searches](/help/tools/use-sayso-map/).
- **Sayso Map and Sayso Composer** are included for current Sayso customers at no extra cost. Map finds similar homes for sale near any address, and Composer writes a prospecting script for your next call. Email support@asksayso.com for access. See [Sayso Map](/help/tools/sayso-map/) and [Sayso Composer](/help/tools/sayso-composer/).
- **Sayso Map: download a PDF of your results.** Use the download icon next to <Ui>Email me</Ui>, or download the report from the email.
- **Sayso Map: value estimates and condition badges.** Off-market homes can show [a value estimate](/help/tools/sayso-map-off-market-estimates/). Similar homes can show [condition badges](/help/tools/sayso-map-condition-ratings/) based on available listing photos.
- **Sayso Map: separate buyer and seller text templates.** Edit <Ui>Buyer text</Ui> and <Ui>Seller text</Ui> under <Ui>Filters</Ui>. Sayso Map chooses the template based on whether the home you searched is listed for sale or off market. See [Change Sayso Map filters](/help/tools/sayso-map-filters/).

## October 2026

**Desktop app 1.3.5** (Oct 26)

- More reliable microphone start at the beginning of a session.
- The microphone no longer restarts while your computer is asleep. Sayso offers <Ui>Reset</Ui> when you come back.
- Clear messages when your subscription is not active or your team has reached its hour cap.
- Fixed a crash on Windows computers with Snapdragon (ARM) processors.

**Web app**

- **Edit Smart Capture after a call** (Oct 30). Fix or fill in any LPMAMA field in Conversations, and resend it to your CRM if the conversation is already linked. You can also open or change the linked lead. See [Edit Smart Capture notes after a call](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).
- **Voicemails and calls that never connect** no longer count as conversations or use coaching minutes (Oct 22). See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).
- **Invite agents with a role and a team** (Oct 19). See [Invite agents to your company](/help/admins/invite-agents/).
- **New Account Settings and Subscription view** showing your plan, hours and usage (Oct 16). See [Your subscription](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/).
- **Owners and admins see which agent had each conversation** (Oct 15). See [Review your agents' conversations](/help/admins/review-team-conversations/).
- **Coach Tips** on your Dashboard, personal tips based on your own calls (Oct 14). See [Coach Tips](/help/web-app/coach-tips/).
- **New Dashboard and Conversations pages,** and a new sidebar (Oct 13 to 15). See [Your Dashboard](/help/web-app/dashboard/) and [Find and review your conversations](/help/web-app/conversations/).
- **Only the owner can change the company name** (Oct 13). See [Rename your company](/help/admins/rename-your-company/).
- **The Company page,** with Members and Teams tabs, for owners and admins (Oct 8). Create teams, set hour caps and manage agents in one place. See [How companies and teams work](/help/admins/teams-overview/).
- **Team invites last 14 days,** up from 7.
- New look for Sayso's emails.

## September 2026

**Desktop app 1.3.3 and 1.3.4** (Sep 18 and Sep 25)

- **Sayso for Windows.** Sayso runs on Windows 10 and 11, with its own shortcuts (<Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>S</Kbd> for the Coach widget, <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>B</Kbd> for Playbooks) and a microphone-only permissions screen. See [Install Sayso on Windows](/help/desktop-app/install-on-windows/).
- **Calls in your browser.** Sayso now hears your voice on calls in Safari and in browser dialers such as Google Meet and the Follow Up Boss web dialer. See [Use Sayso on Zoom or Google Meet calls](/help/agents/video-calls/).
- **My Account** in the Sayso menu opens the web app already signed in.
- **Find in a playbook** (<Kbd>Cmd</Kbd> + <Kbd>F</Kbd>) searches the playbook you have open.
- On a Mac, clicking the Sayso icon in the Dock opens the Sayso menu.
- **Launch at Login** works on Windows, and uninstalling Sayso removes it.
- The "can't hear your microphone" banner is gone.
- Cue stops cleanly when your session expires.
- Logs no longer include sign-in tokens, and only the last week of logs is kept. See [Send your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).

**Web app**

- **A new download window** for Mac and Windows, with help choosing between Apple Silicon and Intel (Sep 28). The email with your download links now includes Windows. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).
- Clearer error messages when an invite can't be sent (Sep 17).
- "Groups" are now called **teams** everywhere.
- A new, faster sign up (Sep 4).
