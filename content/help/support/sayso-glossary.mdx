## A to C

**Admin.** A role on a team account. Admins manage agents, teams and reports, but not billing. See [Owner, Admin and Member](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/).

**Appointments booked.** A number on your Dashboard. Sayso marks a conversation as booked when the call ends with a confirmed appointment. See [Your Dashboard](/help/web-app/dashboard/).

**Auto Stop.** A desktop setting that ends a session after the lead has been quiet for a set number of minutes. See [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/).

**Buffer Time.** A Cue setting that waits a few seconds after you press Start or Reset before insights appear, so voicemail greetings and screeners do not trigger them. See [Buffer Time](/help/desktop-app/buffer-time/).

**Buyer or Seller.** The lead type you choose when you start a session. It tells Cue which kind of conversation to coach. See [Choose Buyer or Seller before you start](/help/agents/choose-buyer-or-seller/).

**Coach widget.** The Sayso desktop window you use during calls. It holds Cue, Smart Capture and Pulse. See [Coach widget tour](/help/desktop-app/coach-window-tour/).

**Coach Tips.** Personal coaching tips on your Dashboard, based on your own calls. They appear after you have had enough coached conversations. See [Coach Tips](/help/web-app/coach-tips/).

**Company.** Your organization in Sayso. On a team account, the company holds all your agents and teams. See [How companies and teams work](/help/admins/teams-overview/).

**Condensed.** The shorter of the two Prompt Style options. See [Prompt Style](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/).

**Connect, Discover, Convert.** The three stages Cue follows during a call: building rapport, learning about the lead, and asking for the appointment. See [How Cue follows your call](/help/agents/call-stages/).

**Connections.** The tab in Account Settings where you connect Follow Up Boss or Sure Send. See [Connect Follow Up Boss](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/).

**Conversation.** The saved record of one coached session, with its summary, Smart Capture notes, Cue insights and Pulse data. Find them under Conversations in the web app. See [Find and review your conversations](/help/web-app/conversations/).

**Conversation time.** How long you spent in coached conversations, shown on your Dashboard. Your plan's coaching minutes are counted from it. See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).

**Cue.** Sayso's live prompting. Cue listens to the conversation and shows you what to say or ask next. See [Cue](/help/desktop-app/cue/).

## D to M

**Dashboard.** The home page of the web app, with your activity, conversation time, appointments booked and Coach Tips. See [Your Dashboard](/help/web-app/dashboard/).

**Email reports.** A weekly or monthly email to owners and admins with the company's conversation time. See [Weekly and monthly email reports](/help/admins/email-reports/).

**Full Insights.** The longer of the two Prompt Style options, with more detail in each prompt. See [Prompt Style](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/).

**Hours allocated.** The monthly cap on coaching hours an owner or admin can set for a team. See [Set a team's monthly hour cap](/help/admins/set-team-hours/).

**Insight.** A single prompt from Cue. You can pin insights during a call and rate them afterwards. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).

**Launch Coach.** The Sayso menu item that opens the Coach widget. See [Sayso's menu explained](/help/desktop-app/tray-menu/).

**LPMAMA.** Location, Price, Motivation, Agent, Mortgage, Appointment. The six things Smart Capture listens for. See [Smart Capture](/help/desktop-app/smart-capture/).

**Meeting detection.** When a call starts in your web browser, Sayso shows a small prompt, "Sayso identified a call", with a Start button. See [Meeting detection](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/).

**Member.** The standard role on a team account, for agents. Members use Sayso on their own calls and see their own conversations. See [Owner, Admin and Member](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/).

## N to Z

**Not captured.** What a Smart Capture field shows in the web app when Sayso did not hear that detail on the call. You can fill it in yourself. See [Edit Smart Capture notes after a call](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).

**Owner.** The role that controls a company's billing, roles and name. See [Owner, Admin and Member](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/).

**Pin.** Keeps an insight on screen while new ones arrive. You can pin up to three. See [Pin an insight](/help/desktop-app/pin-insights/).

**Playbooks.** Your scripts in their own window, next to the Coach widget. Sayso includes ready-made playbooks, and you can upload your own. See [Playbooks](/help/desktop-app/playbooks/).

**Prompt Style.** A Cue setting that chooses between Full Insights and Condensed prompts. See [Prompt Style](/help/desktop-app/prompt-style/).

**Pulse.** Local market data by ZIP code, inside the Coach widget. See [Pulse](/help/desktop-app/pulse/).

**Reset.** Saves the current conversation and starts a new one with the same lead type, without leaving the Coach widget. Use it when a new person picks up. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

**Session.** The time between starting coaching in the Coach widget and pressing Stop or Reset. Each session becomes a conversation in the web app. See [Start a coaching session](/help/desktop-app/start-a-session/).

**Smart Capture.** Sayso's live note-taking. It fills in your LPMAMA notes while you talk. See [Smart Capture](/help/desktop-app/smart-capture/).

**Sayso menu.** The Sayso icon in your Mac menu bar or Windows taskbar, and the menu it opens. See [Sayso's menu explained](/help/desktop-app/tray-menu/).

**Sayso Note.** The note Sayso adds to a lead in Follow Up Boss when you send a conversation to your CRM. See [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/).

**Team.** A group of agents inside a company, with its own optional hour cap. An agent can be on one team at a time. See [How companies and teams work](/help/admins/teams-overview/).

**Two-factor authentication.** An extra sign-in step that asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. See [Turn on two-factor authentication](/help/web-app/two-factor-authentication/).
