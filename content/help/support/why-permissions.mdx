Sayso coaches you by listening to the conversation. To hear both sides, it needs access to your microphone and to the sound your computer plays.

## On a Mac

The first time you open Sayso, the <Ui>Permissions</Ui> screen asks for two things:

| Permission | What it is for |
|---|---|
| <Ui>Microphone</Ui> | To hear your side of the conversation |
| <Ui>Screen & System Audio</Ui> | To hear the lead through your computer's audio |

<Screenshot src="/help/support/why-permissions-1.webp" alt="The Sayso Permissions screen on Mac listing Microphone and Screen & System Audio" caption="The Permissions screen on a Mac." />

macOS groups system audio with screen recording, so the second permission is listed under **Screen Recording** in System Settings. Sayso only uses the audio. Nothing on your screen is captured or saved.

Without both permissions, Sayso hears only half the call and prompts will not work. After you allow Screen & System Audio, macOS needs Sayso to restart, which is why the button says <Ui>Quit and Reopen →</Ui>.

For the full steps, see [Allow microphone and screen & system audio on Mac](/help/desktop-app/mac-permissions/).

## On Windows

Windows lets Sayso hear your computer's audio without a separate permission, so Sayso only asks for the <Ui>Microphone</Ui>. Turn on <Ui>Microphone access</Ui> and <Ui>Let desktop apps access your microphone</Ui> in Windows Settings, then click <Ui>Continue →</Ui>. No restart is needed.

For the full steps, see [Allow microphone access on Windows](/help/desktop-app/windows-microphone-permission/).

## When Sayso listens

Sayso only captures audio while a coaching session is active, after you start a session in the Coach widget. It stops when you press Stop or when [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/) ends the session.

[Meeting detection](/help/desktop-app/meeting-detection/) is different: it only checks whether your browser is using the microphone, so Sayso can ask if you want to start coaching. It does not listen to the call.

Audio is never saved. A written transcript of each coached conversation is saved with the conversation. See [Privacy and your data](/help/support/privacy-and-data/).

## Clicked "Don't Allow" by accident?

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Mac">

1. Open **System Settings**, then **Privacy & Security**.
2. Open **Microphone** and turn on **Sayso**.
3. Open **Screen Recording** and turn on **Sayso**.
4. Quit Sayso and open it again.

If the switches are on but Sayso still says a permission is missing, you may have two copies of Sayso installed. See [Install Sayso on Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/).

</Tab>
<Tab label="Windows">

1. Open **Settings**, then **Privacy & security**, then **Microphone**.
2. Turn on **Microphone access**.
3. Turn on **Let desktop apps access your microphone**.
4. Go back to Sayso and click <Ui>Continue →</Ui>.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Other pop-ups you might see

- **"Downloaded from the internet" on a Mac:** click **Open**. macOS shows this for apps downloaded outside the App Store.
- **Local network or notifications:** Sayso works whether you allow these or not.
