## Check the quick fixes first

Check these known fixes first:

- [Why am I not seeing prompts?](/help/desktop-app/no-prompts/)
- [Sayso hears me but not the lead](/help/desktop-app/lead-not-heard/)
- [My microphone isn't working](/help/desktop-app/microphone-not-working/)
- [The Coach widget or Sayso won't open](/help/desktop-app/coach-wont-open/)

If none of those help, report the bug.

## How to report a bug

1. Write down what happened: what you clicked, what you expected, and what you saw instead.
2. Take a screenshot of any error message or anything that looks wrong.
3. Find your Sayso version. In the desktop app, open the Sayso menu, choose <Ui>Settings</Ui>, then <Ui>Software Update</Ui>.
4. If the bug is in the desktop app, find your log file for that day. See [Send your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).
5. Email everything to [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) with "Bug report" in the subject.

<Screenshot src="/help/support/report-a-bug-1.webp" alt="The Software Update tab in Sayso desktop settings showing the installed version" caption="Your Sayso version is under Settings, then Software Update." />

## Copy this into your email

```
Subject: Bug report

Account email:
Company (if you are on a team):
Mac or Windows (and version):
Sayso version:
Date and time it happened (with time zone):
Where it happened: desktop app or web app
What I did:
What I expected:
What happened instead:
Does it happen every time? Yes / No / Sometimes
Attached: screenshot / logs
```

<Callout type="tip">
The time of day matters most. Logs cover every minute you had Sayso open, so "around 2:15pm Eastern" lets us go straight to the right moment.
</Callout>

## What happens next

The team reads every report. We may ask a follow-up question or ask you to try something and tell us what you see. If it is easier to show us, bring it to [office hours](/help/support/office-hours/).
