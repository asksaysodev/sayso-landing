## When and where

Office hours happen **every Wednesday at 11am PT (2pm ET)** on Google Meet. Anyone with a Sayso account can join.

[Join office hours on Google Meet](https://meet.google.com/nyf-nfyz-okr)

You do not need to sign up. Open the link a minute or two before the hour and the team will let you in.

<Screenshot src="/help/support/office-hours-1.webp" alt="The Help Center support links with weekly office hours and the Join on Google Meet button" />

## Reminder emails

If your account is active, you get a reminder email the day before, on Tuesday. When a session is cancelled, there is no reminder that week. Every reminder has an unsubscribe link at the bottom if you would rather not get them. Unsubscribing from office hours reminders does not stop other Sayso emails.

We also hold a monthly workshop for Sayso users, and you get an email before each one.

## What to bring

Bring something specific, such as:

- A setup question, such as audio, permissions or your dialer
- A call you want to talk through, including what Cue showed you and what you said
- A feature you are not sure how to use, such as Smart Capture, Pulse or Playbooks
- Questions about rolling out Sayso across your team, if you are an admin or owner

<Callout type="tip">
If something is not working on your computer, send your logs to support before the session. See [Send your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/). We can look at them together on the call.
</Callout>

## Can't make it?

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) any time. See [Contact Sayso support](/help/support/contact-support/) for what to include.
