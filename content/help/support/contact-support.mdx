## Email support

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com). This one address handles every kind of question: setup, billing, your team, bugs and feature ideas. Replies to the office hours and workshop emails come to this inbox too.

## What to include

A few details save a lot of back and forth:

1. **The email on your Sayso account.** If you are on a team, add your company name.
2. **Your computer.** Mac or Windows, and the version if you know it.
3. **Your Sayso version.** In the desktop app, open the Sayso menu, choose <Ui>Settings</Ui>, then <Ui>Software Update</Ui>. It shows something like "Sayso v1.3.8 is up to date."
4. **What happened, and what you expected.** The steps you took, in order.
5. **When it happened.** The date and roughly what time, with your time zone.
6. **A screenshot,** if there is something on screen to show.

For anything that goes wrong in the desktop app, attach your logs too. See [Send your logs](/help/support/send-your-logs/).

## Talk to us live

Join [weekly office hours](/help/support/office-hours/) every Wednesday at 11am PT (2pm ET) on Google Meet.
