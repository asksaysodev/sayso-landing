Your name shows on your conversations and, if you're on a team, to your team owner and admins.

## Edit your profile

1. In the web app, click your name in the top-right corner and choose <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>. It opens on <Ui>Profile</Ui>.
2. Edit <Ui>Name</Ui>, <Ui>Last Name</Ui> or <Ui>Phone</Ui>.
3. Click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/edit-profile-1.webp" alt="The Profile tab in Account Settings" />

## Change your email

The <Ui>Email</Ui> field is read-only. To move your account to a different email, contact [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) from your current account email and tell us the new address.

## Change your company name

That lives on the <Ui>Company</Ui> tab, and only a team owner can change it. See [Rename your company](/help/admins/rename-your-company/).
