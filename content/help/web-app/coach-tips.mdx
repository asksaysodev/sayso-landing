Coach Tips sit at the top of your Dashboard. Sayso reads your recent coached calls, looks for patterns, and turns them into short tips for your next calls.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/coach-tips-1.webp" alt="Coach Tips on the Dashboard with the arrows to move between tips" />

## When your first tips appear

Your first tips show up after **10 eligible calls**. Until then, the card reads <Ui>Your coach tips are on the way</Ui>.

A call is eligible when it was a sales conversation that:

- Lasted at least one minute
- Had both you and the other person talking, with some back-and-forth
- Was counted toward your coaching minutes

Voicemails, calls that didn't connect, and practice or demo sessions don't count.

## How tips stay fresh

Sayso updates your tips after every 5 new eligible calls.

## Tips for a team or the whole company

Team owners and admins can see tips for a team or the whole company by filtering the Dashboard. Those tips start after 10 eligible calls from at least 2 different members, and they update after every 10 new calls.

## Get better tips

- Press Reset when the lead picks up, so each call is logged cleanly. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).
- Rate Cue's insights in Conversations. See [Rate Cue's insights](/help/web-app/rate-cue-insights/).
