You create your account in the web app at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com/signup). Sign-up has two steps: **Account** and **Subscription**. Use Google Chrome.

<Callout type="note" title="Joining a team?">
If your brokerage or team uses Sayso, don't sign up on your own. Open the invite email from your team owner or admin and accept it. See [Join your team from an invite](/help/agents/join-your-team/).
</Callout>

## Step 1: Account

1. Go to [app.asksayso.com/signup](https://app.asksayso.com/signup). Already on the sign-in page? Click <Ui>Sign up</Ui>.
2. Under <Ui>Personal details</Ui>, enter your <Ui>First name</Ui>, <Ui>Last name</Ui> and <Ui>Brokerage or team name</Ui>. <Ui>Phone number (optional)</Ui> can stay empty.
3. Click <Ui>Continue</Ui>.
4. Under <Ui>Login details</Ui>, enter your <Ui>Email</Ui>, a <Ui>Password</Ui>, and the same password again in <Ui>Confirm password</Ui>.
5. Click <Ui>Continue</Ui>.

Your password needs **at least 8 characters** and **at least one number**. A checklist under the field turns green as you meet each rule.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/create-account-1.webp" alt="The Create account page on the Login details step" caption="Step 1 of sign-up: Account" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Use an email you'll keep">
Your sign-in email can't be changed from the web app later.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Subscription

After <Ui>Continue</Ui>, you land on <Ui>Select your plan</Ui>.

1. Choose <Ui>Monthly</Ui> or <Ui>Annually</Ui> at the top. Annual billing costs less per month.
2. On the plan you want, click the button (for example <Ui>Choose</Ui> followed by the plan name). Team plans ask you to <Ui>Select your package</Ui> first, based on team size and hours.
3. Pay in the Stripe checkout.

When payment goes through, you land on your Dashboard. For plan details, see [Choose a plan and subscribe](/help/web-app/choose-a-plan/) or the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## Next: download the desktop app

Live coaching happens in the Sayso desktop app, not in the browser. On your Dashboard, the <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui> button in the left sidebar is highlighted with a <Ui>Next step</Ui> tip. Click it and pick Mac or Windows. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).

Sign in to the desktop app with the same email and password.
