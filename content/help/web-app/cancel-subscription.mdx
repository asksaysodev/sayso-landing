You cancel or pause your subscription on our secure billing page, which is run by Stripe. On a team account, only the **owner** can do this.

## Cancel or pause

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com) and open <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> from the menu under your name.
2. Click <Ui>Subscription</Ui>.
3. Under <Ui>Subscription management</Ui>, click <Ui>Manage billing</Ui>. The billing page opens in a new tab.
4. Choose to cancel or pause your plan and follow the steps.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/subscription-tab-1.webp" alt="The Subscription tab with the plan card, invoices and the Manage billing button" caption="Manage billing is at the bottom of the Subscription tab." />

## After you cancel

- Your status shows <Ui>Cancelling</Ui>, with the line "You can continue using Sayso until" your end date.
- You keep full access, coaching included, until the end of the period you already paid for.
- Your plan doesn't renew, and you aren't charged again.

## While your plan is paused

- Your status shows <Ui>Paused</Ui>.
- You can still sign in and see your conversations, but coaching is off until your plan resumes.

## Upgrade, downgrade or switch billing

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) with what you want, for example "Move us to annual billing" or "We need more hours for our team." We'll confirm the change and any price difference before it takes effect.

## Coming back later

Subscribe again from the same account whenever you're ready. Your conversations are still there.
