Plan changes and cancellations go through our support team today. There isn't a cancel button in the web app.

## Cancel

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) from your account email and tell us you want to cancel. On a team account, the **owner** sends the request.

After you cancel:

- Your status shows <Ui>Cancelling</Ui>, with the line "You can continue using Sayso until" your end date.
- You keep full access, coaching included, until the end of the period you already paid for.
- Your plan doesn't renew, and you aren't charged again.

## Upgrade, downgrade or switch billing

Email support with what you want, for example "Move us to annual billing" or "We need more hours for our team." We'll confirm the change and any price difference before it takes effect.

## Want a break instead?

Tell support what's going on. We'll walk through your options with you.

## Coming back later

Subscribe again from the same account whenever you're ready. Your conversations are still there.
