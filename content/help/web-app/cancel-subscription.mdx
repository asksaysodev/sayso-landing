You cancel or pause your subscription on the <Ui>Subscription</Ui> tab in the web app. On a team account, only the **owner** can do this.

## Cancel or pause

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com) and open <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> from the menu under your name.
2. Click <Ui>Subscription</Ui>.
3. Under <Ui>Subscription management</Ui>, click <Ui>I want to cancel</Ui>.
4. Choose <Ui>Pause subscription</Ui> or <Ui>Cancel subscription</Ui> and follow the steps.

## Pause your plan

Pick how long to pause: 1, 2 or 3 months.

- The pause starts when your current paid period ends. Until then, keep using Cue and the web app. Your plan shows <Ui>Pause scheduled</Ui>.
- During the pause, your plan shows <Ui>Paused</Ui>. Cue is off and you aren't charged. You can still sign in and see your conversations.
- Your plan resumes on its own on the date shown next to <Ui>Auto-resumes</Ui>.

To come back early, click <Ui>Resume now</Ui> on the Subscription tab. You pay for a new period that day, your hours refill, and Cue turns back on once the payment goes through.

To cancel a pause that hasn't started yet, remove the scheduled pause from the Subscription tab. You aren't charged, and your renewal date stays the same.

## Cancel your plan

1. Choose <Ui>Cancel subscription</Ui>.
2. Pick a reason, or click <Ui>Skip this step</Ui>.

   <Screenshot src="/help/web-app/cancel-subscription-2.webp" alt="Why are you leaving? with six reasons and Skip this step" width={420} />

3. Sayso may offer you a lower price to stay. Accept it, or continue to cancel.
4. Confirm. You see "Renewal canceled" with the date your access ends.

After you cancel:

- Your plan shows <Ui>Cancellation pending</Ui>.
- You keep full access, coaching included, until the end of the period you already paid for. On a team account, the whole team keeps access.
- Your plan doesn't renew, and you aren't charged again.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/cancel-subscription-3.webp" alt="The Subscription tab showing Cancellation pending, Full access until the end date and Reactivate subscription" />

Changed your mind before the end date? Click <Ui>Reactivate subscription</Ui> under <Ui>Subscription management</Ui>. You aren't charged that day, and your plan renews on its original date.

## Upgrade, downgrade or switch billing

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) with what you want, for example "Move us to annual billing" or "We need more hours for our team." We'll confirm the change and any price difference before it takes effect.

## Card and invoices

<Ui>Manage billing</Ui> on the Subscription tab opens a billing page run by Stripe, where you update your card and billing details. See [Update your card and download invoices](/help/web-app/update-payment-method/).

## Coming back later

Subscribe again from the same account at any time. Your conversations are still there.
