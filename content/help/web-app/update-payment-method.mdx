Sayso uses Stripe for payments. Your card and billing details are on a Stripe page you open from the web app.

## Open the billing page

1. Click your name in the top-right corner and choose <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>.
2. Open <Ui>Subscription</Ui>.
3. Under <Ui>Subscription management</Ui>, click <Ui>Manage billing</Ui>.

The Stripe page opens. When you're done, use its link back to Sayso.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/subscription-tab-1.webp" alt="The Subscription tab with Manage billing" />

## What you can do there

- **Update your payment method:** add a new card and make it the default, or remove an old one.
- **Update billing details:** name, email, phone, address and tax ID shown on your invoices.
- **See invoice history:** open, download or print past invoices.

To cancel or pause your plan from this same page, see [Cancel, pause or change your plan](/help/web-app/cancel-subscription/).

<Callout type="note" title="Payment failed?">
If a renewal payment fails, your status changes to <Ui>Past due</Ui>. Update your card on the Stripe page and Stripe retries the payment.
</Callout>

## Invoices without leaving Sayso

The <Ui>Invoices</Ui> list on the Subscription tab shows every invoice. Click one to open it.

## On a team?

Only the team owner can open <Ui>Manage billing</Ui>. See [Billing for teams](/help/admins/team-billing/).
