The Dashboard is the first page you see in the web app. It has three parts: <Ui>Coach Tips</Ui>, <Ui>Activity</Ui> and <Ui>Overview</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/dashboard-1.webp" alt="The Sayso Dashboard with Coach Tips, Activity and Overview" />

## Coach Tips

Tips based on patterns in your recent calls. Use the arrows to move between tips. Tips appear once Sayso has enough calls to learn from. See [Coach Tips](/help/web-app/coach-tips/).

## Activity

A bar chart of your coached minutes for each day of the week, with the total minutes for that week.

- Use the <Ui>Previous week</Ui> and <Ui>Next week</Ui> arrows to look at other weeks.
- A week with no coached calls shows <Ui>No activity this week</Ui>.

## Overview

Two numbers for the period you pick:

- <Ui>Conversation time</Ui>: total time on coached calls.
- <Ui>Appointments booked</Ui>: coached calls where Sayso detected that an appointment was set.

Pick the period from the menu: <Ui>Last 7 days</Ui>, <Ui>Last 14 days</Ui>, <Ui>Last 30 days</Ui>, <Ui>Last 3 months</Ui> or <Ui>Last 6 months</Ui>. Each number shows how it changed against the period before, for example "+2 vs. previous 7 days".

## Refresh the numbers

The line under <Ui>Dashboard</Ui> shows when the data last updated. Click the refresh button next to the title to load the latest numbers.

## What counts

The Dashboard counts coached calls that Sayso logged as conversations. Calls where Sayso gave no insight, caught no Smart Capture notes and showed no Pulse data are not counted, and neither are voicemails or calls that never connected. See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).

<Callout type="note" title="Team owners and admins">
You see the whole company by default. The <Ui>Filters</Ui> button narrows the Dashboard to one team or one member. See [See your team's activity on the Dashboard](/help/admins/team-dashboard/).
</Callout>

## No Dashboard?

If your plan isn't active, the Dashboard shows <Ui>Your coaching starts here</Ui> with a prompt to choose a plan. Team members see a note to contact their account owner instead.
