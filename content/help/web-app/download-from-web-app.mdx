Live coaching happens in the Sayso desktop app for Mac and Windows. You download it from the web app.

## Download on your computer

1. Sign in at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com/login).
2. Click <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui> at the bottom of the left sidebar.
3. In <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui>, pick your computer. The one Sayso detects is marked as recommended.
   - **Windows:** under <Ui>Sayso for Windows</Ui>, click <Ui>Download</Ui>. Needs Windows 10 or later.
   - **Mac:** under <Ui>Sayso for Mac</Ui>, click <Ui>Download</Ui> and choose <Ui>Apple Silicon</Ui> or <Ui>Intel</Ui>.

Each option shows the latest version number.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/download-from-web-app-1.webp" alt="The Download Sayso window with Sayso for Windows and Sayso for Mac" />

<Callout type="tip" title="Not sure which Mac you have?">
Click <Ui>Not sure which chip you have?</Ui> in the download window. In short: open the Apple menu > **About This Mac**. If it says **Chip: Apple**, pick Apple Silicon. If it says **Processor** with Intel, pick Intel. See [Which Mac do I have?](/help/desktop-app/which-mac-chip/)
</Callout>

Then install it. See [Install on Mac](/help/desktop-app/install-on-mac/) or [Install on Windows](/help/desktop-app/install-on-windows/).

## On your phone

Sayso can't be installed on a phone. If you tap a download button on your phone, Sayso offers to email you the link:

1. Enter your email.
2. Tap <Ui>Send me the Link</Ui>.
3. Open the email on your computer and download from there.

## Need help?

Click <Ui>Contact Support</Ui> at the bottom of the download window to email our team.
