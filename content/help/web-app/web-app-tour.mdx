Use the web app to set up your account and review your calls. Live coaching happens in the desktop app. Both use the same sign-in.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/web-app-tour-1.webp" alt="The Sayso web app with the left sidebar and the Dashboard open" caption="The web app on a computer" />

## Left sidebar

| Item | What it does | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Ui>Dashboard</Ui> | Coach Tips, your weekly activity and your conversation time and appointments. See [Your Dashboard](/help/web-app/dashboard/). | Everyone with an active plan |
| <Ui>Conversations</Ui> | Every coached call with its summary, Cue insights, Smart Capture notes and Pulse data. See [Find and review your conversations](/help/web-app/conversations/). | Everyone with an active plan |
| <Ui>Company</Ui> | Members and teams for your company. See [Owner, Admin and Member](/help/admins/roles-and-permissions/). | Team owners and admins |
| <Ui>Help center</Ui> | Opens the Sayso Help Center. | Everyone |
| <Ui>Launch Coach</Ui> | Opens the Coach widget in the Sayso desktop app, if it's installed on this computer. | Everyone, on a computer |
| <Ui>Download Sayso</Ui> | Downloads the desktop app for Mac or Windows. See [Download Sayso from the web app](/help/web-app/download-from-web-app/). | Everyone |

Click <Ui>Collapse</Ui> at the bottom of the sidebar to shrink it to icons. Click the same button again to expand it.

On a phone, tap the menu button in the top bar to open the sidebar.

## Top bar

- **Back and forward arrows** move through the pages you've visited.
- **Your name** in the top-right corner opens your menu:
  - Your role badge (for example <Ui>Owner</Ui>, <Ui>Admin</Ui> or <Ui>Member</Ui>) if you're on a team
  - <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>: profile, security, CRM connections, subscription and more. See [Account Settings, tab by tab](/help/web-app/settings-overview/).
  - <Ui>Log out</Ui>
  - Links to the <Ui>Privacy Policy</Ui> and <Ui>Terms &amp; Conditions</Ui>

## Announcements

Sometimes the Sayso team posts an update, such as a new feature or an event. It shows in a small panel in the corner of the web app. You can collapse it or dismiss it.
