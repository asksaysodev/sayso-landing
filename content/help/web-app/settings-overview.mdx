Open Account Settings from your name in the top-right corner of the web app > <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>. On a computer it opens as a window over the page you were on. On a phone it opens as its own page.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/settings-overview-1.webp" alt="Account Settings with the list of tabs on the left" />

## The tabs

| Tab | What you do there | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Ui>Profile</Ui> | Edit your name and phone. Your email shows here but can't be changed. [Details](/help/web-app/edit-profile/) | Everyone |
| <Ui>Company</Ui> | Rename your company. [Details](/help/admins/rename-your-company/) | Team owners |
| <Ui>Connections</Ui> | Connect Follow Up Boss or Sure Send, or request another integration. [Details](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/) | Everyone |
| <Ui>Security</Ui> | Turn on two-factor authentication and change your password. [Details](/help/web-app/two-factor-authentication/) | Everyone |
| <Ui>Subscription</Ui> | See your plan, usage and invoices, and manage billing. [Details](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/) | Individual accounts and team owners |
| <Ui>Referrals</Ui> | Copy your referral link. [Details](/help/web-app/referrals/) | Individual accounts, team owners and admins |
| <Ui>Reports</Ui> | Turn weekly or monthly email reports on or off. [Details](/help/admins/email-reports/) | Team owners and admins |

If a tab in this list is missing for you, your role doesn't include it.

## Settings in the desktop app

Coaching settings such as Prompt Style, Buffer Time, Auto Stop, meeting detection and your playbooks live in the desktop app, not the web app. See [Desktop settings overview](/help/desktop-app/desktop-settings/).
