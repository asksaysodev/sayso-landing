You pick a plan on the <Ui>Select your plan</Ui> page, the second step of sign-up. You can also reach it from the web app if your account doesn't have an active plan.

For current prices and what each plan includes, see the [pricing page](/pricing/). As of this update, Individual Agent+ is $179 a month billed monthly, or $125 a month billed annually.

## Subscribe

1. At the top of <Ui>Select your plan</Ui>, choose <Ui>Monthly</Ui> or <Ui>Annually</Ui>. Annual billing saves up to 30%.
2. Click <Ui>See what's included</Ui> on any plan to read its features.
3. Choose your plan:
   - **Individual:** click the plan's button. Each plan shows its conversation hours per month.
   - **Team:** click <Ui>Select your package</Ui>, pick the option that matches your team size and hours, then click the plan's button.
   - **Plans that say "Let's talk":** click <Ui>Contact sales</Ui> and we'll build a package with you.
4. Pay in the Stripe checkout. Sayso never sees your full card number.

When payment goes through, you land on your Dashboard. Then download the desktop app. See [Download Sayso](/help/desktop-app/download-sayso/).

<Callout type="note" title="Canceled checkout?">
If you close the Stripe page without paying, you come back to <Ui>Select your plan</Ui> with the note "Payment canceled. You can pick a plan again."
</Callout>

## Already subscribed?

Your plan is on the <Ui>Subscription</Ui> tab of Account Settings. See [Your subscription: status, hours and invoices](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/). To switch plans, email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com). See [Cancel, pause or change your plan](/help/web-app/cancel-subscription/).

## On a team?

Only the team **owner** manages billing. Agents and admins on a team don't see plans or the Subscription tab. See [Billing for teams](/help/admins/team-billing/).
