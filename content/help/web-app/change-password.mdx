Change your password in Account Settings. Forgot your current password? Use [Reset a forgotten password](/help/web-app/reset-password/) instead.

## Change it

1. In the web app, click your name in the top-right corner and choose <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>.
2. Open <Ui>Security</Ui>.
3. On the <Ui>Password</Ui> card, click <Ui>Change</Ui>.
4. In <Ui>Update password</Ui>, enter your <Ui>Current password</Ui>, a <Ui>New password</Ui>, and the new one again in <Ui>Repeat new password</Ui>.
5. Click <Ui>Update password</Ui>.

You see a <Ui>Password updated.</Ui> message when it's done. New passwords need at least 8 characters and at least one number.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/change-password-1.webp" alt="The Update password window in Account Settings, Security" />

## If you use two-factor authentication

With two-factor authentication on, the button says <Ui>Continue</Ui> instead. After you click it, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to confirm the change.

## After you change it

Use the new password the next time you sign in to the desktop app.
