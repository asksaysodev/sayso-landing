Every Sayso plan includes a number of coaching hours per month.

## When a session counts

A session uses minutes only if at least one of these happened:

- Cue showed you an insight
- Smart Capture caught a note
- You used Pulse

If none of those happened, the session uses **no minutes**.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/subscription-tab-1.webp" alt="The Subscription tab showing minutes used this period" />

## How much it uses

A counted session uses the time from <Ui>Start</Ui> to <Ui>Stop</Ui>, rounded **up** to the next whole minute. A session of 4 minutes and 10 seconds uses 5 minutes.

<Callout type="tip" title="Save your hours">
Stop the session when your dial block ends. If you forget, Auto Stop ends it after the other person has been quiet for a while. See [Auto Stop](/help/desktop-app/auto-stop/).
</Callout>

## Voicemails and calls that don't connect

They don't show in Conversations or on your Dashboard. But if Cue showed an insight during a voicemail, that session still uses minutes. During a dial block, press Reset when someone picks up so the time stays tied to live conversations. See [When to hit Reset](/help/agents/when-to-reset/).

## When minutes refill

Your minutes refill to your plan's monthly amount each time your subscription invoice is paid. Unused minutes don't carry over. The <Ui>Subscription</Ui> tab shows how many you've used and when the current period ends. See [Your subscription: status, hours and invoices](/help/web-app/subscription-tab/).

## On a team

Everyone in your company shares one pool of minutes. Your owner or admins can also set an hour cap for each team. See [Set a team's monthly hour cap](/help/admins/set-team-hours/).

## If you run out

The Coach widget won't start a new session, and you'll see a message in the desktop app.

- **On your own plan:** email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) to talk about more hours or a bigger plan.
- **On a team:** let your team owner or admin know. See [Your team is out of coaching minutes](/help/agents/out-of-minutes/).
