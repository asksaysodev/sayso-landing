You can't delete your account from inside the web app today. Our support team handles deletion requests by email.

## Ask for deletion

Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) **from the email on your Sayso account** and tell us:

- That you want your account deleted, or only your conversation data
- Whether you also want to cancel your subscription (if you pay for Sayso yourself)

We reply by email to confirm the request.

<Callout type="note" title="Paying for your own plan?">
Deleting your account doesn't cancel billing by itself. Say in the same email that you want to cancel. See [Cancel, pause or change your plan](/help/web-app/cancel-subscription/).
</Callout>

## If you're on a team

Your team owner or admin manages your account. When they use <Ui>Remove member</Ui> on the Company page, your account and its history are deleted permanently. See [Remove an agent from your company](/help/admins/remove-an-agent/).

Leaving your team but want to keep using Sayso on your own? Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) before you're removed.
