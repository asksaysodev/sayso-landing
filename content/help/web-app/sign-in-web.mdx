The Sayso web app lives at [app.asksayso.com](https://app.asksayso.com/login). It works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone. The desktop app is where coaching happens during calls; the web app is where you review conversations and manage your account.

## Sign in

1. Go to [app.asksayso.com/login](https://app.asksayso.com/login).
2. Enter your <Ui>Email</Ui> and <Ui>Password</Ui>.
3. Click <Ui>Sign in</Ui>.

If you turned on two-factor authentication, the next screen asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. Enter it and click <Ui>Verify</Ui>. See [Turn on two-factor authentication](/help/web-app/two-factor-authentication/).

## If sign-in fails

<Faq q="It says “Email or password is incorrect.”">
Check the email for typos, then try your password again. Still stuck? Click <Ui>Forgot password?</Ui> under the password field to get a reset link. See [Reset your password](/help/web-app/reset-password/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="The 6-digit code isn't accepted.">
Codes change every 30 seconds. Wait for a fresh code in your authenticator app and enter it right away. Make sure the clock on your phone is set automatically.
</Faq>

<Faq q="I was invited to a team but have no password yet.">
Open the invite email and click the link in it. You set your password while accepting the invite. See [Join your team from an invite](/help/agents/join-your-team/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="I see “Contact your account owner to get access to Sayso.”">
Your account belongs to a team that doesn't have an active subscription right now. Ask your team owner to check billing.
</Faq>

## Sign out

Click your name in the top-right corner, then <Ui>Log out</Ui>.
