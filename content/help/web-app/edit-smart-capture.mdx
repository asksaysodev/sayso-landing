Smart Capture fills in your LPMAMA notes while you talk. It can mishear a name or a street, or miss a detail. You can fix any field after the call.

## Edit a field

1. Open <Ui>Conversations</Ui> and click the call.
2. Click the <Ui>Smart Capture</Ui> tab. You see six cards: <Ui>Location</Ui>, <Ui>Price</Ui>, <Ui>Motivation</Ui>, <Ui>Agent</Ui>, <Ui>Mortgage</Ui> and <Ui>Appointment</Ui>.
3. Click the pencil on a card. Empty fields read <Ui>Not captured</Ui>, and you can fill those in too.
4. Type your change.
5. Press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> or click <Ui>Save</Ui>.

To add a line break, press <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. To cancel, press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd>.

The <Ui>Appointment</Ui> card shows a <Ui>Booked</Ui> badge when Sayso detected that you set an appointment.

## Resync to your CRM

If the call is linked to a lead in Follow Up Boss or Sure Send, Sayso asks <Ui>Resync this conversation?</Ui> after you save.

- Click <Ui>Yes, resync</Ui> to send the updated notes to the lead. You see <Ui>Notes saved. Conversation resynced.</Ui> when it's done.
- Click <Ui>Dismiss</Ui> to keep the change in Sayso only.

If the resync fails, click <Ui>Retry</Ui>. If your CRM connection expired, reconnect it first. See [Disconnect or reconnect a CRM](/help/web-app/disconnect-or-reconnect/).

## Good to know

- Only the agent who made the call can edit its notes.
- Use <Ui>Not captured</Ui> fields as your list of what to ask on the next call.
