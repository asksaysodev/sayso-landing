With Follow Up Boss or Sure Send connected, each of your conversations has an <Ui>Add lead</Ui> button. Pick the lead the call was with, and Sayso posts the call notes to that lead in your CRM.

Not connected yet? See [Connect Follow Up Boss](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/) or [Connect Sure Send](/help/web-app/connect-sure-send/).

## Link the call to an existing lead

1. Open <Ui>Conversations</Ui> and find the call.
2. Click <Ui>Add lead</Ui> under the summary.
3. Type the lead's email or phone number in <Ui>Search by email or phone</Ui>.
4. Click the lead.

The lead's name now shows on the conversation, and Sayso adds the note to that lead in your CRM.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead-1.webp" alt="The Add lead menu with a search box and matching leads" />

## Create a new lead

If the person isn't in your CRM yet:

1. Click <Ui>Add lead</Ui>, then <Ui>Create lead</Ui> at the bottom of the menu.
2. Fill in <Ui>First name</Ui>, <Ui>Last name</Ui> and <Ui>Phone</Ui>. <Ui>Email</Ui> is optional for Follow Up Boss and required for Sure Send. Sure Send also asks for a <Ui>Stage</Ui>.
3. Click <Ui>Create lead</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead-2.webp" alt="The Create lead window for Follow Up Boss with first name, last name, phone and email fields" width={520} />

Sayso creates the lead in your CRM and links the call to it. In Follow Up Boss, the new lead's source is **Sayso**.

## What the note in your CRM looks like

Sayso adds a note titled **Sayso Note** to the lead. It includes:

- The call **summary**
- Every **Smart Capture** field (Location, Price, Motivation, Agent, Mortgage, Appointment). Empty fields say "Not captured", which tells you what to ask next time.
- **Market Pulse** numbers, if you used Pulse
- **Appointment: Booked**, if Sayso detected one

<Callout type="tip">
Smart Capture can mishear names and street names. Check the notes before you link the lead, or fix them afterward and resync. See [Edit Smart Capture notes after a call](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).
</Callout>

## Limits

- You can only link leads on your own calls.
- Calls aren't sent to your CRM automatically. Linking is what sends the note.
- Using a CRM Sayso doesn't connect to yet? Copy the notes from the Smart Capture tab and paste them into your CRM. Then [request an integration](/help/web-app/request-an-integration/).
