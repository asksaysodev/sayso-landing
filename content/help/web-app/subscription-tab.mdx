Open it from your name in the top-right corner > <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Subscription</Ui>.

Only people who pay for their own plan and the **owner** of a team account see this tab. Agents and admins on a team don't.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/subscription-tab-1.webp" alt="The Subscription tab with the plan card, invoices and Manage billing" />

## Your plan

The top card shows:

- Your **plan name** and **status**
- **Monthly** or **Yearly** billing, and the hours included per month
- Your renewal date, for example "Your subscription renews on December 15, 2026"
- How much you've used: "You've used 312 minutes this period, which ends on…"

### Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Active</Ui> | Your plan is paid and working. |
| <Ui>Cancellation pending</Ui> | You canceled your renewal. You can keep using Sayso until the date shown. |
| <Ui>Pause scheduled</Ui> | Your pause starts when your paid period ends. Until then, nothing changes. |
| <Ui>Cancelled</Ui> | Your plan has ended. |
| <Ui>Paused</Ui> | Your plan is on hold. You can still sign in, but coaching is off until it resumes or you click <Ui>Resume now</Ui>. |
| <Ui>Past due</Ui> | The last payment didn't go through. Update your card under <Ui>Manage billing</Ui>. |

## Invoices

Every invoice is listed with its <Ui>Date</Ui>, <Ui>Total</Ui> and <Ui>Status</Ui> (<Ui>Paid</Ui>, <Ui>Pending</Ui> or <Ui>Rejected</Ui>). Click an invoice to open it, download it or print it. Click a column name to sort.

## Subscription management

Click <Ui>Manage billing</Ui> to open the Stripe billing page, where you update your card and billing details. See [Update your card and download invoices](/help/web-app/update-payment-method/).

## Paid by a partner?

If a partner organization pays for your plan, the card says so, and there's no <Ui>Manage billing</Ui> button. Contact that organization about billing.
