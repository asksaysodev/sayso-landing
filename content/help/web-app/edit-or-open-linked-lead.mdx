After you link a call to a lead, the lead's name shows on the conversation. Click the name to open the lead menu.

## Open the lead in your CRM

Click <Ui>Open linked lead in CRM</Ui>. The lead opens in Follow Up Boss or Sure Send in a new tab.

## Edit the lead's details

1. Click <Ui>Edit linked lead</Ui>.
2. Change the name, phone or email in <Ui>Edit lead</Ui>. The window notes that changes sync to your CRM.
3. Click <Ui>Save changes</Ui>. You see <Ui>Lead updated.</Ui>

## Linked the wrong lead?

Click the lead's name, search for the right lead and click it. The call moves to the new lead and Sayso adds the note to it.

## Unlink the lead

Click <Ui>Unlink from conversation</Ui>. Sayso adds a short note to the lead saying it was unlinked from a Sayso conversation, and the call goes back to showing <Ui>Add lead</Ui>.

## Changed your notes after linking?

Edit the Smart Capture fields and choose <Ui>Yes, resync</Ui> when Sayso asks. See [Edit Smart Capture notes after a call](/help/web-app/edit-smart-capture/).
