The top of the <Ui>Conversations</Ui> page has a search bar, a date picker and a <Ui>Filters</Ui> button. Use them together to find a call fast.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/search-and-filter-conversations-1.webp" alt="The Conversations search bar, Date button and the Filters drawer" />

## Search

Type in <Ui>Search conversations...</Ui>. Sayso matches words in the call summaries, for example a street name, a neighborhood or "pre-approved". Results update as you type.

## Pick dates

1. Click <Ui>Date</Ui>.
2. Pick a start date and an end date on the calendar.
3. Click <Ui>Apply</Ui>.

To go back to all dates, open the calendar again and click <Ui>Reset</Ui>.

## Filters

Click <Ui>Filters</Ui> to open the filter panel:

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Summary</Ui> | <Ui>Summary created</Ui> (on by default): only calls that have a summary |
| <Ui>Lead type</Ui> | <Ui>All</Ui>, <Ui>Buyer</Ui> or <Ui>Seller</Ui> |
| <Ui>Appointment</Ui> | <Ui>All</Ui>, <Ui>Booked</Ui> or <Ui>Not booked</Ui> |

Click <Ui>Apply filters</Ui>. Each active filter shows as a chip under the search bar. Click the x on a chip to remove it, or open <Ui>Filters</Ui> and click <Ui>Clear all</Ui>.

<Callout type="note" title="Team owners and admins">
The filter panel also has <Ui>Team</Ui> and <Ui>Members</Ui> sections, so you can look at one team or one agent.
</Callout>

## No results?

You see a message such as "No conversations match your filters." Remove a chip or widen the dates, and check the spelling in the search box.
