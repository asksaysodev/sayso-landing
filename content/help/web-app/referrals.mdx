Share your referral link with other agents.

## Get your link

1. Click your name in the top-right corner and choose <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>.
2. Open <Ui>Referrals</Ui>.
3. Under <Ui>Your referral link</Ui>, click <Ui>Copy link</Ui>. You see <Ui>Referral link copied!</Ui>

Paste it in a text, an email or a post.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/referrals-1.webp" alt="The Referrals tab with the Copy link button" />

## How the reward works

1. The agent opens your link and creates their Sayso account.
2. They get **$25 off** when they subscribe.
3. When they pay their first invoice, **$25 in credit** is added to your Sayso billing account.
4. Your credit comes off your next invoice automatically.

The agent needs to sign up through your link for it to count.

## Who has a referral link

Agents with their own plan, and team owners and admins. Agents on a team plan don't see the Referrals tab.
