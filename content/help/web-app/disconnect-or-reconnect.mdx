## Disconnect

1. Open <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Connections</Ui>.
2. On the connected card, click <Ui>Manage</Ui>. The window shows the account that's connected.
3. Click <Ui>Disconnect Follow Up Boss</Ui> or <Ui>Disconnect Sure Send</Ui>.
4. Confirm with <Ui>Disconnect</Ui>. Changed your mind? Click <Ui>Keep connected</Ui>.

Sayso stops sending data to that CRM. Your conversations and the notes already in your CRM stay where they are. To connect again later, you'll sign in or paste a token again.

## Reconnect an expired connection

Sometimes a CRM connection expires, for example when access is revoked on the CRM side. You'll notice it when you click <Ui>Add lead</Ui> on a conversation: the menu says your connection expired and shows a <Ui>Reconnect</Ui> link.

1. Click the link, or go to <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Connections</Ui>.
2. Disconnect the CRM, then click <Ui>Connect</Ui> and follow the steps again.

See [Connect Follow Up Boss](/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss/) or [Connect Sure Send](/help/web-app/connect-sure-send/).

## Switch CRMs

Disconnect the old one, then connect the new one. Conversations you already linked keep their old link.
