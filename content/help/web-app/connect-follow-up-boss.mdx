Sayso works alongside Follow Up Boss. Once connected, link any conversation to a Follow Up Boss lead and Sayso adds a note with the call summary, your Smart Capture notes and Pulse data.

## Connect

1. In the web app, click your name in the top-right corner and choose <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>.
2. Open <Ui>Connections</Ui>.
3. On the <Ui>Follow Up Boss</Ui> card, click <Ui>Connect</Ui>.
4. Sign in to Follow Up Boss if asked, and approve access for Sayso.
5. You land back on <Ui>Connections</Ui> with the message <Ui>Follow Up Boss connected!</Ui> The card now shows <Ui>Connected</Ui>.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/connect-follow-up-boss-1.webp" alt="The Connections tab with the Follow Up Boss card showing Connected" />

If you cancel on the Follow Up Boss screen, you see <Ui>Follow Up Boss connection cancelled.</Ui> Click <Ui>Connect</Ui> to try again.

## Connection questions

<Faq q="Do I need a Follow Up Boss API key?">
No. You sign in to Follow Up Boss and approve access.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Does my Follow Up Boss role matter?">
No. Any Follow Up Boss user can connect.
</Faq>

<Faq q="The connection failed or showed an error.">
Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) with what you saw.
</Faq>

## How the connection works

- **Each agent connects their own account.** Connections belong to your Sayso login, not your team. See [CRM connections are per agent](/help/admins/crm-connections-for-teams/).
- **Notes go out when you link a lead.** Sayso doesn't send every call. You choose the lead for each conversation. See [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/).
- **Leads you create from Sayso** are added to Follow Up Boss with **Sayso** as the lead source.
- **Connected to Sure Send too?** When both are connected, Sayso uses Follow Up Boss for linking leads.
