When you use Pulse during a call, Sayso saves the market data with the conversation. Open the <Ui>Pulse</Ui> tab to see it again.

## Open it

1. Open <Ui>Conversations</Ui> and click the call.
2. Click the <Ui>Pulse</Ui> tab.

The row for the call also shows <Ui>Market Pulse</Ui> with the ZIP code when Pulse was used, or <Ui>No Market Pulse</Ui> when it wasn't.

<Screenshot src="/help/web-app/conversation-pulse-1.webp" alt="The Pulse tab of a conversation with four market numbers" />

## What you see

| Number | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| <Ui>Average days on market</Ui> | How long homes in that ZIP code typically sit before going under contract |
| <Ui>Average price / ft²</Ui> | Typical price per square foot in that ZIP code |
| <Ui>Price change</Ui> | Recent price trend, as a percentage with an up or down arrow |
| <Ui>Market type</Ui> | Whether the area leans toward buyers, sellers or is balanced |

A dash means that number wasn't available for the area. If the tab says <Ui>No market data available for this conversation.</Ui>, Pulse wasn't used on that call.

## Use it in your follow-up

The numbers are also included in the note Sayso sends to your CRM when you link the call to a lead. See [Send a conversation to your CRM](/help/web-app/link-conversation-to-lead/).
