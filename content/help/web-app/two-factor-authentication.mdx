Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step to sign-in. After your password, Sayso asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password. Someone with your password alone can't get in.

## Turn it on

1. In the web app, click your name in the top-right corner and choose <Ui>Account Settings</Ui>.
2. Open <Ui>Security</Ui>.
3. On the <Ui>Two-factor authentication</Ui> card, switch the toggle on.
4. In <Ui>Set up two-factor authentication</Ui>, scan the QR code with your authenticator app. Can't scan? Copy the code under <Ui>Can't scan? Enter this code manually</Ui> and add it to the app by hand.
5. Click <Ui>Next</Ui>.
6. Enter the 6-digit code your app shows and click <Ui>Verify</Ui>.

The card now shows <Ui>Enabled</Ui> and reads "Your account is protected with 2FA".

## Signing in with 2FA

- **Web app:** after <Ui>Sign in</Ui>, enter the code on the <Ui>Two-factor authentication</Ui> screen and click <Ui>Verify</Ui>.
- **Desktop app:** after <Ui>Log In</Ui>, the app asks for the same 6-digit code.
- **Changing your password:** you also enter a code to confirm. See [Change your password](/help/web-app/change-password/).

## Turn it off

1. Go to <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Security</Ui>.
2. Switch the <Ui>Two-factor authentication</Ui> toggle off.
3. In <Ui>Disable Two-Factor Authentication</Ui>, click <Ui>Yes, Disable</Ui>.

<Callout type="warning" title="Lost your phone?">
If you can't get codes anymore, you can't sign in by yourself. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) from your account email and we'll help you get back in.
</Callout>
