<Faq q="Why was I charged?">
Your plan renews automatically. Monthly plans renew on the same day each month, and annual plans once a year. Check the date and amount under <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Subscription</Ui> > <Ui>Invoices</Ui>. If you're on a team, the charge goes to the team owner's card.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Can I get a refund?">
Refunds are handled case by case. Email [support@asksayso.com](mailto:support@asksayso.com) with your account email, the invoice date and what happened.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Where do I find a receipt or invoice?">
On the <Ui>Subscription</Ui> tab, click any invoice in the <Ui>Invoices</Ui> list to open, download or print it. See [Update your card and download invoices](/help/web-app/update-payment-method/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="How do I change the name, address or tax ID on my invoices?">
Click <Ui>Manage billing</Ui> on the Subscription tab and update your billing details on the Stripe page.
</Faq>

<Faq q="My card was declined.">
Your status shows <Ui>Past due</Ui>. Add a working card under <Ui>Manage billing</Ui>. Coaching stops if the subscription isn't active, so fix it before your next dial block.
</Faq>

<Faq q="Do unused hours roll over?">
No. Minutes refill to your plan's amount when each invoice is paid. See [How coaching minutes are counted](/help/web-app/how-minutes-work/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="How does referral credit show up?">
When someone you referred pays their first invoice, a $25 credit is added to your Sayso billing account. It comes off your next invoice automatically. See [Refer an agent and earn credit](/help/web-app/referrals/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="I'm an agent on a team. Why can't I see billing?">
Only the team owner manages billing. Ask your owner, or see [Billing for teams](/help/admins/team-billing/).
</Faq>

<Faq q="How do I cancel?">
Go to <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Subscription</Ui> > <Ui>Manage billing</Ui>. You can cancel or pause there, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. See [Cancel, pause or change your plan](/help/web-app/cancel-subscription/).
</Faq>
