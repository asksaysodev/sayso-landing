Today Sayso connects with **Follow Up Boss** and **Sure Send**. Sayso works alongside any CRM, dialer or lead source, but only those two receive call notes directly.

## Send a request

1. Open <Ui>Account Settings</Ui> > <Ui>Connections</Ui>.
2. On the <Ui>Don't see your integration?</Ui> card, click <Ui>Request</Ui>.
3. Your email app opens a message to support@asksayso.com. Tell us:
   - Which tool you use
   - What you'd want Sayso to do with it (for example, send call notes to the lead)
   - How often you'd use it

Requests with clear use cases help us decide what to build next.

## In the meantime

Copy your Smart Capture notes from the conversation and paste them into your CRM. You can also copy them from the desktop app right after the call.
