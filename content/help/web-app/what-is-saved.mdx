When you press Stop in the desktop app, Sayso saves the call as a conversation. Here's what that includes.

## What you see in the web app

- A short **summary** of the call
- Every **Cue insight** you were shown, with its time
- Your **Smart Capture** notes (LPMAMA)
- **Pulse** market data, if you used it
- Whether an **appointment was booked**
- The **lead type** you chose (Buyer or Seller)
- The date, time and length of the call
- The **CRM lead** you linked, if any

## What Sayso keeps that you don't see

- A **text transcript** of the conversation. Sayso uses it to write your summary, Smart Capture notes and Coach Tips. The web app doesn't show the transcript.
- The **type of call** (for example a conversation, a voicemail or a call that didn't connect), so voicemails don't clutter your list or your stats.

## What Sayso never stores

**Audio.** Sayso listens during an active session to transcribe the call as it happens, but it never records or saves the audio.

## Who can see your conversations

- You.
- If you're on a team: your company's **owners and admins**. They can read your conversations but can't edit your notes or rate your insights.

## Delete a conversation

You can't delete a single conversation from the web app today. To ask for your data to be removed, see [Delete your account or your data](/help/web-app/delete-your-account/).
