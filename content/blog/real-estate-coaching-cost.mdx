Among the coaching programs that publish their prices on their own websites (checked October 24, 2026), real estate coaching costs from $799 for a six-week course and $997 a year for group coaching, to $549 to $1,299 a month for one-on-one coaching, $3,500 a month for the top individual plan at one program, and $2,999 to $4,999 a month for team plans. Several well-known programs publish no price at all, so the true range is wider than any list can show. What moves the price most is how much private time with a coach you get, whether you buy for yourself or a team, and whether you pay by the month or up front.

Below you will find a price table sourced from each program's own page, what drives the difference between them, the commitments and extra fees to ask about, and a worked example that compares a year of coaching with what you take home from a closing. For a comparison of what each program teaches and who it suits, see our guide to [real estate coaching programs](/blog/real-estate-coaching-programs/), and if you are still deciding whether you need a coach at all, start with [what a real estate coach does and how to choose one](/blog/real-estate-coach/).

## What Changes the Price

### How Much Private Time You Get

The clearest driver is the number of one-on-one sessions. Dividing the yearly price by the number of private sessions gives a rough price per session, which makes plans easier to compare.

| Plan | Yearly price | Private sessions a year | Roughly per session |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Buffini One2One (before shipping and tax) | $6,588 | 24 (two a month) | $275 |
| Tom Ferry Elite | $14,488 | 48 | $302 |
| Tim and Julie Harris Premier 1:1 | $15,564 | 48 (four a month) | $324 |
| Tom Ferry Core | $8,388 | 24 | $350 |

The per-session figure is only a comparison tool. Each price also pays for other things: a CRM and social tools at Buffini, the full 13-level system at Harris, an accountability platform, community and events at others. A cheaper session is not automatically a better buy, and a pricier one may include more than you will use.

### Group Coaching vs Private Coaching

Group and semi-private formats cost less because the coach's time is shared. The Harris Premier program, with weekly semi-private coaching, is $997 a year, while adding four private calls a month brings Premier 1:1 to $1,297 a month. If your main need is structure and scripts, the group tier may be enough. If you need someone to look at your numbers every week, the private tiers are what you are paying for.

### Individual Agent vs Team

Team plans charge for seats and events as well as sessions. Tom Ferry's Team Growth includes 72 coaching sessions a year and 10 team invitations to its accountability platform, and InnerCircle includes 50 team member accounts. On individual plans, a business partner seat is an add-on at $250 a month on Core and $450 a month on Elite.

### Paying by the Month or Up Front

Paying for a longer period usually lowers the total. Tom Ferry's annual prices are about 7 percent below twelve monthly payments: Core is $8,388 a year against $8,988 paid monthly, and Elite is $14,488 against $15,588. Diamond+ is $8,000 paid in full for the first six months, against $9,000 for six months at $1,500 a month. Harris Premier is $997 a year, against $1,101 for three payments of $367.

## Terms and Extra Costs as Published

The monthly price is only part of what you commit to. These are the terms the programs publish, and the gaps to ask about.

- **Month to month or annual.** Harris Premier 1:1 is month to month, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the current 30-day period. Harris Premier renews automatically each year at the locked $997 rate unless you cancel, and Harris Elite is month to month unless a written agreement sets a term. The Harris terms also say fees are non-refundable, including for partial use, non-use or change of mind, except for proven billing errors or fraud.
- **Starting commitments.** Diamond+ asks for a 60-day commitment to start with no refunds in the first 60 days, then month to month at the same price for as long as you stay. It takes 20 new members a month by application. Floyd Wickman's site lists weekly one-hour coaching with a six-month commitment.
- **Trial offers.** Harris advertises a seven-day, $1 trial of Premier, and you are not charged the $997 if you cancel before day 8.
- **What is not stated.** The Tom Ferry plans and team plans pages say monthly and annual plans are available but do not state cancellation or contract terms. Buffini's One2One page does not state a minimum term. Ask for both in writing.
- **Shipping, tax and setup fees.** Buffini's page lists shipping and handling and tax in addition to the $549, billed in the first month along with any add-ons, and it shows a $395 activation fee as waived under a current promotion, so ask whether the standard enrollment includes one.
- **Extra seats.** A business partner seat adds $3,000 a year on Tom Ferry Core and $5,400 on Elite if billed monthly.
- **Events and travel.** Some plans include event tickets, and attending an event usually costs you travel and time even when the ticket is included.

## A Worked Example: A Year of Coaching Against Your Take-Home

The useful question is how many extra closings a program must add to pay for itself. Start with what you keep from one closing:

**Sale price × your side's commission rate × your split, minus your own fees.** For example, on a $300,000 sale with a 3 percent commission on your side, the commission is $9,000. If your brokerage split leaves you 70 percent, you keep $6,300 before income tax and your own expenses. Your rates will differ, so substitute your own.

This table shows how many extra closings a year of each program equals at three levels of take-home per closing.

| Program (year at published price) | At $4,000 per closing | At $6,300 per closing | At $9,000 per closing |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| KW MAPS Onward BOLD ($799) | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.1 |
| Harris New Premier ($997) | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.1 |
| Buffini One2One ($6,588, before extras) | 1.6 | 1.0 | 0.7 |
| Tom Ferry Core ($8,388) | 2.1 | 1.3 | 0.9 |
| Tom Ferry Elite ($14,488) | 3.6 | 2.3 | 1.6 |
| Harris Premier 1:1 ($15,564) | 3.9 | 2.5 | 1.7 |
| Tom Ferry Team Growth ($33,649) | 8.4 | 5.3 | 3.7 |

Read the middle column as an example: at $6,300 per closing, a year of Tom Ferry Core equals about 1.3 closings of take-home pay. Then turn the number into appointments. If about one in three of your appointments becomes a closing, the 2.3 closings that Elite needs at that level means roughly seven more appointments over the year. The [prospecting calculator](/tools/prospecting-calculator/) can help you estimate how many calls and conversations that takes at your own rates.

Be honest about the estimate. The question is not whether coaching helps in general but whether it will realistically add that many closings for you, over and above what you would have done anyway.

## When a Lower-Cost Option Fits

You do not have to start at the top of the table. A low-cost course or group program covers structure and scripts, a mentor may cost little or nothing (one mentoring guide notes that group mentorships may be free or charge membership fees), and your brokerage may offer training at no extra cost, so ask what is included.

Coaching programs mostly work on your business between calls. Live call coaching works during the call itself and is priced like software: Sayso's Individual Agent+ plan is $179 a month, or $125 a month billed annually ($1,499 a year), and team and brokerage plans are priced by team size. It does not replace a coach who sets targets and holds you to them, but it is a different kind of spend that you can add on its own, and the [Why Sayso page](/why-sayso/) explains how it works on a call.

## FAQ

**How much does Tom Ferry coaching cost?**
The coaching plans page (October 2026) lists Core at $749 a month or $8,388 a year, with 24 private sessions a year, and Elite at $1,299 a month or $14,488 a year, with 48 private sessions. A business partner seat is $250 a month on Core and $450 a month on Elite. Team Growth is $2,999 a month and InnerCircle $4,999 a month.

**How much does Buffini coaching cost?**
One2One coaching is listed at $549 a month in the US, or $599 in Canada, plus shipping and handling and tax, with two coaching calls a month. Shipping, tax and any add-ons are billed in the first month.

**Can I get real estate coaching for less than $1,000 a year?**
Among the programs checked, yes: the Tim and Julie Harris New Premier program is $997 a year, and Onward BOLD is a one-time $799 for Keller Williams agents and prospective agents. Both are group formats rather than private coaching.
