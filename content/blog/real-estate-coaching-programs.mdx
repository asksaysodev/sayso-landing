The best-known real estate coaching programs in 2026 are Tom Ferry, Buffini & Company, Tim and Julie Harris, the Mike Ferry Organization and KW MAPS, with newer communities such as Ricky Carruth's Diamond+ close behind. Among the programs compared here, published prices for an individual agent run from $997 a year for group coaching to about $1,500 a month for weekly one-on-one coaching or a mastermind community, team plans cost more, and some of the biggest names only quote a price after a consultation. The right program depends less on the name than on what you need help with: prospecting scripts, referrals, building a team, or simply someone holding you to your numbers.

Below, the programs are grouped by the kind of coaching they offer, compared side by side with the prices and terms each one publishes on its own website, and matched to the agents, team leaders and brokers they suit best.

## The Five Kinds of Real Estate Coaching

Most lists rank coaches as if they all sold the same thing. They do not. Before you compare names, decide which of these you are actually buying.

1. **Prospecting and script coaching.** Daily call targets, scripts for expired listings, FSBOs and past clients, and a coach who checks your numbers. Best when you know you need more conversations and want structure. The Mike Ferry Organization is the classic example.
2. **Referral and relationship coaching.** Building a business from your database and sphere instead of strangers. Fewer cold conversations, more check-in calls, notes and events. Buffini & Company is built around this model.
3. **Business and team-building coaching.** Goals, budgets, hiring, systems and leverage for agents who already produce and want to grow or build a team. Tom Ferry and Tim and Julie Harris both cover this ground at their higher tiers.
4. **Brokerage programs.** Coaching run by or for a brokerage, such as KW MAPS for Keller Williams agents. Often cheaper, sometimes in person, and tied to that company's systems.
5. **Communities and masterminds.** A group of agents at a similar level sharing what is working, with group calls and a leader. Ricky Carruth's Diamond+ is a current example.

Most agents need one of the first three. If you are not sure which, look at last quarter: if you had too few conversations, you need prospecting coaching; if you had conversations but your referrals dried up, you need relationship coaching; if you are busy but stuck at the same income, you need business coaching.

## The Programs, One by One

### Tom Ferry

Tom Ferry is one of the best-known names in real estate coaching. Core includes 24 private coaching sessions a year, about two a month, and Elite doubles that to 48. A business partner seat costs extra ($250 a month on Core, $450 on Elite), annual billing saves about 7 percent, and the plans page lists a lower-cost Pivot group plan at $149 a month as coming soon. Team plans are priced separately (see the section for team leaders below). Ask who your coach will be: in a program this size you are matched with a coach on the company's team, so the fit with that person matters more than the brand.

### Buffini & Company One2One

Buffini's model is referral-based: work your database, stay in touch on a schedule and let past clients send you business. One2One includes two calls a month with a dedicated coach, a strengths assessment, the Referral Maker CRM and social tools, training courses and the Buffini community. The published price is $549 a month, with shipping, tax and any add-ons billed in the first month. It suits agents with a solid sphere who would rather call people they know than strangers.

### Tim and Julie Harris

The Harrises sell a full system. New Premier Coaching is a 13-level program with scripts, tools, a community and weekly semi-private coaching for $997 a year (or three payments of $367), the lowest-priced ongoing option in this comparison. Premier 1:1 adds four private calls a month with a Harris Certified Coach for $1,297 a month. Start with Premier if you want structure on a budget, and move to 1:1 if you need more personal accountability.

### Mike Ferry Organization

The Mike Ferry Organization delivers its systems through one-on-one coaching, seminars and audio training, with separate management coaching for brokers and managers. It does not publish prices; you start with a free coaching evaluation. It suits agents who want a set prospecting system with regular accountability.

### KW MAPS Onward BOLD

Onward BOLD is Keller Williams' six-week program: six one-day classes held in person at market centers, aimed at building a sustainable, predictable and profitable business. It is a one-time $799 investment, which makes it the lowest-cost structured option in this list, but it is built around Keller Williams systems and suits KW agents best.

### Ricky Carruth's Diamond+

Ricky Carruth's Diamond+ Community is an application-only group of producing agents. It costs $1,500 a month with a 60-day commitment to start and month to month after that, or $8,000 paid up front for the first six months, and the site says there are no refunds in the first 60 days. The system is installed over six months, and the site says monthly spots fill early. Several older lists still describe his program as free, so check the current offer before you plan around it.

### Ramsey Real Estate Pro Coaching

This program is narrower than the others. It coaches agents who work with Ramsey referrals on serving those clients, with a one-on-one coach, a monthly mastermind call and a training course. No price is published. If you are not part of the Ramsey referral network, one of the general programs above is a better fit.

## How Much Does Real Estate Coaching Cost?

Going by the prices the programs in this comparison publish, group coaching starts at $997 a year, a six-week course costs $799, twice-a-month one-on-one coaching runs about $550 to $750 a month, weekly one-on-one coaching runs about $1,300 a month, communities and masterminds run about $1,500 a month, and team plans run $2,999 a month and up. For an individual agent, a year of one-on-one coaching costs roughly $6,600 to $18,000 before travel. For every published price, the commitments and a table of closings needed to cover each program, see [how much real estate coaching costs](/blog/real-estate-coaching-cost/).

Watch for the costs that sit outside the monthly price:

- **Commitments.** Some programs ask for an initial commitment (Diamond+, for example, asks for the first 60 days with no refunds) or offer a discount for paying up front, so compare the total you would pay, not only the monthly price.
- **Add-ons.** A second seat for a partner or assistant can add hundreds a month.
- **Shipping, tax and setup fees.** Some programs add these on top of the listed price; Buffini, for example, lists shipping and tax on top of its monthly fee.
- **Events and travel.** Conferences and retreats may cost extra, and getting to them always does.

A worked example helps. Say your average commission is $9,000 and your brokerage split leaves you 70 percent, so you keep $6,300 per closing before your own expenses. A year of Tom Ferry Core costs $8,388, which is about 1.3 closings of take-home pay, while a year of Harris Premier at $997 is less than a sixth of one closing. Use your own numbers, then ask whether the program will realistically help you close at least that many more transactions than you would without it.

## What Does a Real Estate Coach Actually Do?

A real estate coach meets with you on a regular schedule (weekly, twice a month or monthly), reviews your numbers from the last period, helps you set targets for the next one and holds you to them. Good coaches also role-play your hardest conversations with you, give you scripts and systems that have worked for other agents, and help you decide where to spend your time. If you are still deciding whether you need a coach at all, see [whether you need a real estate coach and how to choose one](/blog/real-estate-coach/).

Most coaching happens in scheduled sessions before and after your calls rather than during them. Some programs review recorded calls or role-play with you, so ask how yours handles real calls. Scheduled sessions are the right setting for planning and accountability, but the live call itself is where many agents still lose the thread when a prospect pushes back.

## Coaching for Brokers and Team Leaders

If you run a team or a brokerage, you need a different kind of coaching: recruiting, training new agents, setting expectations, and keeping production up without doing every call yourself. Searches for broker coaching mostly turn up agent programs, so look specifically for leadership tracks:

- The **Mike Ferry Organization** offers management coaching aimed at office production, retention and accountability.
- **KW MAPS** opens Onward BOLD to market center leaders and productivity coaches as well as agents.
- **Tom Ferry** publishes team plans: Team Growth at $2,999 a month (72 coaching sessions a year, team seats and event tickets) and InnerCircle at $4,999 a month for larger teams.

Team leaders often find that the hardest part is not the plan but coaching each agent on real calls. A weekly role play helps, but you cannot listen in on every conversation. ## How to Choose a Real Estate Coaching Program

Match the program to your stage and your problem:

- **Newer agents (first two years).** You need conversations and habits more than business strategy. A prospecting-focused program or a structured course such as Onward BOLD (for Keller Williams agents) gives you the most for the money. Pair it with daily script practice; our guide to [practicing real estate scripts](/blog/how-to-practice-real-estate-scripts/) is a free place to start.
- **Experienced solo agents.** If your income has stalled, choose business coaching with one-on-one time. If your referrals have stalled, choose relationship coaching.
- **Team leaders and brokers.** Choose a program with a leadership or management track, not an agent program you will outgrow in three months.

Before you sign, ask every program the same questions:

1. Who exactly will coach me, and what is their own production or coaching record?
2. How often will we meet, and for how long?
3. What is the minimum term, and what does it cost to cancel?
4. What is included in the price, and what is extra?
5. Can I speak with two agents at my level who have been in the program for a year?

## FAQ

**Who has the best real estate coaching program?**
There is no single best program; it depends on what you need. Tom Ferry and Tim and Julie Harris suit agents who want structured business coaching, Buffini suits referral-based agents, the Mike Ferry Organization suits heavy prospectors, and KW MAPS Onward BOLD is the lowest-cost structured option for Keller Williams agents.

**How much does a real estate coach cost?**
Among the programs compared here (prices checked October 2026), group coaching starts at $997 a year (Tim and Julie Harris Premier), twice-monthly one-on-one coaching costs $549 to $749 a month (Buffini, Tom Ferry Core), and weekly one-on-one coaching or premium communities cost about $1,300 to $1,500 a month (Harris Premier 1:1, Tom Ferry Elite, Diamond+). Team plans start around $2,999 a month, and several programs only quote a price after a consultation.

**What does a real estate coach do?**
A coach meets with you on a schedule, reviews your numbers, sets targets with you, holds you accountable and role-plays difficult conversations. Most also provide scripts, systems and a community of other agents.

**Is real estate coaching worth it?**
It is worth it when the program fits your problem and you do the work between sessions. Compare the total cost with what you keep from an average closing after your split, and only sign up if you expect the coaching to bring in clearly more than that.

**What is real estate broker coaching?**
Broker coaching focuses on running an office or team: recruiting, training, accountability and profitability. Look for programs with a management or leadership track, such as the Mike Ferry Organization's management coaching, rather than an agent program.

**Can I get real estate coaching for free?**
Some brokerages include coaching or training for their agents, and many coaches publish free podcasts and videos. Structured one-on-one coaching is almost always paid, so check what your brokerage offers before you buy a program.
