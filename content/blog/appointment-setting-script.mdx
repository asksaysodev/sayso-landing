This page is the script itself. Four appointment setting templates, one per lead type: buyer, seller, past client, and ISA handoff. Each one runs from the first line to the booked time, word for word, with [blanks] you fill in before you dial. A short printable version sits at the end.

For when to ask and how to deliver the close, see [how to close for the appointment](/blog/how-to-close-for-appointment/) and the full guide on [how to book appointments in real estate](/blog/how-to-book-appointments-real-estate/).

## How to Use These Templates

Fill every blank before the call. A script with "[Area]" still in it sounds like a script.

Each template has the same five parts:

1. **Open.** Who you are and why you are calling.
2. **Two questions.** Timeline and motivation. Nothing more before the ask.
3. **Close.** A reason to meet plus two specific times.
4. **Fallback.** One softer line if they hesitate.
5. **Lock-in.** Day, date, time, place, and who will be there.

The blanks you will see most:

| Blank | What goes there |
|---|---|
| [Name] | The prospect's first name |
| [Your Name] / [Brokerage] | You and your company |
| [Area] | Their neighborhood or the area they are searching |
| [Reason] | The thing they told you or the form they filled out |
| [Day 1] / [Day 2] | Two open slots on your calendar |
| [Deliverable] | What you will bring: comps, a pricing plan, a short list of homes |
| [Agent] | For ISAs, the agent who will run the meeting |

## Buyer Lead Script

Use this for an online inquiry, a sign call, or a buyer who reached out about a specific home.

<ScriptExample label="Buyer Lead: Full Script">
**Open:** Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. You asked about [Property Address / Search] on [Source]. Did I catch you at an okay time for two minutes?

**Question 1:** What has you looking right now?

**Question 2:** And when would you ideally like to be moved in?

**Bridge:** That helps. Based on [their reason] and [their timeline], the best next step is a short sit-down so I can show you what is available in [Area] in your range, plus a few homes that never hit the big sites.

**Close:** It takes about 15 minutes. I have [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]. Which is easier for you?

**Fallback (if they hesitate):** No problem. Let me send you the [3 to 5] homes I would show you first. If any of them look right, we can grab 15 minutes after. Sound good?

**Lock-in:** Perfect, [Day], [Date] at [Time], at [Location]. Will [Partner] be joining? What's the best cell for a reminder text?
</ScriptExample>

If they ask you to just send listings, the [just send listings](/objections/just-send-listings/) objection page has more replies.

## Seller Lead Script

Use this for a home value request, an [expired listing](/blog/expired-listing-scripts/), a [FSBO](/blog/fsbo-scripts/), or any homeowner who has said they might sell.

<ScriptExample label="Seller Lead: Full Script">
**Open:** Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Brokerage]. I'm calling about your home on [Street]. [You requested a home value / I saw it came off the market / I saw it listed for sale by owner]. Did I catch you at an okay time?

**Question 1:** If you did move, where would you be heading next?

**Question 2:** And when would you want to be there?

**Bridge:** Got it. I put together what homes like yours on [Street] have sold for over the last [number] months. It's specific to your street, and it's easier to walk through in person than over the phone.

**Close:** I can come to you for 15 minutes and go through it, plus what I would price yours at today. I have [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]. Which works better?

**Fallback (if they hesitate):** Totally fair. Let me send the breakdown over this week, and I'll check back on [Day] to answer any questions. Okay?

**Lock-in:** Great, [Day], [Date] at [Time] at [Address]. Will [Partner] be there too? I'll text you the morning of.
</ScriptExample>

## Past Client Script

Use this for a past buyer or seller, or anyone in your sphere who has not talked to you in a while. The open is a check-in, not a pitch. The close only comes if they give you a reason.

<ScriptExample label="Past Client: Full Script">
**Open:** Hi [Name], it's [Your Name]. I was thinking about you when [reason: a sale on their street / their home anniversary / something they mentioned last time]. How's everything at [the house / with the family]?

**Question 1:** Do you still love the place, or has anything changed in what you need?

**Question 2:** Any chance you would make a move in the next year or two, or are you staying put?

**Bridge (if they hint at a move):** Homes like yours in [Area] have moved a lot since you [bought / sold]. I can show you where yours sits today so you have the number when you need it.

**Close:** Let me drop by for 15 minutes with the numbers. I have [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]. Which is easier?

**Fallback (if they are staying put):** Glad to hear it. Can I send you a quick value update once a [quarter / year] so you always know where you stand?

**Referral line (before you hang up):** And if anyone you know mentions moving, I'd be glad to help them the same way I helped you.
</ScriptExample>

## ISA Handoff Script

Use this when you set the appointment and someone else runs it. The close has to name the agent and an open slot on that agent's calendar.

<ScriptExample label="ISA Handoff: Full Script">
**Open:** Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with [Team / Brokerage]. You [requested a home value / asked about a home] on [Source]. Do you have two minutes?

**Question 1:** What has you thinking about [buying / selling]?

**Question 2:** And what timeline are you working with?

**Bridge:** That's helpful. The next step is a short meeting with [Agent], who handles [Area] for our team. [Agent] will go through [Deliverable] with you.

**Close:** [Agent] has [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]. Which is easier for you?

**Fallback (if they hesitate):** No problem. I'll have [Agent] send over [Deliverable] first, and I'll follow up on [Day] to see if a time makes sense. Okay?

**Lock-in:** Perfect. [Agent] will see you [Day], [Date] at [Time] at [Location]. You'll get a text from [Agent] today so you have their number. Is anyone else involved in the decision who should be there?
</ScriptExample>

Before you hang up, send the agent a handoff note so the meeting does not start over:

| Note | Why the agent needs it |
|---|---|
| Date, time, and address | The basics, confirmed back to the prospect on the call |
| Why they are considering a change | Lets the agent open with what the prospect cares about |
| Timeline | Sets how much urgency to bring to the meeting |
| Who else is involved in the decision | The agent can ask for both people to attend |
| What they asked or worried about | Anything the agent should address in the first five minutes |

## The Close Lines, Word for Word

Every template above ends in one of these three lines. Swap them in based on how the prospect sounds.

<ScriptExample label="Two-Choice Close: Hot Prospect">
Based on everything you've shared, I think the smartest next step is 15 minutes together so I can show you exactly what's happening in [Area]. I've got [Day 1] afternoon or [Day 2] morning. Which is easier for you?
</ScriptExample>

<ScriptExample label="Value-First Close: Warm Prospect">
I put together a breakdown of what homes like yours have sold for this quarter. It's specific to your street. Would it be worth grabbing 15 minutes so I can walk you through it? I can come to you, no pressure.
</ScriptExample>

<ScriptExample label="Permission Close: Cool Prospect">
I don't want to take too much of your time today. Would it be okay if I sent you a quick market update for [Area] and followed up in a week or so to see where things stand?
</ScriptExample>

<ScriptExample label="Downshift: From Two-Choice to Value-First">
Totally fair, no rush at all. Let me put together a quick breakdown of what's been selling in your area and send it over. If you find it useful, we can find 15 minutes to go over it together. Sound good?
</ScriptExample>

If the prospect says ["call me later"](/objections/call-me-later/) or ["not ready yet"](/objections/not-ready-yet/), those pages have the next line.

## Printable Version

Print this, fill in the blanks, and keep it next to the phone.

<ScriptExample label="Printable: Four Scripts on One Page">
**BUYER.** Hi [Name], [Your Name] with [Brokerage], you asked about [Property]. What has you looking? When do you want to be moved in? Let's grab 15 minutes so I can show you what's available in [Area]. [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]? *Fallback:* I'll send the [3 to 5] homes I'd show you first.

**SELLER.** Hi [Name], [Your Name] with [Brokerage], calling about your home on [Street]. Where would you head next? When? I have what homes on [Street] sold for. 15 minutes, I'll come to you. [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]? *Fallback:* I'll send the breakdown and check back [Day].

**PAST CLIENT.** Hi [Name], it's [Your Name], thinking of you because [reason]. Still love the place? Any move in the next year or two? I can show you where your home sits today. [Day 1] or [Day 2]? *Fallback:* Can I send a value update every [quarter / year]?

**ISA HANDOFF.** Hi [Name], [Your Name] with [Team], you [requested a value / asked about a home]. Why now? What timeline? Next step is 15 minutes with [Agent]. [Agent] has [Day 1] at [Time] or [Day 2] at [Time]. *Lock-in:* date, time, address, who attends, note to [Agent].
</ScriptExample>

## FAQ

**What is a real estate appointment setting script?**
It is the word-for-word language an agent or ISA uses to move a call from first hello to a booked meeting. A good one has an open, two qualifying questions, a close with two specific times, one fallback line, and a lock-in that confirms the details.

**Should I read an appointment setting script word for word?**
Read it word for word until you know it, then keep it in front of you as a guide. Fill in every blank before you dial so the specific parts, like the street name and the two times, sound like you and not like a template.

**Can I use the same script for buyers and sellers?**
The structure is the same, but the questions and the reason to meet are different. Buyers meet to see what is available in their range. Sellers meet to see what their home would sell for. Use the template that matches the lead.

**What should an ISA say when handing off an appointment to an agent?**
Name the agent, offer two open slots on that agent's calendar, and tell the prospect they will hear from the agent directly. Then send the agent a note with the time, address, motivation, timeline, and who else is involved.

**What's the difference between an appointment setting script and a cold calling script?**
A cold calling script covers the full conversation: opener, rapport, qualification, and close. An appointment setting script is built around one outcome, the booked meeting, with a short set of questions and the exact close lines for one type of lead. The best agents master both.
