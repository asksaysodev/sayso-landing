**What the ranking pages cover (table stakes):** a numbered list of named products, a pros and cons note and a price for each, marketing tools (website, design, email, social), and AI tools for writing and listings.

**What they do poorly:**

1. They are product lists, mostly by vendors that sell one of the tools, not category guides; there is no "which kind of tool solves which problem."
2. Phone prospecting is missing. The Xara list leaves out dialers, voicemail, call coaching and every tool for the call itself. Lead data appears only as advertising.
3. They do not say how the tools fit into one call workflow or what each does not solve.
4. No consent and Do Not Call caution, no "which to add first," no buying checklist, no 30-day test.
5. Comparison and selection guidance is thin (therealtyschool: "no comparison between tools or guidance on selecting among options").

**Format check.** A video (top prospecting methods) ranks and the head term sits among marketing lists, so the searcher may want ideas and methods more than tools. The post handles this by tying each tool type to a step in the prospecting process, linking to the strategies and plan posts for methods, and including a "which tool first" table, a sequence of a single call, and a buying checklist the reader can copy.
