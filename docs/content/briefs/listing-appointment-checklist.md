Table stakes: what to bring, CMA and comps, questions for the seller, presentation contents, follow-up email.

What they do poorly: none gives timing (what to do two days out versus the night before), only one has a sourced duration, almost none treats a telephone or video listing appointment, follow-up after the meeting is thin or missing, and the "free checklist" is often a gated download. The terms "pre-listing" and "first listing appointment" are not explained.

**Format check.** Video leads "what happens" and "what to do"; reddit threads lead "first listing appointment" and "what to bring"; checklist PDFs are a repeated related search. What that means: a plain-text sequence the reader can copy (a ScriptSheet, with a print button), a table of what happens in order (the part a video shows), and a section for a first-time agent. The "telephone listing appointment" group is answered with a short section on remote appointments.

Forum wording (titles only, pages blocked): "My first listing appointment is in two days, any advice?", "What do you guys take on listing appointments?", "Listing appointments, what do you do pre-listing?". The searcher asks for a practical order of work. Not used as a fact source.
