**What the ranking pages cover (table stakes):** a cost range in dollars per month by type of coaching, a list of named programs with prices, ROI framing, and "how to choose."

**What they do poorly:**

1. Prices are unsourced or out of date and disagree with each other and with the owners' own pages. One says Tom Ferry does not publish prices; the owner's plans page lists Core, Elite, team plans and an upcoming group plan.
2. No page converts price into an annual budget or compares it with an agent's take-home pay; ROI is shown with testimonials.
3. Terms are mostly missing: minimum commitments, auto-renewal, refund rules, add-on seats, shipping and tax, activation fees.
4. Nobody shows price per private session or what bundles are included.
5. No page says what is not published.
