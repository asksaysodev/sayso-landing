For "how to find a mentor in real estate" page one is mostly Reddit plus general mentor guides (breakintocre, 360training, vaned, Rocket Mortgage, Manifestly, Forbes investing piece).

**What the ranking pages cover (table stakes):** a list of well-known programs, what a coach provides ("structure, support and motivation", accountability, lead generation and negotiation training), group vs one-on-one, and a short "how to choose" (reflect on goals, check fit, check budget, try a consultation first).

**What they do poorly:**

1. Nobody separates the four things an agent could be buying: a coach, a mentor, brokerage training and live call coaching. The two mentor guides read (vaned, 360training) never mention coaches at all; the coaching guides never explain mentors.
2. "How to choose" is generic (know your goals) with no questions to ask and nothing to copy.
3. No page says when you do not need a coach yet, which is the honest answer for many new agents.
4. The new-agent guide is a program list; it does not say what to do first.
5. Nothing on how to find and approach a mentor with a message you can send.

**Format check.** A video (How to Coach Real Estate Agents, a manager-focused process) and a forum thread (Reddit coach suggestions) rank on page one, and a sales page leads. This tells us searchers want a clear, human answer to "should I and who" more than a list. The post answers that in plain text: a decision table, a copyable question scorecard, a message template for approaching a mentor, and a 90-day test. The Reddit thread could not be read, so it is not used for wording.
