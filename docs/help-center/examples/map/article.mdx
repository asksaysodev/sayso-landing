---
title: Change the text message template in Sayso Map
description: Edit the message Sayso Map prepares for similar homes.
group: map
order: 50
updated: 2026-11-25
---

Open <Ui>Filters</Ui> in Sayso Map and scroll to <Ui>Text message</Ui>.

1. Tap inside <Ui>Template</Ui> and type your message.
2. Tap <Ui>Subject address</Ui> to insert the address. Tap <Ui>Similar homes list</Ui> where you want the list to appear.
3. To restore the original message, tap <Ui>Reset template</Ui>.
