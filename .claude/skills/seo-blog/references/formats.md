# Post formats

Pick the format from the backlog `category` and what page one shows (`leading_format`, the ranking pages' structure). Match the format Google already ranks, then beat it on usefulness.

## Comparison list (coaching programs, best dialers, prospecting tools, X vs Y)

1. Direct answer: the short list and how to choose, in two or three sentences.
2. How we compared (criteria in a short list: focus, format, price, who it suits).
3. Comparison table: one row per option, the same columns for every row.
4. One H3 section per option: what it is, who it suits, price (sourced, dated), one honest limitation.
5. How to choose (by situation: new agent, team leader, experienced agent, budget).
6. Where live call coaching fits (the in-context Sayso mention, framed as a different kind of help, not as a ranked entry).
7. Closing section, then FAQ.

Fairness rules: describe every option from its own published material; no invented ratings; Sayso is never ranked against the others as if neutral. If Sayso appears in a table, label it "Sayso (our product)".

## Review (Mojo Dialer, Follow Up Boss)

1. Direct answer: who it suits and the one-line verdict.
2. What it is and what it costs (sourced, dated).
3. Features that matter to agents (table).
4. What users like and what they complain about (from public reviews, cited).
5. Compliance or setup questions people search for.
6. Alternatives (short, linked to the comparison post).
7. Using it with live coaching (in-context mention), closing section, FAQ.

## Cost guide (coaching cost, Mojo pricing)

The Cut Brush template.

1. Direct answer: the price range in one sentence, then what drives it.
2. Price table by type or plan, each row sourced and dated.
3. What changes the price (H3 per factor).
4. Hidden or extra costs.
5. Is it worth it (by situation).
6. Cheaper or different alternatives (in-context mention here when relevant; state Sayso's own price plainly).
7. Closing section, FAQ.

## How-to guide (use Mojo, listing appointment checklist, find expired listings, convert leads, coach agents, prospecting plan)

1. Direct answer: the steps in one or two sentences.
2. Numbered steps as H2s or H3s, each with what to do, what to say if it involves a call, and a common mistake.
3. A checklist, template or table the reader can copy.
4. In-context mention where a step happens on a call.
5. Closing section, FAQ.

## Script post (seller, buyer, just listed and just sold, withdrawn, new agents, negotiation, referral, voicemail, role play, conversation examples)

1. Direct answer: the core script in two or three sentences, or the opening line.
2. When to use these scripts and what the call is for (the next step, not the listing).
3. One `<ScriptExample>` per situation, each followed by "what they say next" branches: the common replies and what to say to each.
4. Voicemail versions where it fits.
5. Mistakes that kill the call.
6. In-context mention: practicing or keeping the script on screen during the call.
7. Closing section, FAQ.

Scripts come from `docs/content/ultimate-script-book.md` where it covers the situation. Keep them buyer and seller neutral unless the post is about one side.
